# Reconquista (example)

- Christian kingdoms retook Iberia gradually from 722 to 1492.
- Covadonga (722) mythologizes the movement's tiny start.
- El Cid (Valencia) served Christian and Muslim masters pragmatically.
- Las Navas de Tolosa (1212) broke Almohad power decisively.
- Granada's 1492 fall closed eight centuries of frontier warfare.
- Repoblación resettled frontier towns with charters (fueros).
- Military orders (Santiago, Calatrava) blended monks with knights.
- Alhambra Decree expelled Jews the same year Granada fell.
- Convivencia debates weigh tolerance against persecution realities.
- Crusading ideology plus land hunger both fueled expansion.
