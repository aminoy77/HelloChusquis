# Mesopotamia (example)

- Mesopotamia, between the Tigris and Euphrates, is called the cradle of civilization.
- Sumerians invented cuneiform writing around 3400 BC, among the first scripts.
- Uruk was among the world's first true cities, rising around 4000 BC.
- The Code of Hammurabi (around 1754 BC) codified Babylonian laws famously as eye for eye.
- Mesopotamians divided hours into 60 minutes, a system still in use.
- The wheel first appeared in Mesopotamia around 3500 BC.
- Ziggurats were massive stepped temples dominating Sumerian city centers.
- The Epic of Gilgamesh is among humanity's oldest surviving stories.
- Akkadians under Sargon built one of history's first empires around 2334 BC.
- Assyrians later ruled the region with iron weapons and siege engineering.
