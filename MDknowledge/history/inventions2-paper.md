# Paper (example)

- Cai Lun's Han-court recipe (~105 AD) standardized bark-hemp-rag papermaking.
- Paper displaced bamboo strips and silk for everyday Chinese writing.
- Samarkand mills (751, Talas prisoners legend) spread craft westward.
- Islamic paper enabled bureaucracies from Baghdad to Cordoba.
- European mills (Fabriano, 1276) added watermarks and sizing gelatin.
- Wood-pulp (1840s Keller) plus Fourdrinier machines made paper cheap.
- Newspaper empires rode cheap paper into mass literacy.
- Acid-free standards now preserve archives for centuries deliberately.
- Recycling loops fibers ~7 times before shortening unusably.
- Digital predictions of paperlessness repeatedly missed resilient niches.
