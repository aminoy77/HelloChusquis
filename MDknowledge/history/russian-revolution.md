# Russian Revolution (example)

- February 1917 toppled the Tsar amid war, hunger, and strikes.
- October 1917 put Lenin's Bolsheviks in power with soviet backing.
- Tsar Nicholas II abdicated ending 300 Romanov years.
- Treaty of Brest-Litovsk (1918) bought peace with Germany at huge cost.
- Civil War (1918-1922) pitted Reds against Whites plus interveners.
- War Communism seized grain, collapsing output before NEP retreat.
- Lenin died 1924; Stalin outmaneuvered Trotsky for control.
- USSR formed 1922 uniting Russia, Ukraine, Belarus, Transcaucasia.
- Women's Zhenotdel and literacy campaigns marked early social pledges.
- Historians debate spontaneity versus Bolshevik organization ever since.
- Petrograd Soviet Order No. 1 dissolved officer authority in March.
- April Theses demanded all power to soviets, stunning moderates.
- July Days failed rising taught Bolsheviks timing discipline.
- Kornilov's attempted coup discredited the right and armed workers.
- Constituent Assembly dispersed 1918 after one day for Bolshevik minority.
