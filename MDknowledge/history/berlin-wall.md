# Berlin Wall (example)

- The Wall rose overnight 12-13 August 1961 sealing East Berlin.
- Concrete segments, guards, and death strips enforced the border lethally.
- Checkpoint Charlie staged the 1961 US-Soviet tank standoff.
- Escape attempts (tunnels, balloons, wires) killed 140+ people.
- Kennedy's 1963 'Ich bin ein Berliner' speech backed West Berlin.
- Reagan's 1987 'tear down this wall' speech framed Cold War endgame.
- A botched press briefing opened crossings on 9 November 1989.
- Crowds dismantled the Wall with hammers as guards stood down.
- German reunification followed formally in October 1990.
- East Side Gallery preserves painted segments as memorial art.
- Stasi files documented pervasive informant surveillance afterward.
- Ostalgie nostalgia markets GDR kitsch to later generations.
- Wall death tolls remain researched and revised by historians.
- Border museums (Bernauer Strasse) preserve escape-tunnel stories.
- Reunification costs still shape German budgets and politics.
