# Renaissance (example)

- The Renaissance revived classical learning from 14th-century Italy outward.
- Humanism centered education on human potential rather than scholastic theology.
- Leonardo da Vinci painted the Mona Lisa and filled notebooks with inventions.
- Michelangelo sculpted David and painted the Sistine Chapel ceiling.
- The Medici bankers of Florence funded much Renaissance art and science.
- Perspective geometry gave paintings realistic depth for the first time.
- The printing press spread Renaissance texts beyond elite manuscript circles.
- Erasmus's writings fueled reform debates across Northern Europe.
- Copernicus placed the Sun at the center, launching the scientific revolution.
- Shakespeare's plays crowned the English Renaissance around 1600.
