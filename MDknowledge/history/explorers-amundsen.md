# Amundsen (example)

- Amundsen beat Scott to the South Pole (14 Dec 1911) by 34 days.
- Dogs, skis, and Inuit clothing beat ponies and tractors decisively.
- Secret pivot: announced Arctic drift, sailed south instead.
- Fram's round hull survived crushing pack ice by design.
- Northwest Passage transit (1903-06) preceded polar fame quietly.
- Dirigible Norge (1926) likely first overflew the North Pole.
- Disappeared 1928 rescuing Nobile's airship crew heroically.
- Scott's frozen diaries contrast planning philosophies starkly.
- Polheim tent (spare!) marked the pole for followers to find.
- Oslo's Fram museum houses his polar ship intact.
