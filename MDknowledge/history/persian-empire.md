# Persian Empire (example)

- Cyrus the Great founded Achaemenid Persia (~550 BC) with tolerance policy.
- The Cyrus Cylinder is cited as an early charter of rights.
- Darius organized satrapies, royal roads, and standardized coinage.
- Persepolis terrace-palaces displayed imperial ceremony magnificently.
- Royal Road couriers covered ~2,700 km in about nine days.
- Greco-Persian Wars (Marathon, Thermopylae, Salamis) checked western expansion.
- Zoroastrianism shaped Persian ethics with good/evil dualism.
- Parthians later frustrated Rome with horse-archer tactics at Carrhae.
- Sassanids revived Persia (224-651 AD) rivaling Byzantium.
- Alexander burned Persepolis in 330 BC ending Achaemenid rule.
- Qanats (underground channels) watered arid Persia for millennia.
- Persian gardens (paradise) formalized quadripartite paradise design.
- Naqsh-e Jahan square anchors Safavid Isfahan's glory.
- Modern Iran inherits Persian language, Nowruz, and imperial memory.
