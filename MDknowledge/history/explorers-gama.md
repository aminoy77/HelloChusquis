# Vasco da Gama (example)

- Da Gama rounded Africa to India (1498), opening the sea spice route.
- Monsoon pilot Ahmad ibn Majid likely guided the Indian Ocean leg.
- Calicut's Zamorin received trade demands arrogantly rebuffed.
- Second voyage (1502) bombarded Calicut establishing Portuguese terror.
- Viceroy of India title crowned his 1524 third voyage (he died there).
- Scurvy killed half the first crew before homecoming.
- Cape Route profits funded Portugal's Asian fort-network empire.
- His flagship São Gabriel pioneered ocean carrack design.
- Tomb rests in Belem's Jeronimos Monastery among explorers.
- Arab pilots' knowledge underpinned the 'discovery' substantially.
