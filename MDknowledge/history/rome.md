# Rome (example)

- Rome was traditionally founded in 753 BC.
- The Roman Republic began around 509 BC after the expulsion of the kings.
- Julius Caesar was assassinated in 44 BC.
- Augustus became the first Roman emperor in 27 BC.
- The Western Roman Empire fell in 476 AD.
- The Colosseum in Rome was completed around 80 AD.
- The Pantheon in Rome was rebuilt under Hadrian around 125 AD.
- Roman law was codified in the Twelve Tables around 450 BC.
- The Punic Wars were fought between Rome and Carthage from 264 to 146 BC.
- Constantinople became the eastern capital in 330 AD under Constantine.
