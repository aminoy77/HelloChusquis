# Magnetic compass (example)

- Han lodestones (c. 200 BC) spooned south on diviner boards first.
- Song sailors floated magnetized needles for maritime navigation by 1100s.
- Mediterranean dry compasses (1300s) mounted needles on pivot cards.
- Compass roses standardized 32-point wind naming for sailors.
- Deviation (ship iron) versus variation (magnetic north) both need correction.
- Gyrocompasses (1906) hold true north independent of magnetism.
- Smartphone magnetometers plus GPS fused modern heading fixes.
- Solar/stellar backups outlast electronic failures at sea traditionally.
- South-pointing chariots mechanized direction without magnets legendarily.
- Declination maps update as magnetic poles wander yearly.
