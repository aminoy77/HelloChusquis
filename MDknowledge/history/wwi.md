# World War I (example)

- Archduke Franz Ferdinand's June 1914 assassination in Sarajevo triggered war.
- Alliance cascades mobilized Europe within weeks (Schlieffen Plan).
- Western Front trenches froze from Switzerland to the sea by late 1914.
- Verdun and Somme (1916) each killed around a million men.
- Unrestricted submarine warfare plus Zimmermann Telegram drew America in (1917).
- Russian collapse (1917) freed German divisions for failed 1918 offensives.
- Armistice came 11 November 1918 at 11am after German revolution.
- Treaty of Versailles (1919) blamed Germany and redrew maps punitively.
- Spanish flu (1918-1920) killed more than the war's ~20 million.
- League of Nations emerged, lacking US membership and enforcement teeth.
- Poison gas, tanks, and aircraft debuted as industrial warfare tools.
- Lawrence's Arab revolt harassed Ottoman supply lines famously.
- Christmas Truce 1914 saw spontaneous fraternization in trenches.
- Total war mobilized women into factories, expanding suffrage after.
- Empires (Austrian, Ottoman, Russian, German) all collapsed from the strain.
