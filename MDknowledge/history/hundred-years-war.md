# Hundred Years' War (example)

- England and France fought intermittently 1337-1453 over crowns and land.
- Edward III's claim through his mother triggered dynastic conflict.
- Crecy (1346) longbows shredded French chivalry shockingly.
- Agincourt (1415) repeated the archer lesson against odds.
- Joan of Arc lifted Orleans (1429) before capture and burning (1431).
- Burgundian alliances swung fortunes between claimants repeatedly.
- Longbow to gunpowder transition matured across the conflict.
- French victory centralized monarchy, seeding absolutism.
- Parliament's war funding grew English representative power.
- 1337-1453 spans 116 years despite the round name.
