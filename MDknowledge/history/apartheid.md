# Apartheid (example)

- Apartheid (1948-1994) legally enforced white-minority rule in South Africa.
- Population Registration classified everyone by race for separate amenities.
- Sharpeville massacre (1960) killed 69 protesters, radicalizing resistance.
- Mandela's Rivonia Trial (1964) brought life imprisonment, not execution.
- Soweto uprising (1976) began with schoolchildren protesting Afrikaans lessons.
- International sanctions, boycotts, and divestment squeezed Pretoria economically.
- De Klerk unbanned the ANC and freed Mandela in 1990.
- First democratic election (1994) made Mandela president peacefully.
- Truth and Reconciliation Commission traded amnesty for testimony.
- Bantustans granted fake independence to strip Black citizenship.
- Pass laws controlled Black movement and employment relentlessly.
- Robben Island imprisoned Mandela 18 of his 27 years inside.
- Sport boycotts (Olympics, rugby) isolated Afrikaner culture painfully.
- Apartheid statues and street names remain contested heritage today.
- Rainbow Nation branding framed reconciliation over retribution.
