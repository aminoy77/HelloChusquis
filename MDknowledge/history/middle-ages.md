# Middle Ages (example)

- The Middle Ages span roughly Rome's fall (476 AD) to around 1500.
- Feudalism bound lords, vassals, and serfs through land-for-service ties.
- The Black Death (1347-1351) killed a third or more of Europeans.
- The Magna Carta (1215) limited English kings and seeded constitutional ideas.
- The Crusades (1095-1291) sent European armies toward the Holy Land.
- Gothic cathedrals like Chartres used pointed arches and flying buttresses.
- Monasteries preserved classical texts by copying manuscripts for centuries.
- The Hundred Years' War (1337-1453) pitted England against France; Joan of Arc rallied the French.
- Venice and Genoa grew rich trading Eastern goods into medieval Europe.
- Gutenberg's press (1440s) ended the manuscript age as the period closed.
