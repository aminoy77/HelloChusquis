# Vikings (example)

- Viking Age raiding ran roughly 793 (Lindisfarne) to 1066 (Stamford Bridge).
- Longships combined shallow draft with ocean range for raids and trade.
- Norse settled Iceland (~870), Greenland (~985), and briefly Vinland.
- Varangians served Byzantine emperors as elite guards in Constantinople.
- Thing assemblies practiced early participatory law in Scandinavia.
- Runic alphabets (futhark) carved short memorial and magical texts.
- Norse mythology (Odin, Thor, Ragnarok) survives via Icelandic sagas.
- Danelaw Norse rule shaped English language and place names deeply.
- Normans (Norse-descended) conquered England in 1066 under William.
- Greenland colonies faded amid cooling climate and trade collapse.
- Burial ships (Oseberg, Gokstad) preserved Viking craft superbly.
- Skis, skates, and saunas trace partly to Norse daily life.
- Leif Erikson reached North America around 1000 AD.
- Jorvik (York) was a major Viking trade town in England.
- Christianization (10th-12th centuries) ended the raiding era gradually.
