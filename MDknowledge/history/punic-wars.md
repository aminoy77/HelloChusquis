# Punic Wars (example)

- Rome and Carthage fought three wars (264-146 BC) for Mediterranean mastery.
- First war's naval buildup (corvus boarding) broke Carthaginian sea power.
- Hannibal's Alpine elephants (218 BC) opened fifteen Italian years.
- Cannae (216 BC) annihilated larger Roman armies tactically perfectly.
- Fabian delaying tactics traded space for Hannibal's supply exhaustion.
- Scipio invaded Africa forcing Hannibal home to Zama defeat (202 BC).
- Third war ended with Carthage razed and salted (legend says).
- Cato's 'Carthago delenda est' closed every Senate speech obsessively.
- Mercenary War backstory explains Carthaginian manpower fragility.
- Roman sea discipline learned from a wrecked Carthaginian quinquereme.
