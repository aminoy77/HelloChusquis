# Mexican Revolution (example)

- Diaz's 30-year dictatorship cracked in the rigged 1910 election.
- Madero's Plan of San Luis sparked uprisings nationwide.
- Zapata's Plan of Ayala demanded land reform ('Tierra y Libertad').
- Villa's Division del Norte won sweeping northern campaigns.
- US Veracruz occupation (1914) complicated factions further.
- Constitution of 1917 enshrined labor plus land reform radically.
- Assassinations (Madero, Zapata, Villa, Carranza, Obregon) decimated leaders.
- Obregon's 1920s stabilized a one-party (PRI) order for decades.
- Muralists (Rivera, Orozco) painted revolutionary mythology publicly.
- Day of the Dead plus revolutionary ballads (corridos) carry memory culturally.
