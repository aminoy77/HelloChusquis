# Meiji Restoration (example)

- Tokugawa shogunate fell (1868) restoring teenage Emperor Meiji nominally.
- Charter Oath promised assemblies and knowledge-seeking famously.
- Iwakura Mission toured West (1871-73) importing institutions wholesale.
- Conscription plus railways/telegraphs built modern state capacity fast.
- Sino-Japanese (1895) and Russo-Japanese (1905) victories announced arrival.
- Zaibatsu conglomerates industrialized with state partnership closely.
- Constitution (1889, Prussian-modeled) granted limited parliamentary rule.
- Samurai stipends commuted into bonds, dissolving the class peacefully-ish.
- Compulsory schooling plus conscription forged national identity deliberately.
- Shinto established as state ideology around the emperor.
