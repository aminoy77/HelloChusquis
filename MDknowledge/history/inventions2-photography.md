# Photography (example)

- Niepce's 1826 heliograph fixed the first permanent camera image.
- Daguerreotypes (1839) delivered sharp one-off portraits commercially.
- Talbot's negatives enabled unlimited paper prints instead.
- Roll film (Eastman, 1888) plus 'you press the button' democratized cameras.
- Color processes (Autochrome, Kodachrome) matured across 1900-1935.
- 35mm Leica portability created photojournalism's golden age.
- Digital sensors (1975 Sasson prototype) disrupted film by the 2000s.
- Phone cameras made everyone a photographer from 2007 onward.
- RAW files preserve sensor data for flexible post-processing.
- Archival prints (pigment inks, acid-free) outlive screens reliably.
