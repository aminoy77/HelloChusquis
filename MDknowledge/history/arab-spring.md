# Arab Spring (example)

- Bouazizi's self-immolation (Dec 2010 Tunisia) sparked regional uprisings.
- Tunisia democratized (lone success) via union-brokered dialogue.
- Egypt's Tahrir toppled Mubarak in 18 days, then seesawed to Sisi.
- Libya's NATO-backed revolt collapsed into militia fragmentation.
- Syria's protests militarized into catastrophic civil war.
- Bahrain's Pearl Roundabout rising was crushed with Gulf troops.
- Yemen's transition failed into Houthi war and famine.
- Social media coordinated leaderless crowds past state media blackouts.
- Counterrevolutions (2013+) restored autocrats across most states.
- Second wave (2019 Algeria, Sudan, Lebanon, Iraq) revived street politics.
