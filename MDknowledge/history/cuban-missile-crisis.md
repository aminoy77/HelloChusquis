# Cuban Missile Crisis (example)

- U-2 photos (Oct 1962) revealed Soviet missiles 90 miles off Florida.
- Thirteen Days brought the world closest to nuclear war verifiably.
- Naval 'quarantine' blockaded Cuba without declaring war legally.
- Vasili Arkhipov's refusal likely prevented submarine nuclear launch.
- Secret Jupiter-missile trade (Turkey) bought public Soviet retreat.
- Hotline installation afterward guaranteed leader-to-leader contact.
- ExComm tapes document deliberation under existential pressure.
- Tactical nukes already in Cuba escaped Washington's knowledge scarily.
- Naval brinkmanship norms tightened after near-misses catalogued.
- Kennedy's approval soared, masking how close catastrophe came.
