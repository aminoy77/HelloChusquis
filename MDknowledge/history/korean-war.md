# Korean War (example)

- North Korea invaded the South on 25 June 1950 across the 38th parallel.
- UN forces (US-led) counterattacked after Inchon landings (September 1950).
- China intervened massively as UN troops neared the Yalu river.
- MacArthur's dismissal (1951) asserted civilian control over generals.
- Stalemate trenchesolated near the 38th parallel from 1951 onward.
- Armistice (July 1953) paused fighting; no peace treaty ever followed.
- Seoul changed hands four times during see-saw campaigns.
- US bombing flattened most North Korean cities and dams.
- DMZ remains the world's most fortified border since 1953.
- South Korea's later boom contrasts the North's isolation starkly.
- 38th parallel division originated as 1945 US-Soviet arrangement.
- Pusan Perimeter (1950) held the last UN toehold before Inchon.
- MiG Alley dogfights made Korea the first jet-vs-jet air war.
- Chinese 'human wave' offensives overwhelmed thin UN lines initially.
- War powers debates still cite Korea's undeclared-war precedent.
