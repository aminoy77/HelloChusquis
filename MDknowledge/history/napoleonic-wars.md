# Napoleonic Wars (example)

- Revolutionary France's wars (1792-1815) climaxed under Napoleon Bonaparte.
- Austerlitz (1805) shattered the Third Coalition masterfully.
- Trafalgar (1805) secured British naval supremacy for a century.
- Peninsular War bled French armies in Spanish guerrilla ulcer.
- Russian campaign (1812) destroyed the Grande Armée via winter plus scorched earth.
- Leipzig (1813, Battle of Nations) turned the tide decisively.
- Elba exile, Hundred Days comeback, Waterloo (1815) ended the saga.
- Congress of Vienna (1815) redrew Europe conservatively for decades.
- Napoleonic Code spread legal equality across conquered Europe.
- Nationalism, both spread and sparked, outlived the empire everywhere.
