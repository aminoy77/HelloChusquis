# Satellites (example)

- Sputnik 1 (1957) beeped Cold War space race into existence.
- Explorer 1 discovered Van Allen radiation belts promptly after.
- Telstar (1962) relayed first live transatlantic television.
- GPS constellation (24+ satellites) guides billions daily since the 1990s.
- Geostationary slots hover fixed points 35,786 km above equators.
- Weather satellites track hurricanes from formation to landfall.
- CubeSats shrank access so universities launch routinely now.
- Starlink megaconstellations beam broadband from low orbit fleets.
- Space junk (tracked 30,000+ objects) threatens orbits increasingly.
- Voyager probes (1977) still phone home from interstellar space.
