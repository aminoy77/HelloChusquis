# Email (example)

- Ray Tomlinson sent first network mail (1971) choosing the @ sign.
- SMTP (1982) standardized sending; IMAP/POP retrieve to clients.
- Spam overwhelmed inboxes by the 2000s, spawning filters and laws.
- Attachments (MIME) extended text mail to files and media.
- Webmail (Hotmail 1996, Gmail 2004) moved archives into browsers.
- DKIM/SPF/DMARC authenticate senders against spoofing today.
- Threading views group replies; labels replaced folders for many.
- Email remains work's system of record despite chat apps' rise.
- Phishing weaponized email into top breach vector persistently.
- Encryption (PGP/S-MIME) stays niche against usability friction.
