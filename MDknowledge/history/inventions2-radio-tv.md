# Radio and television (example)

- Marconi's 1901 transatlantic 'S' proved wireless telegraphy viable.
- Vacuum tubes amplified broadcasts into 1920s living rooms.
- TV mechanical scans (Baird) gave way to electronic CRTs by the 1930s.
- BBC (1936) and NBC pioneered scheduled television service.
- Color standards (NTSC/PAL/SECAM) fragmented global broadcasting.
- Transistors (1947) shrank radios from furniture to pockets.
- Satellite relays (Telstar 1962) beamed live intercontinental TV.
- Cable plus remotes fragmented the three-network era from the 1980s.
- Streaming (2007+) unbundled schedules into on-demand catalogs.
- Podcasts revived audio storytelling on RSS-plus-phone rails.
