# Maya civilization (example)

- Maya city-states flourished in the Classic period (250-900 AD).
- Tikal's temples rise above Guatemalan jungle canopies today.
- Maya glyphs form a full logosyllabic script, deciphered late 20th century.
- The Long Count calendar tracked deep time from 3114 BC mythic start.
- Chichen Itza's pyramid shadows serpents at equinox sunsets.
- Maya astronomers predicted eclipses and Venus cycles precisely.
- Cenotes (sinkholes) watered Yucatan cities and hosted offerings.
- Classic collapse (~900 AD) likely mixed drought, war, and overextension.
- Postclassic Maya (Chichen Itza, Mayapan) persisted until Spanish conquest.
- Diego de Landa burned Maya books; only four codices survive.
- Ballgames blended sport, ritual, and sacrifice in purpose-built courts.
- Zero as a placeholder appears early in Maya mathematics.
- Cacao currency and tribute financed Maya elite economies.
- Modern Maya peoples (7M+) still speak Mayan languages today.
- Palenque's Pakal tomb revealed jade-masked royal burial.
