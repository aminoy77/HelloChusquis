# Ottoman Empire (example)

- Osman I founded the Ottoman state around 1299 in Anatolia.
- Mehmed II took Constantinople in 1453, ending Byzantium.
- Suleiman the Magnificent (1520-1566) brought peak territory and law codes.
- Janissaries formed an elite slave-soldier infantry corps.
- The empire spanned three continents at its 1683 height before Vienna's siege failed.
- Millet courts let religious communities self-govern personal law.
- Coffeehouse culture and Tulip-era arts flourished in 1700s Istanbul.
- Greek independence (1830) began the empire's Balkan unravelling.
- Tanzimat reforms (1839-1876) modernized law, armies, and schools.
- World War I alliance with Germany ended the empire; Turkey rose in 1923.
- Topkapi Palace administered the empire for nearly 400 years.
- Topkapi relics and kitchens displayed imperial ceremony daily.
- Suleymaniye Mosque crowns Istanbul's skyline as Sinan's masterwork.
- Armenian deportations and massacres (1915) remain internationally condemned.
- Ataturk abolished the sultanate (1922) then the caliphate (1924).
