# Spanish Empire (example)

- 1492 (Granada, Columbus, expulsion) launched Spain's imperial century.
- Conquistadors toppled Aztec (1521) and Inca (1532) empires swiftly.
- Silver fleets from Potosi and Zacatecas financed Habsburg wars.
- The Spanish Armada's 1588 wreck checked invasion plans against England.
- Tordesillas (1494) split the non-European world with Portugal papally.
- Mission systems (California to Paraguay) spread faith plus forced labor.
- Price revolution inflation followed American silver into Europe.
- Bourbon reforms (1700s) centralized administration before Napoleon's invasion.
- Latin American independences (1810-1826) ended most continental rule.
- 1898 losses (Cuba, Philippines) closed the imperial era traumatically.
- Golden Age arts (Cervantes, Velazquez) coincided with imperial peak.
- Escorial palace-monastery embodied Philip II's austere power.
- Flamenco, bullfighting, and siesta stereotypes crystallized in this era.
- Spanish remains the world's second native language by speakers.
- Catholic missions mapped Baja to Texas presidios and pueblos.
