# British Empire (example)

- The empire peaked around 1920 covering a quarter of Earth's land.
- East India Company rule gave way to the Crown Raj after 1857.
- American independence (1783) ended the first empire; Asia/Africa built the second.
- Suez Canal shares (1875) secured the India route strategically.
- Scramble for Africa (1884-85 Berlin) partitioned the continent colonially.
- Opium Wars forced Chinese ports open for British trade.
- Dominions (Canada, Australia) gained autonomy via 1931 Westminster Statute.
- Indian independence and Partition (1947) began rapid decolonization.
- Suez Crisis (1956) exposed postwar limits of British power.
- Commonwealth association outlived formal empire for most ex-colonies.
- Royal Navy's two-power standard underwrote 19th-century supremacy.
- English spread globally through administration, schools, and trade.
- Industrial Revolution financed and was financed by imperial markets.
- Windrush migration reshaped postwar British society profoundly.
- Hong Kong's 1997 handover closed the major colonial chapter.
