# Fall of the USSR (example)

- Gorbachev's glasnost/perestroika (1985+) loosened controls fatally.
- Chernobyl (1986) exposed systemic lying, accelerating cynicism.
- Baltic human chains (1989, two million) demanded independence visibly.
- Berlin Wall's fall cascaded Eastern European regimes in months.
- Yeltsin atop a tank (Aug 1991) faced down the hardline coup.
- Belavezha Accords (Dec 1991) dissolved the union into CIS states.
- Shock therapy privatizations enriched oligarchs amid 1990s collapse.
- Chechen wars plus NATO expansion embittered successor Russia.
- Archives opened briefly, then re-closed under later management.
- Living standards crashed before recovering unevenly by region.
