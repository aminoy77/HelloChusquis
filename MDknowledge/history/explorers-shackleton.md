# Shackleton (example)

- Endurance trapped and crushed in Weddell Sea ice (1915).
- Elephant Island camp survived Antarctic winter on seals and hope.
- James Caird open-boat voyage (1,300 km) ranks among greatest navigations.
- South Georgia crossing (36 hours, unmapped) completed the rescue chain.
- All 28 men survived without a single loss astonishingly.
- Earlier Nimrod expedition pioneered routes Scott later used.
- Boss leadership (optimism plus discipline) defines management case studies.
- Photographer Hurley's plates survived the ice to document everything.
- Endurance wreck found 2022 upright 3,000 meters down.
- Whisky cases buried at base camps auction for fortunes now.
