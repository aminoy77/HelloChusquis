# Ancient Egypt (example)

- The Great Pyramid of Giza was built for Pharaoh Khufu around 2580 BC.
- The Great Sphinx stands near the Giza pyramids and has a lion's body with a human head.
- Egyptian hieroglyphs were deciphered using the Rosetta Stone, found in 1799.
- Jean-Francois Champollion decoded hieroglyphs in 1822 using the Rosetta Stone's three scripts.
- Tutankhamun's nearly intact tomb was discovered by Howard Carter in 1922.
- The Nile flows northward into the Mediterranean, and its floods enabled Egyptian agriculture.
- The Old Kingdom (around 2686-2181 BC) was the age of the great pyramid builders.
- The New Kingdom (around 1550-1069 BC) was Egypt's imperial age with pharaohs like Ramesses II.
- Cleopatra VII, who died in 30 BC, was the last active pharaoh of ancient Egypt.
- Mummification preserved bodies for the afterlife by removing organs and drying tissue with natron salt.
- Papyrus, made from river reeds, was the writing surface of ancient Egypt.
- The Book of the Dead is a collection of funerary spells, not a single book.
- Abu Simbel's temples were relocated in the 1960s to save them from the Aswan Dam reservoir.
