# French Revolution (example)

- The French Revolution began in 1789 against monarchy, debt, and bread prices.
- The Storming of the Bastille on 14 July 1789 is France's national day origin.
- The Declaration of the Rights of Man proclaimed liberty, equality, and property.
- The Reign of Terror (1793-94) guillotined thousands under Robespierre.
- Louis XVI and Marie Antoinette were executed in 1793.
- The revolutionary calendar even renamed months before Napoleon scrapped it.
- Napoleon seized power in 1799, ending the revolution and starting empire.
- The Napoleonic Code standardized laws across conquered Europe.
- Waterloo (1815) ended Napoleon's rule and restored monarchies.
- The revolution spread nationalism and legal equality across Europe.
