# Gunpowder (example)

- Taoist alchemists (~850 AD) mixed saltpeter seeking immortality elixirs.
- Formula stabilized around saltpeter/charcoal/sulfur (75/15/10).
- Fire lances and bombs preceded true metal-barrel guns (1200s).
- Mongol invasions carried recipes westward across Eurasia.
- Ottoman bombards (1453) shattered Constantinople's Theodosian walls.
- Corned powder (uniform grains) boosted power and shelf life hugely.
- Smokeless powder (1880s) ended battlefield white-out fogs.
- Fireworks preserved gunpowder's festive civilian face globally.
- Rifling plus cartridges later eclipsed muzzle-loading muskets.
- Arms-race dynamics still trace to this chemistry's military logic.
