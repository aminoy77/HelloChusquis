# Spanish Civil War (example)

- Nationalists under Franco rose against the Republic in July 1936.
- Guernica bombing (1937) previewed total-war terror tactics.
- International Brigades volunteered 35,000 foreigners for the Republic.
- Non-Intervention pact starved the Republic while Axis armed Franco.
- Madrid's 'No pasaran!' defiance held through brutal siege.
- Barcelona fell January 1939; war ended that April.
- Franco's dictatorship lasted until his 1975 death.
- Valley of the Fallen monument controversy persists around memory.
- Orwell's Catalonia service radicalized Homage to Catalonia's author.
- Exiles seeded Latin American culture with Spanish intellectuals.
