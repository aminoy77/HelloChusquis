# Lewis and Clark (example)

- Jefferson commissioned Corps of Discovery after the 1803 Purchase.
- Sacagawea's interpreting and survival skills proved expedition-critical.
- Wintered at Fort Mandan (1804-05) then Fort Clatsop (1805-06).
- Reached the Pacific November 1805 after 18 months overland.
- Journals catalogued 178 plants and 122 animals new to science.
- York (enslaved) voted equally in expedition decisions remarkably.
- Return route split to map Yellowstone and Marias branches.
- Seaman (Lewis's Newfoundland) guarded camps famously.
- Reports opened the West to trappers then settlers rapidly.
- Trail bicentennial (2004-06) reenacted segments nationally.
