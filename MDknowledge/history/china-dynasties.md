# Chinese dynasties (example)

- The Xia is China's legendary first dynasty, traditionally from around 2070 BC.
- The Shang (around 1600-1046 BC) left oracle-bone writing, China's earliest script.
- The Zhou introduced the Mandate of Heaven justifying dynastic change.
- Confucius taught ethics and order during the Eastern Zhou turmoil.
- Qin Shi Huang unified China in 221 BC and began the Great Wall's early rammed walls.
- The Han dynasty (202 BC-220 AD) opened the Silk Road and made paper practical.
- The Tang (618-907) is remembered as a golden age of poetry and cosmopolitan Chang'an.
- The Song advanced printing, gunpowder, and the compass while losing the north.
- The Mongol Yuan (1271-1368) made Beijing a capital under Kublai Khan.
- The Ming built the Forbidden City and today's Great Wall stone facing.
