# Scramble for Africa (example)

- Berlin Conference (1884-85) regulated European claims without Africans present.
- Belgian Congo atrocities (rubber terror) killed millions under Leopold II.
- Maxim guns plus railways let tiny expeditions seize vast territories.
- Ethiopia's Adwa victory (1896) repelled Italy uniquely.
- Fashoda Incident (1898) nearly sparked Anglo-French war over Sudan.
- Boer Wars secured South African gold/diamonds for Britain bloodily.
- Artificial borders split ethnicities, seeding postcolonial conflicts.
- Cash-crop economies reoriented subsistence toward export monocultures.
- Missionaries plus explorers preceded flags as softening agents.
- Decolonization wave (1957-1960s) unwound the carve-up within decades.
