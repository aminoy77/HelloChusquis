# Crimean War (example)

- Britain, France, Ottomans fought Russia (1853-1856) over declining Ottoman spoils.
- Sevastopol's year-long siege decided the grinding conflict.
- Charge of the Light Brigade (1854) mythologized miscommunicated valor.
- Florence Nightingale professionalized nursing amid Scutari horrors.
- War photography (Fenton) plus telegraphs brought fronts home firsthand.
- Treaty of Paris (1856) neutralized the Black Sea temporarily.
- Russian defeat exposed serf-army backwardness, spurring 1861 emancipation.
- Victoria Cross instituted (1856) honors extreme bravery still.
- Balaklava's harbor chaos epitomized logistical incompetence studied since.
- Piecemeal alliances previewed 1914's cascade mechanics.
