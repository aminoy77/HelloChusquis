# World War II (example)

- World War II ran from 1939 to 1945 after Germany invaded Poland on 1 September 1939.
- The Axis powers were Germany, Italy, and Japan; the Allies included Britain, the USSR, the USA, and China.
- The Battle of Britain (1940) was Germany's failed air campaign against the UK.
- Operation Barbarossa (June 1941) opened the Eastern Front against the Soviet Union.
- Japan attacked Pearl Harbor on 7 December 1941, bringing the USA into the war.
- The Battle of Stalingrad (1942-43) was the Eastern Front's turning point.
- D-Day (6 June 1944) landed Allied forces in Normandy to open the Western Front.
- The Holocaust murdered six million Jews plus millions of others in Nazi genocide.
- Victory in Europe (VE Day) came on 8 May 1945 after Germany's surrender.
- Atomic bombs on Hiroshima (6 August) and Nagasaki (9 August 1945) preceded Japan's surrender.
- Victory over Japan (VJ Day) followed on 15 August 1945, formally signed 2 September.
- The United Nations was founded in 1945 to prevent future world wars.
- The war caused roughly 70-85 million deaths, the deadliest conflict in history.
- Codebreakers at Bletchley Park (Enigma) shortened the war through signals intelligence.
- The Manhattan Project developed the first atomic bombs under J. Robert Oppenheimer.
