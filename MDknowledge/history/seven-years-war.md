# Seven Years' War (example)

- 1756-1763 fighting spanned Europe, America, and India globally.
- Frederick the Great survived encirclement miraculously ('Miracle of Brandenburg').
- Plains of Abraham (1759) decided French versus British North America.
- British naval/finance edge compounded French land-power limits.
- Treaty of Paris (1763) ceded Canada and east-Mississippi lands.
- War debts drove postwar taxes sparking American Revolution.
- Pitt's strategy subsidized Prussia while winning colonies.
- Colonial militias (Washington included) trained for later rebellions.
- First 'world war' label fits its five-continent scope arguably.
- Prussia's survival forged German militarist mythology durably.
