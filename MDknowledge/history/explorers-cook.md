# Captain Cook (example)

- Cook charted Newfoundland, then Pacific voyages (1768-1779) systematically.
- Endeavour carried Transit-of-Venus scientists to Tahiti first.
- Australia's east coast claimed (1770) at Botany Bay.
- Hawaii named Sandwich Islands on 1778 visit; Cook died there 1779.
- Scurvy prevention (sauerkraut, malt) kept crews healthier than rivals'.
- Chronometer trials (K1 copy) solved longitude practically aboard.
- Antarctic Circle crossed first (1773) proving no great southern continent nearby.
- Charts stayed standard issue for over a century afterward.
- Nootka crisis nearly sparked Anglo-Spanish war over his claims.
- Statues debate imperial legacy across the Pacific today.
