# American Revolution (example)

- The American Revolution ran from 1775 to 1783 against British rule.
- Taxation without representation ('no taxation...') fueled colonial protests.
- The Boston Tea Party (1773) dumped taxed tea into the harbor.
- The Declaration of Independence was adopted 4 July 1776, drafted mainly by Jefferson.
- Washington's crossing of the Delaware (1776) revived the rebel cause.
- French aid after Saratoga (1777) proved decisive for the Americans.
- Yorktown (1781) trapped Cornwallis and effectively ended fighting.
- The Treaty of Paris (1783) recognized United States independence.
- The Constitution (1787) replaced the weak Articles of Confederation.
- The Bill of Rights (1791) added the first ten amendments protecting liberties.
