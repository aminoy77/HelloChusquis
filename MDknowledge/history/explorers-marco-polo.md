# Marco Polo (example)

- Marco Polo reached Kublai Khan's court (~1275) via Silk Road years.
- Rustichello ghost-wrote Travels from Genoese prison storytelling.
- Paper money, coal, and postal relays amazed European readers.
- Skeptics note omissions (tea, wall, foot-binding) questioning depths.
- Manuscript variants (Franco-Italian original lost) complicate verification.
- Columbus annotated his Polo copy sailing west to 'Cipangu'.
- Yangzhou governorship claims lack Chinese corroboration entirely.
- Descriptions match Persian sources where verifiable impressively.
- Venetian will frees Tartar servant Peter, hinting household truth.
- Whether merchant or fabulist, the book reshaped European Asia-images.
