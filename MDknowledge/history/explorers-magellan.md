# Magellan (example)

- Magellan's 1519 fleet first circumnavigated Earth (he died en route).
- Strait of Magellan threads Patagonia's tip past brutal weather.
- Pacific crossing starved crews 99 days without fresh provisions.
- Philippines (1521 Mactan) killed Magellan in local conflict.
- Elcano completed the circuit with 18 of 270 original men.
- Spice cargo (cloves) still profited despite catastrophic losses.
- International Date Line confusion cost the crew a calendar day famously.
- Victoria remains the only surviving ship's name in memory.
- Spain-Portugal rivalry funded the voyage past Tordesillas lines.
- Magellanic Clouds honor the captain in southern skies.
