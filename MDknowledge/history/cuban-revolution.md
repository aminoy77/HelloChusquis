# Cuban Revolution (example)

- Castro's 26th of July Movement toppled Batista on 1 Jan 1959.
- Granma landing (1956) began with 12 survivors from 82 expeditionaries.
- Sierra Maestra guerrillas plus urban underground combined effectively.
- Bay of Pigs (1961) humiliated CIA-backed exiles, consolidating Castro.
- Missile Crisis (1962) brought the world to nuclear brink over Cuba.
- US embargo (1960-) strangles the economy six decades running.
- Literacy/health campaigns achieved standout developing-world metrics.
- Mariel exodus (1980) plus later waves drained dissidents and talent.
- Special Period (post-Soviet collapse) immiserated the 1990s brutally.
- Succession (Raul, then Diaz-Canel 2018) preserved one-party continuity.
