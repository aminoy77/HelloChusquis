# American Civil War (example)

- Secession winter (1860-61) followed Lincoln's election; Fort Sumter opened fire.
- Antietam (1862, bloodiest day) enabled Emancipation Proclamation politically.
- Gettysburg (1863) plus Vicksburg split turned the war irreversibly.
- Total war (Sherman's March) targeted Southern will and logistics.
- Appomattox (April 1865) ended major fighting; surrenders dribbled after.
- Lincoln assassinated five days after Appomattox tragically.
- 620,000+ dead (2 percent of population) proportionally dwarfs later wars.
- 13th-15th Amendments abolished slavery plus promised citizenship/votes.
- Reconstruction's promise collapsed into Jim Crow by 1877 compromise.
- Battlefield preservation began with veterans' reunions themselves.
