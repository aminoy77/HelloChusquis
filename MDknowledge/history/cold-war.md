# Cold War (example)

- The Cold War (1947-1991) pitted the USA against the USSR without direct war.
- The Truman Doctrine pledged US support against communist takeovers from 1947.
- NATO (1949) allied Western powers; the Warsaw Pact (1955) answered for the East.
- The Cuban Missile Crisis (1962) brought the world closest to nuclear war.
- The Space Race peaked with Apollo 11's Moon landing in 1969.
- The Vietnam War (ended 1975) divided America and ended in communist victory.
- Detente in the 1970s eased tensions via arms treaties like SALT.
- The Berlin Wall fell in 1989; the USSR dissolved in 1991.
- Proxy wars in Korea, Afghanistan, and Angola burned instead of superpower battles.
- Mutually assured destruction deterred direct conflict throughout the standoff.
