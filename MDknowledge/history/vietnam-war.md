# Vietnam War (example)

- US combat role grew 1965-1973 over Vietnamese reunification struggle.
- Gulf of Tonkin (1964) authorized escalation on disputed attack reports.
- Tet Offensive (1968) shocked America despite being a military defeat for Hanoi.
- Guerrilla tunnels (Cu Chi) and Ho Chi Minh Trail sustained Southern insurgency.
- Agent Orange defoliation poisoned land and people for generations.
- My Lai massacre (1968) exposed war crimes and eroded home support.
- Draft lottery and campus protests (Kent State 1970) divided generations.
- Paris Accords (1973) exited US troops; Saigon fell in 1975.
- Vietnamese reunified under Hanoi; boat people fled afterward.
- Pentagon Papers leak (1971) revealed systematic official deception.
- Helicopter air mobility defined US tactics in jungle terrain.
- Napalm imagery (Phan Thi Kim Phuc) turned global opinion decisively.
- Vietnam syndrome constrained US interventions for decades after.
- Memorial Wall (1982) names 58,000+ American dead chronologically.
- Doi Moi reforms (1986) later marketized victorious Vietnam's economy.
