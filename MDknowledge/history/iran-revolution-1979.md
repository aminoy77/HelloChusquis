# Iranian Revolution 1979 (example)

- Shah's White Revolution plus SAVAK repression alienated broad coalitions.
- Khomeini's Paris tapes smuggled sermons past censorship efficiently.
- Black Friday (Sept 1978) massacres radicalized moderates decisively.
- Shah fled January 1979; Khomeini returned to millions greeting.
- Referendum made Iran an Islamic Republic (99% claimed) that April.
- Hostage crisis (1979-81, 444 days) doomed Carter and US ties.
- Cultural Revolution shuttered universities purging dissenters.
- Iraq invaded (1980), triggering eight years of trench warfare.
- Veiling laws plus morality policing reshaped public life permanently.
- Green Movement (2009) and later protests test legitimacy recurrently.
