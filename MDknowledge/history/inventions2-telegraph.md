# Telegraph (example)

- Morse's 1844 'What hath God wrought' opened US electric telegraphy.
- Dots/dashes encoded letters compactly for wire transmission.
- Transatlantic cable (1858, permanent 1866) shrank oceans to minutes.
- Railroad dispatching plus news wires built first real-time networks.
- Western Union monopolized US wires by the 1870s.
- Undersea cables extended empires' command into colonies instantly.
- Teleprinters automated Morse into typed messages by 1900s.
- Telephones eclipsed telegrams for conversation by mid-1900s.
- Last Western Union telegram sent in 2006 closed the era.
- QWERTY layouts descend from telegraph-era typewriter jams.
