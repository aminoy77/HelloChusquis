# Early computers (example)

- ENIAC (1945) weighed 30 tons computing artillery tables electronically.
- Transistors (1947 Bell Labs) replaced tubes enabling miniaturization.
- Stored-program architecture (von Neumann) made software rewritable.
- UNIVAC predicted Eisenhower's 1952 win on live TV famously.
- Mainframes batch-processed business data through the 1960s-70s.
- Microprocessors (Intel 4004, 1971) squeezed CPUs onto single chips.
- Apple II (1977) plus VisiCalc sold personal computing's first killer app.
- IBM PC (1981) standardized architecture clones competed on openly.
- Moore's Law (transistor doubling ~2 years) held for half a century.
- Museums (Bletchley, Mountain View) preserve Colossus and Apple I rarities.
