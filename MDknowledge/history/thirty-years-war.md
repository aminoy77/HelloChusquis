# Thirty Years' War (example)

- Defenestration of Prague (1618) launched Europe's catastrophic war.
- Religion, dynasty, and great-power rivalry fused inseparably.
- Wallenstein's armies lived off ravaged lands systematically.
- Swedish intervention (1630, Gustavus) Protestantized momentum decisively.
- Magdeburg's sack (1631) shocked contemporaries with its brutality.
- Westphalia (1648) birthed sovereign-state diplomacy from exhaustion.
- German lands lost a third or more of inhabitants in places.
- Mercenary economies normalized plunder as logistics horribly.
- France (Catholic) funded Protestants purely against Habsburgs.
- Peace congresses pioneered multilateral summitry still used.
