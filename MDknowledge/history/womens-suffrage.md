# Women's suffrage (example)

- Seneca Falls (1848) launched organized US suffrage demands.
- UK suffragettes (Pankhursts) escalated from petitions to militancy.
- New Zealand (1893) enfranchised women first nationally.
- US 19th Amendment (1920) followed wartime contributions plus protests.
- UK equal franchise arrived 1928 after partial 1918 concessions.
- Anti-suffragists (including women) argued separate-spheres ideology.
- Force-feeding jailed hunger strikers backfired publicly for governments.
- Suffrage colors (purple/white/green) branded marches brilliantly.
- Voting proved a beginning: pay and bodily autonomy fights continued.
- Saudi Arabia (2015) enfranchised women last among major states.
