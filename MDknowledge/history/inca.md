# Inca Empire (example)

- The Inca Empire ruled the Andes from its capital Cusco in the 1400s.
- Quechua was the empire's administrative language across the Andes.
- Inca roads spanned thousands of kilometers with relay runners (chasquis).
- Machu Picchu was a royal estate abandoned around the Spanish conquest.
- Inca stonework fits blocks without mortar so tightly blades cannot pass.
- Terraced farming plus freeze-dried potatoes fed mountain populations.
- Pizarro captured Atahualpa in 1532, toppling the empire swiftly.
- The Inca had no writing but recorded numbers on knotted quipu cords.
- Coricancha's gold-clad temple marked Cusco as the empire's sacred center.
- Inca mita labor drafts built roads, terraces, and fortresses communally.
