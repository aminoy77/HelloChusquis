# Ancient Greece (example)

- Athens and Sparta were rival Greek city-states with democratic and militaristic systems.
- Democracy emerged in Athens around 508 BC under Cleisthenes' reforms.
- The first Olympic Games were traditionally held in 776 BC at Olympia.
- The Persian Wars (490-479 BC) included Marathon, Thermopylae, and Salamis.
- The Parthenon was built on the Athenian Acropolis in the 5th century BC.
- Socrates taught by questioning; Plato founded the Academy; Aristotle tutored Alexander.
- Alexander the Great built an empire from Greece to India before dying in 323 BC.
- The Iliad and Odyssey, attributed to Homer, anchor Western literature.
- Greek tragedians Aeschylus, Sophocles, and Euripides premiered at Athenian festivals.
- The Peloponnesian War (431-404 BC) ended Athenian dominance in favor of Sparta.
- Hippocrates gives medicine its ethical oath and clinical tradition.
- Euclid's Elements systematized geometry around 300 BC in Alexandria.
- Archimedes computed pi bounds and formulated buoyancy in Syracuse.
- The Antikythera mechanism shows advanced Hellenistic astronomical engineering.
- Koine Greek spread by Alexander became the eastern Mediterranean's common tongue.
