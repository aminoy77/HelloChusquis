# Aztec Empire (example)

- The Aztecs built Tenochtitlan on a lake island, now Mexico City, in 1325.
- Chinampas (floating gardens) fed the Aztec capital intensively.
- The Templo Mayor honored war and rain gods at the city's heart.
- Aztec markets at Tlatelolco drew tens of thousands of daily traders.
- Human sacrifice fed Aztec cosmology on a massive ritual scale.
- Nahuatl was the empire's lingua franca across central Mexico.
- Cortes allied rival peoples and took Tenochtitlan in 1521.
- Smallpox devastated defenders faster than Spanish weapons did.
- Cacao beans served as Aztec currency for everyday purchases.
- Aztec calendars (260-day ritual plus 365-day solar) structured religious life.
