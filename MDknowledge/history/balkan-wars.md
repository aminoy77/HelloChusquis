- Second War (1913) saw allies turn on Bulgaria over spoils.
- Serbia's gains doubled territory, alarming Vienna fatally.
- Albanian independence (1912) emerged from the wreckage newborningly.
- Great-power meddling (London conferences) redrew lines repeatedly.
- Atrocities (all sides) foreshadowed 1990s Yugoslav horrors grimly.
- Young Turk officers honed in Balkan fighting later ruled Turkey.
- Assassination networks (Black Hand) gestated in Serbian irredentism.
- 1914 Sarajevo roots trace directly to Balkan powder-keg logic.
- Balkanization entered language meaning fragmentation generally.
