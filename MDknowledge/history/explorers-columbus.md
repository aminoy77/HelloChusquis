# Columbus (example)

- Columbus sailed 1492 seeking Asia, landing in the Caribbean instead.
- Nina, Pinta, Santa Maria carried ~90 men across the Atlantic.
- San Salvador (likely Bahamas) hosted the 12 October landfall.
- Four voyages (1492-1504) mapped Caribbean plus Central/South American coasts.
- Columbus died (1506) still claiming Asian outskirts, not a 'new' world.
- Columbian Exchange swapped crops, animals, diseases, and peoples brutally.
- Encomienda systems enslaved Indigenous labor under his governorship.
- Vespucci's letters, not Columbus's, named America via Waldseemuller maps.
- Indigenous Peoples' Day counter-observances reassess his legacy yearly.
-  underestimated Earth's size; luck plus currents saved the expedition.
