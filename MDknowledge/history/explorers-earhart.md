# Amelia Earhart (example)

- Earhart first woman solo Atlantic (1932) five years after Lindbergh.
- 1937 world-flight attempt vanished over the central Pacific.
- Howland Island radio schedules failed amid frequency confusion.
- Gardner Island (Nikumaroro) theory cites bones and artifacts inconclusively.
- Navy's $4M search (1937's fortune) found nothing conclusive.
- Ninety-Nines organization still advances women pilots in her name.
- Purdue funded her Lockheed Electra flying laboratory.
- Distinguished Flying Cross (first woman) honored the Atlantic solo.
- Fuel-range miscalculations likely doomed the Lae-Howland leg.
- Disappearance lore (spies, castaways) persists absent wreckage.
