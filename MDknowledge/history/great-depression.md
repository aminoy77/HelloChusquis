# Great Depression (example)

- US stock crash (Oct 1929) triggered decade-long global slump.
- Bank runs destroyed savings before deposit insurance existed.
- Unemployment hit 25 percent in America, worse in industrial Germany.
- Dust Bowl droughts devastated plains farming simultaneously.
- Smoot-Hawley tariffs deepened the spiral protectionistically.
- New Deal (relief, recovery, reform) expanded federal roles permanently.
- Keynesian deficit spending gained credibility fighting the slump.
- Authoritarians exploited misery (Nazis, militarists) catastrophically.
- WWII mobilization finally absorbed remaining unemployment fully.
- Social Security plus SEC endure as Depression-era institutional legacies.
