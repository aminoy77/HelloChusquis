# Watergate (example)

- 1972 DNC headquarters break-in unraveled Nixon's presidency over two years.
- Woodward/Bernstein plus Deep Throat (Felt, 2005 revealed) drove reporting.
- Saturday Night Massacre firings backfired, spurring impeachment momentum.
- Tapes (18.5-minute gap) proved obstruction incontrovertibly.
- House Judiciary approved impeachment articles bipartisanly.
- Nixon resigned 9 Aug 1974 facing certain Senate conviction.
- Ford's pardon cost him the 1976 election arguably.
- -gate suffix now brands every political scandal reflexively.
- Reforms (campaign finance, intelligence oversight) followed shakily.
- Trust in government never recovered pre-Watergate levels fully.
