# Electric battery (example)

- Volta's pile (1800) stacked zinc/copper discs, debuting steady current.
- Galvani's frog legs inspired (and misled) early animal-electricity debates.
- Daniell cells stabilized telegraph power through the 1800s.
- Lead-acid rechargeables (1859 Plante) still start cars today.
- Dry cells (1886 Gassner) freed flashlights from spillable jars.
- Alkaline chemistry (1950s) extended disposable runtimes dramatically.
- Lithium-ion (1991 Sony) enabled phones, laptops, and EVs.
- Flow batteries decouple power from energy for grid storage.
- Solid-state promises density plus safety in next generations.
- Recycling loops now recover cobalt/nickel/lithium economically.
