# Taj Mahal heritage (example)

- UNESCO listed the Taj in 1983 among India's first inscriptions.
- Full-moon night viewings (five monthly) sell out far ahead.
- Yamuna riverside plus Mathura day-trips extend Agra itineraries.
- Pollution (Mathura refinery) yellows marble despite court-ordered zones.
- Mud-pack (multani mitti) facials bleach marble gently yearly.
- Friday mosque closures pause tourist entries weekly.
- Foreign tickets cost ~20x domestic ones, funding upkeep.
- Guides (licensed) decode inlay symbolism plus photo angles.
- Foggy December dawns hide the dome unpredictably — check seasons.
- Mehtab Bagh across the river frames sunset views best.
