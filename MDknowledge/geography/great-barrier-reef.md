# Great Barrier Reef (example)

- The reef spans ~2,300 km of Queensland coast with ~3,000 individual reefs.
- Corals are colonies of polyps hosting symbiotic algae (zooxanthellae).
- Mass bleaching (2016, 2017, 2020s) expels algae under heat stress.
- Crown-of-thorns starfish outbreaks compound climate damage periodically.
- Reef tourism supports tens of thousands of Australian jobs.
- Marine park zoning balances fishing, tourism, and no-take sanctuaries.
- Sea turtles, dugongs, and reef sharks depend on connected habitats.
- Water quality (farm runoff) smothers inshore corals with sediment.
- Coral spawning synchronizes days after November full moons.
- Restoration trials (larval reseeding, heat-tolerant symbionts) scale up slowly.
- Captain Cook charted the reef after Endeavour grounded in 1770.
- Outer reefs recover faster than inshore ones from disturbances.
- Ocean acidification weakens skeletons as seas absorb CO₂.
- Traditional Owners co-manage sea country with park authorities.
- Satellite plus manta-tow surveys track reef health systematically.
