# Angkor Wat (example)

- Angkor Wat (~1113-1150) is the largest religious monument on Earth.
- Suryavarman II dedicated it to Vishnu before Buddhist conversion.
- Five lotus towers mirror Mount Meru's cosmic peaks.
- Moats (190 m wide) plus reservoirs managed monsoon waters masterfully.
- Bas-reliefs narrate heavens, hells, and Churning of Milk epics.
- Ta Prohm's strangler figs swallow ruins photogenically.
- Bayon's 216 smiling faces watch cardinal directions enigmatically.
- Khmer Empire housed ~1M people (largest preindustrial city) at peak.
- Water-management failure likely contributed to 15th-c. decline.
- Sunrise behind Angkor's towers rewards 5am arrivals daily.
