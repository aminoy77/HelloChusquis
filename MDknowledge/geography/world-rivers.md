# Major world rivers (example)

- The Nile (about 6,650 km) is usually cited as the world's longest river.
- The Amazon carries the largest discharge of any river and rivals the Nile in length.
- The Yangtze is Asia's longest river, flowing entirely within China.
- The Mississippi-Missouri system is North America's longest river system.
- The Danube flows through more countries (ten) than any other river.
- The Rhine is Western Europe's busiest commercial waterway.
- The Ganges is sacred in Hinduism and supports hundreds of millions of people.
- The Mekong runs through six Southeast Asian countries to the South China Sea.
- The Congo is the world's deepest river and second by discharge.
- The Volga is Europe's longest river, flowing entirely within Russia.
- The Murray-Darling is Australia's longest river system.
- The Orinoco dominates Venezuela's drainage into the Atlantic.
- The Lena, Ob, and Yenisei are Siberia's great northward-flowing rivers.
- The Tigris and Euphrates define Mesopotamia, the historic Fertile Crescent.
- The Thames flows through London; the Seine through Paris; the Tiber through Rome.
