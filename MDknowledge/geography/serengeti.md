# Serengeti (example)

- The Serengeti ecosystem spans Tanzania and Kenya's Maasai Mara.
- Two million wildebeest, zebra, and gazelle migrate cyclically with rains.
- Mara river crossings concentrate crocodile predation dramatically.
- Big cats follow herds: lions, leopards, and cheetahs all hunt here.
- Olduvai Gorge fossils record two million years of human evolution.
- Maasai pastoralists coexist (uneasily) with park boundaries.
- Hot-air balloon safaris drift dawn herds silently at treetop height.
- Calving season (Feb) drops half a million wildebeest calves in weeks.
- Tsetse belts historically limited cattle, preserving wildlife habitat.
- Poaching pressure shifted from elephants to bushmeat snares recently.
- Acacia savanna plus kopje outcrops define the classic landscape.
- Park fees fund anti-poaching units and community projects.
- Great Migration timing tracks rainfall, not calendars, yearly.
- Ngorongoro Crater's caldera concentrates wildlife uniquely nearby.
-  UNESCO status (1981) shields the ecosystem internationally.
