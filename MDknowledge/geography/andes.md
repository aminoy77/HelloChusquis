# Andes mountains (example)

- The Andes run about 8,900 km, the longest continental mountain range.
- Aconcagua (6,961 m) is the highest peak outside Asia.
- The range formed as the Nazca Plate subducts under South America.
- Altiplano plateaus (Bolivia/Peru) host cities above 3,500 meters.
- Inca terraces farmed impossible slopes with irrigation mastery.
- Atacama's rain shadow makes it Earth's driest non-polar desert.
- Andean condors soar three-meter wingspans on thermal currents.
- Potosi silver financed the Spanish Empire from Bolivian mines.
- Coffee grows on Colombian and Ecuadorian Andean slopes famously.
- Earthquakes (Chile 1960, largest recorded) mark the subduction violence.
- Llamas and alpacas adapted as pack and wool animals to altitude.
- Lake Titicaca's reed islands host Uros communities traditionally.
- Patagonian Andes end in fjords, glaciers, and granite towers.
- Quito and Bogota rank among the world's highest capitals.
- Glacier retreat threatens Lima and La Paz dry-season water.
