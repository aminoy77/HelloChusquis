# Famous volcanoes (example)

- Vesuvius (79 AD) buried Pompeii, preserving Roman life in ash.
- Etna (Sicily) erupts near-continuously, Europe's most active.
- Fuji last erupted (1707) beautifully dormant above Tokyo.
- Kilauea's effusive flows add Hawaiian landmass steadily.
- Krakatoa's 1883 blast dimmed global skies for years.
- St. Helens (1980) lateral blast flattened 600 km² of forest.
- Mauna Loa is Earth's largest subaerial volcano by volume.
- Arenal (Costa Rica) glowed nightly until 2010 slumber.
- Eyjafjallajokull (2010) grounded European aviation for a week.
- Yellowstone's caldera dwarfs all above as a sleeping supervolcano.
