# Siberia (example)

- Siberia spans ~13M km² (77 percent of Russia) with ~25 percent of people.
- Oymyakon (-67.7°C) contests coldest inhabited place records.
- Trans-Siberian Railway (9,289 km) remains the longest line on Earth.
- Lake Baikal holds a fifth of unfrozen surface fresh water.
- Permafrost thaw buckles roads and releases methane ominously.
- Gulag camps dotted Kolyma and Vorkuta infamously under Stalin.
- Mammoth tusks surface from thawing yedoma commercially now.
- Taiga (boreal forest) forms Earth's largest land biome here.
- Novosibirsk anchors Siberian science (Akademgorodok) since the 1950s.
- Diamond mines (Mirny) pit the permafrost spectacularly.
