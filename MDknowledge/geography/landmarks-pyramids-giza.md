# Pyramids of Giza (example)

- Khufu's pyramid (146 m originally) held tallest-structure record ~3,800 years.
- 2.3M blocks averaging 2.5 tonnes compose the Great Pyramid.
- Khafre's keeps its capstone casing plus Sphinx-guard adjacency.
- Menkaure's smaller pyramid completes the trio southwest.
- Worker cemeteries prove paid crews, not slaves, built them.
- Alignment to cardinal points stays within fractions of a degree.
- Sound-and-light shows narrate nightly from the Sphinx terrace.
- Camel rides circle the plateau in under an hour typically.
- Cairo sprawl now reaches the desert plateau's edge.
- Solar boats (Khufu ship) await museum display beside the pyramid.
