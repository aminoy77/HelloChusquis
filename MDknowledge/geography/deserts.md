# World deserts (example)

- The Sahara is the largest hot desert, spanning much of North Africa.
- Antarctica's Polar Desert is technically the world's largest desert overall.
- The Arabian Desert covers most of the Arabian Peninsula in sand and gravel.
- The Gobi in Mongolia and China is a cold desert with freezing winters.
- The Kalahari's red sands span Botswana, Namibia, and South Africa.
- The Atacama in Chile is the driest non-polar desert on Earth.
- The Mojave holds Death Valley, North America's hottest recorded place.
- The Sonoran's saguaro cacti define the US-Mexico border landscape.
- The Namib's Sossusvlei dunes rank among the world's tallest sand dunes.
- Deserts cover about a third of Earth's land surface in total.
