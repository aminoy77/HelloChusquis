# Australian Outback (example)

- Outback covers ~70 percent of Australia's landmass sparsely.
- Uluru's monolith glows red at dawn/dusk spiritually for Anangu owners.
- Kata Tjuta domes plus Kings Canyon slot nearby itineraries.
- Royal Flying Doctor Service covers stations the size of nations.
- School of the Air schooled isolated children by radio/internet.
- Road trains (53-meter trucks) haul mines-to-ports relentlessly.
- Feral camels (millions) roam descendants of Afghan imports.
- Coober Pedy residents live underground escaping 50°C heat.
- Stuart Highway (3,000 km) bisects Darwin to Adelaide famously.
- Night skies plus silence define the interior's stark appeal.
