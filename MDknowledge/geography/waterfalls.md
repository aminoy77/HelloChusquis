# Great waterfalls (example)

- Angel Falls (979 m, Venezuela) is the world's tallest waterfall.
- Tugela Falls (948 m, South Africa) rivals Angel by some surveys.
- Victoria Falls (1,708 m wide) is the largest curtain of falling water.
- Iguazu's 275 cascades span Argentina-Brazil thunderously.
- Niagara moves the most North American water scenically.
- Kaieteur (Guyana) drops 226 m in single rainforest plunge.
- Yosemite Falls (739 m) ranks North America's tallest cascade.
- Dudhsagar (India) whites like milk (its namesake) in monsoon.
- Dettifoss (Iceland) thunders Europe's most powerful flow rate.
- Multnomah's two tiers front Oregon postcards iconically.
