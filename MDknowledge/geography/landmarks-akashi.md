# Akashi Kaikyo Bridge (example)

- Akashi Kaikyo (1,991 m center span) is the world's longest suspension span.
- Kobe earthquake (1995) lengthened the unfinished span a full meter mid-build.
- Two-hinged stiffening trusses resist typhoon winds over 80 m/s.
- Tuned mass dampers steady towers against quakes and gusts.
- Deck carries six road lanes plus maintenance rails exclusively.
- Maiko observatory overlooks the strait construction saga.
- Deep caisson foundations settled 60 meters below sea level.
- Spinning compacted 300,000 km of wire into main cables.
- Night lighting shifts colors for local festivals gracefully.
- Wind-tunnel testing at 1/100 scale preceded construction thoroughly.
