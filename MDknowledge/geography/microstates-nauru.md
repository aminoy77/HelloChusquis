# Nauru (example)

- Nauru (21 km²) is the world's smallest island nation.
- Phosphate mining enriched then devastated the interior plateau.
- Yaren district hosts government (no official capital exists).
- Nauru Airlines' single-island hopping defines Pacific remoteness.
- Obesity/diabetes rates top global charts tragically.
- Australian detention center hosting dominated recent politics.
- Buada Lagoon's freshwater surprises amid coral atoll aridity.
- Command Ridge preserves Japanese WWII guns and bunkers.
- Rehabilitation funds replant mined-out Topside gradually.
- Fishing licenses (Vessel Day Scheme) now fund budgets substantially.
