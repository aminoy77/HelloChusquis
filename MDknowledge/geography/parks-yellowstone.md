# Yellowstone (example)

- Yellowstone (1872) was the world's first national park.
- Old Faithful erupts ~90-minute intervals predictably for crowds.
- Grand Prismatic's rainbow bacteria span football-field widths.
- Supervolcano caldera (640,000 years ago) still lifts the ground measurably.
- Wolves reintroduced (1995) rebalanced elk-browsed valleys trophically.
- Bison herds (~4,000) jam roads during summer rut reliably.
- Lamar Valley dawn patrols spot bears and wolves best.
- Winter (snowcoach only) reveals steamy solitude minus crowds.
- Campgrounds book out the morning reservations release yearly.
- Thermal burns injure more visitors than wildlife encounters do.
