# Grenada (example)

- Grenada's 'Spice Isle' grows nutmeg and mace famously.
- St George's horseshoe harbor ranks among the Caribbean's prettiest.
- US invasion (1983) followed Bishop's coup and airport-crisis escalation.
- Underwater sculpture park snorkels art onto seabeds uniquely.
- Grand Anse's two-mile beach anchors resort life.
- Chocolate (Tree-to-Bar) cooperatives add value locally.
- Oil/nutmeg price swings buffet tiny economies cyclically.
- St George's University trains doctors from 140+ nations.
- Carnival (Spicemas, August) paints streets joyously.
- Dive wrecks (Bianca C) rank regionally for advanced divers.
