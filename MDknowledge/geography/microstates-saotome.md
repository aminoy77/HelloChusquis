# Sao Tome and Principe (example)

- Twin-island nation floats in the Gulf of Guinea (~220,000 people).
- Sao Tome town governs; plantation rocas dot both islands.
- Cocoa once made it the world's top producer (early 1900s).
- Chocolate tourism now revives estate heritage deliciously.
- Obô parks shelter endemic birds (dwarf ibis, grosbeak).
- Equator markers invite straddling-hemispheres photos.
- Portuguese creole (Forro) flavors daily speech musically.
- Coup attempts punctuate politics without lasting damage historically.
- Turtle nesting (Jalé beach) draws conservation volunteers.
- Oil prospects offshore promise (and threaten) futures debatably.
