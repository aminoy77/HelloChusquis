# Christ the Redeemer (example)

- Corcovado's Christ (30 m + 8 m pedestal) overlooks Rio since 1931.
- French sculptor Landowski designed; Brazilian engineer da Silva Costa built.
- Soapstone tiles skin the reinforced-concrete Art Deco figure.
- Lightning strikes regularly; repairs follow each major hit.
- Arms span 28 meters embracing the city symbolically.
- Cog-train climbs Tijuca forest slopes to the summit.
- Night floodlighting colors causes (pink October, etc.) yearly.
- 2014 World Cup crowds watched matches from its feet.
- New Seven Wonders listing (2007) boosted visitation hugely.
- Advance tickets beat multi-hour summit queues reliably.
