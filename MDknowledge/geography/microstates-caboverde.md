# Cabo Verde (example)

- Cabo Verde's ten volcanic islands sit 570 km off Senegal.
- Praia (Santiago) governs; Mindelo (Sao Vicente) cultures the north.
- Droughts historically forced mass emigration (New England diaspora).
- Morna music (Cesaria Evora) mourns saudade globally.
- Creole (Kriolu) bridges Portuguese officialdom and daily life.
- Salt pans (Sal) plus turtles anchor ecotourism growth.
- Wind sports (kite/surf) exploit trade-wind reliability.
- Democratic stability outshines regional peers consistently.
- Inter-island ferries/flights schedule around swells pragmatically.
- Grogu (sugarcane spirit) fuels coladeira nightlife locally.
