# Eiffel Tower (example)

- The Eiffel Tower (330 m with antennas) anchors Paris's Champ de Mars.
- Gustave Eiffel's company built it for the 1889 World's Fair.
- Critics called it ugly; Parisians now defend it fiercely.
- Repainting every seven years consumes ~60 tonnes of paint.
- Radio antennas atop saved it from planned demolition after 1909.
- Summit winds sway the iron lattice measurably in storms.
- Night sparkles (hourly, 5 minutes) use copyrighted lighting design.
- Stair climbers face 1,665 steps to the top platform.
- Second-floor restaurants (Jules Verne) book out months ahead.
- Most-visited paid monument: ~7 million yearly pre-pandemic.
