# Stonehenge (example)

- Stonehenge's sarsens (~2500 BC) plus bluestones stage solstice alignments.
- Bluestones traveled 225 km from Welsh Preseli hills mysteriously.
- Heel Stone frames midsummer sunrise down the ceremonial avenue.
- Construction spanned ~1,500 years across distinct cultural phases.
-  Cremations (150+) make it Britain's early Bronze Age cemetery partly.
- Lintel joints (tongue-and-groove) locked uprights earthquake-tight.
- Visitor center shuttles replaced roadside parking to restore setting.
- Solstice crowds (20,000+) access inner circle twice yearly.
- Nearby Durrington Walls housed builders seasonally (pig-bone evidence).
- Laser scans revealed 71 Bronze Age carvings (axes, daggers) in 2003.
