# Taj Mahal (example)

- Shah Jahan built the Taj (1632-53) for beloved Mumtaz Mahal.
- White Makrana marble inlaid with jasper, jade, and carnelian pietra dura.
- Minarets tilt outward so quakes drop them away from the tomb.
- Symmetry breaks only at Shah Jahan's own off-center cenotaph.
- Yamuna riverfront mists frame dawn views famously.
- Friday mosque plus guest house balance the complex religiously.
- Pollution yellowing prompts regular mud-pack cleaning treatments.
- 200M+ annual visitors strain Agra's infrastructure seasonally.
- UNESCO listed it in 1983 among first Indian inscriptions.
- Moonlit viewings (five nights monthly) sell out far ahead.
