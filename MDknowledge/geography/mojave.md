# Mojave desert (example)

- Mojave spans California/Nevada/Arizona between mountain rain shadows.
- Death Valley (282 ft below sea level) holds heat records (134°F, 1913).
- Joshua trees grow only in Mojave's narrow elevation band.
- Las Vegas sprawls at its northeastern edge improbably green.
- Edwards AFB lakebeds land space shuttles and test jets.
- Desert tortoises (threatened) bulldoze burrows sheltering 300+ species.
- Solar farms (Ivanpah) harvest fierce insolation controversially.
- Route 66 nostalgia crosses its ghost towns photogenically.
- Spring superblooms carpet valleys after wet winters rarely.
- Military ranges restrict huge tracts (Fort Irwin training).
