# Plitvice Lakes (example)

- Plitvice's 16 terraced lakes cascade via travertine dams uniquely.
- Boardwalks thread waterfalls at water level intimately.
- Lower lakes (canyon) plus Upper lakes (forest) split visits naturally.
- Early entry beats midday tour-bus floods reliably.
- Swimming bans (since 2006) protect travertine-forming mosses.
- Autumn colors plus low water reveal textures summer crowds miss.
- UNESCO listed the park in 1979 among first natural sites.
- Bears, wolves, and lynx roam forests beyond boardwalk bustle.
- Two-day tickets allow unhurried upper-plus-lower coverage.
- Zagreb day-trips pair Plitvice with Rastoke village mills.
