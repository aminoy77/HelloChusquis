# Gobi desert (example)

- Gobi spans Mongolia/China as a cold desert of rock, not just dunes.
- Winters plunge to -40°C, summers exceed 40°C violently.
- Flaming Cliffs (Bayanzag) yielded first dinosaur eggs scientifically.
- Khongoryn Els dunes sing (booming sands) under ideal winds.
- Two-humped Bactrian camels endure its extremes natively.
- Trans-Mongolian Railway crosses its emptiness dramatically.
- Genghis's empire mustered around its southern oases historically.
- Cashmere goats overgraze fringes, expanding desertification measurably.
- Shambhala myths place hidden kingdoms beyond its horizons.
- Ger camps host tourists in traditional felt dwellings.
