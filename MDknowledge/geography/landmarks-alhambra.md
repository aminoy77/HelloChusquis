# Alhambra (example)

- Granada's Alhambra palaces crown Nasrid power (1238-1492).
- Court of Lions' fountain rests on twelve marble beasts enigmatically.
- Muqarnas (honeycomb vaults) dissolve stone into starry geometry.
- Poetic inscriptions ('no victor but God') wallpaper stucco bands.
- Generalife summer gardens cascade water through cypress alleys.
- Catholic Monarchs conquered it 1492, same year as Columbus sailed.
- Washington Irving's Tales revived Romantic fascination from its rooms.
- Charles V's Renaissance palace interrupts Islamic fabric famously.
- Night visits illuminate Nasrid plasterwork dramatically.
- Ticket caps sell out weeks ahead in high season reliably.
