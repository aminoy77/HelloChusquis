# Sagrada Familia (example)

- Gaudi's Sagrada Familia has built in Barcelona since 1882.
- Completion targets the 2026 centenary of Gaudi's death.
- Eighteen planned spires symbolize apostles, evangelists, Mary, and Christ.
- Nature-mimicking columns branch like trees inside the nave.
- Gaudi devoted his final 15 years exclusively to the basilica.
- Anarchists burned plans in 1936; architects reconstructed from fragments.
- Stained glass bathes mornings east-blue, evenings west-red deliberately.
- UNESCO listed its Nativity facade and crypt in 2005.
- Entry fees fully fund construction without state money.
- Hyperboloid vaults achieve height with minimal material structurally.
