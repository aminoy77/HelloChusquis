# Alps (example)

- The Alps arc 1,200 km across eight countries from France to Slovenia.
- Mont Blanc (4,808 m) crowns Western Europe's rooftop.
- Matterhorn's pyramid defines Swiss-Italian border postcards.
- Alpine tourism (skiing, hiking) underpins mountain economies.
- Transalpine tunnels (Gotthard Base, 57 km) speed freight underneath.
- Glaciers like Aletsch retreat visibly within single lifetimes now.
- Alpine farming (transhumance) moves herds seasonally by tradition.
- Avalanche forecasting (bulletins 1-5) governs winter backcountry safety.
- St. Bernard dogs rescued pass travelers for centuries (mythologized barrels aside).
- Ötzi the Iceman preserved 5,300-year-old Alpine life in ice.
- Via ferratas wire cliff routes for summer hikers safely.
- Alpine ibex rebounded from near-extinction under protection.
- Dolomites' pale rock glows pink at sunset (enrosadira).
- Chamonix hosted the first Winter Olympics in 1924.
- Climate shifts push ski resorts toward snowmaking dependence.
