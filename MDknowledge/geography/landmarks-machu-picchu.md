# Machu Picchu (example)

- Hiram Bingham publicized Machu Picchu in 1911 (locals always knew it).
- Inca dry-stone walls survive quakes that topple modern buildings.
- Intihuatana stone likely tracked solar events ritually.
- Huayna Picchu climbs reward with postcard panoramas (permit needed).
- Cloud forest setting hosts orchids and spectacled bears nearby.
- Daily caps plus timed circuits manage tourist pressure now.
- Inca Trail (4 days) approaches through Sun Gate traditionally.
- Purpose debated: royal estate (Pachacuti) leads current theories.
- Llamas graze terraces photogenically for visitor delight.
- Aguas Calientes below offers hot springs and train access.
