# Kalahari (example)

- Kalahari's red sands span Botswana, Namibia, and South Africa.
- Semi-arid savanna (not true desert) supports surprising wildlife density.
- Meerkats (Kalahari-studied for decades) model cooperative breeding famously.
- San peoples thrived hunter-gatherer here for 20,000+ years.
- Okavango Delta floods miraculously into its northwest seasonally.
- Central Kalahari Game Reserve is among Earth's largest protected areas.
- Deception Valley's brown hyenas star in nature documentaries.
- Diamond mines (Jwaneng) fund Botswana from Kalahari pipes.
- Brief rains transform dunes into green grazing within weeks.
- Night skies rank among the planet's darkest for stargazing.
