# Sydney Harbour Bridge (example)

- Harbour Bridge ('Coathanger') opened 1932 linking Sydney shores.
- Arch span (503 m) carried trains, cars, bikes, and pedestrians together.
- BridgeClimb guides summit seekers 134 meters above harbor.
- Depression-era jobs (1,400 men) built it through hard years.
- Paintwork never ends: crews cycle the steel continuously.
- New Year's fireworks launch from its arch globally televised.
- Pylon lookouts (southeast) offer cheaper skyline views.
- Protest marches (2000 reconciliation walk) crossed symbolically.
- Arch halves joined with millimeter precision in 1930.
- Ferry rides underneath frame the arch classically.
