# Big Ben (example)

- Big Ben properly names the Great Bell, not the clock tower itself.
- The tower (renamed Elizabeth Tower, 2012) stands 96 meters tall.
- Great Bell weighs 13.7 tonnes with its famous E-note strike.
- Parliament's clock keeps time within a second via penny-weighted pendulum.
- WWII bomb damage silenced chimes briefly; tower survived the Blitz.
- 2017-2022 restoration cleaned stonework and regilded dials.
- Bongs mark Remembrance Sunday and New Year nationally.
- Prisoners once held in the tower include suffragettes famously.
- Gas lighting gave way to electric dials in the tower long ago.
- Tours admit UK residents via MPs with months-long queues.
