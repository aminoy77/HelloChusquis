# Amazon rainforest (example)

- Amazonia spans nine countries with Brazil holding the majority share.
- The Amazon river discharges more than the next seven rivers combined.
- Rainforest canopy layers (emergent to understory) stack distinct ecosystems.
- About 10 percent of known species live in the Amazon basin.
- Rubber boom towns (Manaus opera house) recall extraction wealth.
- Deforestation for cattle and soy peaked alarmingly in the 2000s-2010s.
- Indigenous territories demonstrably slow forest loss where recognized.
- Flying rivers (atmospheric moisture) water South American farmlands.
- Pink river dolphins navigate flooded-forest (varzea) seasons.
- Forest fires are mostly human-set for clearing, not natural cycles.
- Brazil nuts harvest wild without felling, modeling sustainable use.
- Canopy walkways and towers opened research to non-climbers.
- Tipping-point models warn savannization past ~20-25 percent loss.
- Chocolate, rubber, and quinine all originated as Amazonian plants.
- Satellite monitoring (PRODES/DETER) tracks clearing in near real time.
