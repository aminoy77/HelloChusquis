# Torres del Paine (example)

- Torres' granite towers anchor Patagonia's premier trekking park.
- W trek (4-5 days) samples towers, Grey glacier, and French Valley.
- O circuit (8-10 days) circles the whole Paine massif completely.
- Patagonian weather flips sunshine-to-sleet within hours notoriously.
- Guanacos plus condors animate steppe approaches reliably.
- Refugios book out months ahead; campsites need reservations too.
- Grey Glacier kayaks paddle among calving blue icebergs.
- Wind (100+ km/h gusts) tests tent stakes and tempers equally.
- Shoulder seasons (Oct/Nov) balance crowds against closures.
- Puerto Natales outfitters rent every missing gear item affordably.
