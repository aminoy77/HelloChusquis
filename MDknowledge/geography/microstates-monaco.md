# Monaco (example)

- Monaco packs ~39,000 residents into 2 km² of Riviera coast.
- Grimaldis have ruled since 1297 (minus revolutionary gaps).
- Monte Carlo casino plus Grand Prix define glamour globally.
- No income tax draws wealthy residents famously.
- Prince's Palace guard change entertains daily at 11:55.
- Oceanographic Museum (Cousteau-directed) anchors marine science.
- Condamine market halls feed locals affordably amid luxury.
- Land reclamation (Portier Cove) grows territory seaward expensively.
- AS Monaco football competes in France's Ligue 1.
- Yacht shows each September flaunt floating wealth spectacularly.
