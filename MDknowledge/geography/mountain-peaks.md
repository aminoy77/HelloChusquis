# Great mountain peaks (example)

- Mount Everest (8,849 m) is the highest peak above sea level.
- K2 is the second-highest peak and among the deadliest to climb.
- Kangchenjunga ranks third highest on the Nepal-India border.
- Aconcagua is the highest peak outside Asia, in Argentina.
- Denali is North America's highest peak, in Alaska.
- Kilimanjaro is Africa's highest peak, a dormant Tanzanian volcano.
- Mont Blanc is the highest Alpine peak on the French-Italian border.
- Mount Vinson crowns Antarctica as its highest peak.
- Puncak Jaya is Oceania's highest peak in New Guinea.
- Mount Kosciuszko is mainland Australia's highest peak.
