# Famous straits (example)

- Gibraltar (14 km) joins Atlantic and Mediterranean strategically.
- Bosphorus threads Istanbul linking Black Sea to Marmara.
- Malacca funnels a third of global trade past Singapore.
- Hormuz (33 km narrowest) carries a fifth of oil shipments.
- Bering Strait (82 km) separates Russia from Alaska.
- Magellan's strait threads Patagonia's tip tempestuously.
- Dover Strait (33 km) is the Channel's busiest crossing.
- Taiwan Strait separates the island from mainland China tensely.
- Messina Strait's whirlpools inspired Scylla/Charybdis myths.
- Oresund Bridge-tunnel links Denmark and Sweden fixedly.
