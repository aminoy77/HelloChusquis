# US states guide (example)

- California (Sacramento) is the most populous US state.
- Texas (Austin) is the second most populous and second largest state.
- Florida (Tallahassee) is the third most populous state.
- Alaska (Juneau) is the largest state by area.
- Rhode Island (Providence) is the smallest state by area.
- Hawaii (Honolulu) is the only state of islands and the newest besides Alaska.
- Vermont's Montpelier is the smallest state capital by population.
- New York's Albany is often confused with New York City, the largest city.
- Illinois's Springfield is often confused with Chicago, the largest city.
- Nevada's Carson City is often confused with Las Vegas, the largest city.
