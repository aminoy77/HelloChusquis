# Kyrgyzstan

- The capital of Kyrgyzstan is Bishkek.
- Kyrgyzstan is in Central Asia.
- The official languages of Kyrgyzstan are Kyrgyz and Russian.
- The currency of Kyrgyzstan is the Kyrgyzstani som.
- About 7 million people live in Kyrgyzstan.
- Lake Issyk-Kul never freezes despite its mountain altitude.
