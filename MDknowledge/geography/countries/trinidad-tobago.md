# Trinidad and Tobago

- The capital of Trinidad and Tobago is Port of Spain.
- Trinidad and Tobago is in the Caribbean.
- People in Trinidad and Tobago speak English.
- The currency of Trinidad and Tobago is the Trinidad and Tobago dollar.
- About 1.5 million people live in Trinidad and Tobago.
- Trinidad is the birthplace of calypso and steelpan.
