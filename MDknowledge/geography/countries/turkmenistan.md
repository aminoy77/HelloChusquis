# Turkmenistan

- The capital of Turkmenistan is Ashgabat.
- Turkmenistan is in Central Asia.
- People in Turkmenistan speak Turkmen.
- The currency of Turkmenistan is the Turkmenistani manat.
- About 7 million people live in Turkmenistan.
- The Darvaza gas crater has burned in Turkmenistan for decades.
