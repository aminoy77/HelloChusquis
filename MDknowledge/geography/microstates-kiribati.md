# Kiribati (example)

- Kiribati's 33 atolls straddle equator plus International Date Line.
- Kiritimati (Christmas Island) is the world's largest coral atoll.
- A 1995 date-line shift made Kiribati first to see the year 2000.
- South Tarawa crowds half the nation's ~120,000 residents.
- Sea-level rise threatens freshwater lenses existentially.
- Migration-with-dignity policies prepare for worst-case relocation.
- WWII Tarawa battle (1943) cost Marines dearly in 76 hours.
- Causeways link South Tarawa islets into one urban strip.
- Copra and fishing licenses carry the tiny economy.
- Phoenix Islands Protected Area ranks among largest marine reserves.
