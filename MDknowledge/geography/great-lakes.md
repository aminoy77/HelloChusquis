# Great lakes (example)

- The Caspian Sea is the world's largest lake by area and volume.
- Lake Superior is the largest freshwater lake by surface area.
- The African Great Lakes (Victoria, Tanganyika, Malawi) hold a quarter of Earth's fresh surface water.
- Lake Baikal is the deepest lake (over 1,600 m) and oldest at ~25 million years.
- The Great Lakes (Superior, Michigan, Huron, Erie, Ontario) straddle the US-Canada border.
- Lakes Michigan and Huron are hydrologically one lake joined at Mackinac.
- The Dead Sea is Earth's lowest lake surface, about 430 m below sea level.
- The Great Salt Lake's salinity exceeds the ocean's, floating swimmers easily.
- Lake Titicaca is the highest large navigable lake, in the Andes.
- The Aral Sea shrank catastrophically after Soviet river diversions.
