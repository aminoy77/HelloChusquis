# Neuschwanstein (example)

- Ludwig II's fairy-tale castle rose over Bavaria from 1868.
- Wagner operas inspired every themed hall's murals directly.
- Throne Hall's Byzantine dome was never consecrated for use.
- Ludwig slept 11 nights there before mysterious 1886 death.
- Disney's Cinderella castle copies its silhouette famously.
- Summer crowds exceed 6,000 daily; winter visits breathe easier.
- Marienbrucke bridge frames the classic postcard angle.
- Unfinished interiors (chapel, keep) reveal construction halts.
- Tour routes cover 30 minutes of 360+ rooms selectively.
- Hohenschwangau (father's castle) neighbors across the valley.
