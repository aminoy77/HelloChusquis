# Kruger (example)

- Kruger (1898/1926) spans ~20,000 km² of South African bushveld.
- Big Five (lion, leopard, buffalo, elephant, rhino) anchor safari dreams.
- Self-drive paved loops plus rest camps democratize safaris affordably.
- Dawn/dusk game drives catch predators active around temperature edges.
- Rhino poaching wars deploy rangers, dogs, and drones intensely.
- February calving plus migrant birds reward green-season visits.
- Private concessions (Sabi Sand) guarantee off-road sightings luxuriously.
- Malaria prophylaxis belongs in every Kruger packing list.
- Skukuza airport flies visitors straight into the park.
- Walking trails with armed guides thrill on foot safely.
