# Asian islands (example)

- Borneo (shared 3 ways) holds Earth's oldest rainforests (~140M years).
- Sumatra's Lake Toba fills a supervolcano caldera massively.
- Java packs 150M people, over half of Indonesia, densely.
- Honshu holds Tokyo, Osaka, Kyoto plus Mount Fuji.
- Hokkaido's powder draws skiers; Okinawa's reefs divers.
- Jeju's lava tubes plus haenyeo divers define Korean island culture.
- Hainan markets itself as China's Hawaii for domestic tourists.
- Sri Lanka's Adam's Peak pilgrims climb multiple faiths' sacred summit.
- Taiwan's Taroko Gorge marbles a must-see canyon drive.
- Philippines' 7,600 islands frustrate counts (tide-dependent totals).
