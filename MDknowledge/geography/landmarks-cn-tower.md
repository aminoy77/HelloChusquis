# CN Tower (example)

- Toronto's CN Tower (553 m) held the tallest-tower record 1976-2007.
- Built for TV/radio transmission over growing skyscrapers.
- Glass Floor (342 m) tests nerves above Yonge Street directly.
- EdgeWalk harnesses visitors around the roof rim (356 m).
- Concrete slip-forming poured continuously for 40 months.
- Lightning strikes dozens of times yearly by design attraction.
- 360 Restaurant revolves fully every 72 minutes up top.
- Climbing its 1,776 stairs races fundraisers annually.
- Microwave horns relayed signals before fiber dominance.
- Illumination themes mark city events nightly.
