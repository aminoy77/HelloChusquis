# Great Wall of China (example)

- Warring-state walls joined under Qin (220s BC) into early Great Wall.
- Ming brick facing (1368-1644) built most visited sections standing.
- Total branches exceed 21,000 km across dynasties collectively.
- Beacon towers relayed smoke/fire signals along ridges rapidly.
- Sticky-rice mortar strengthened Ming masonry legendarily.
- Badaling crowds versus Mutianyu/Jiankou wildness split visitor tastes.
- Jiayuguan fort ends the wall in Gobi desert dramatically.
- Shanhaiguan meets the Bohai Sea at the eastern 'Old Dragon Head'.
- Wall mythology (visible from Moon) is false; low orbit barely shows it.
- Erosion plus tourism wear demand constant restoration triage.
