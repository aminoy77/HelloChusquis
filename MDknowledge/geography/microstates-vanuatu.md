# Vanuatu (example)

- Vanuatu's 83 islands arc through the southwest Pacific.
- Port Vila (Efate) plus Luganville (Espiritu Santo) anchor life.
- Anglo-French Condominium ruled jointly (and absurdly) until 1980.
- Mount Yasur's accessible lava shows thrill guided tourists nightly.
- Pentecost land diving inspired bungee jumping directly.
- Cargo cults (John Frum) await symbolic American plenty still.
- Bislama creole unites 100+ indigenous languages pragmatically.
- Cyclone Pam (2015) flattened much infrastructure catastrophically.
- Beachfront kava bars (nakamals) socialize men traditionally.
- Diving (Million Dollar Point) explores bulldozed US surplus underwater.
