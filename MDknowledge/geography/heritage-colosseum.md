# Colosseum heritage (example)

- UNESCO's Rome listing (1980) centers on Colosseum plus Forum ensemble.
- Arena floor partial reconstructions host concerts occasionally.
- Underground (hypogeum) tours reveal lift-and-trapdoor stagecraft.
- Night visits cut crowds plus heat dramatically in summer.
- Roma Pass bundles transit with first-museum entries economically.
- Gladiator school (Ludus Magnus) ruins neighbor the east side.
- Vegetation once rooted ( Fiori's flora catalogued 1643 species!) cleaned since.
- Good Friday Via Crucis papal processions start here yearly.
- Restoration (Tod's-funded, €25M) scrubbed two millennia of grime.
- Timed entries replaced endless lines since reservation mandates.
