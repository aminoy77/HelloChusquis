# Empire State Building (example)

- Empire State (443 m with antenna) ruled tallest 1931-1970 in New York.
- Built in 13 months during the Depression by 3,400 workers.
- Art Deco setbacks follow 1916 zoning laws sculpturally.
- Observatories (86th/102nd floors) draw millions yearly.
- Top colors signal holidays, teams, and causes nightly.
- B-25 bomber struck the 79th floor in 1945 fog, killing 14.
- King Kong's 1933 finale mythologized the mooring mast.
- Dirigible docking plans never worked practically in winds.
- LED retrofit (2012) enables million-color dynamic shows.
- LEED Gold retrofit proved historic towers green efficiently.
