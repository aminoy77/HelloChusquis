# Pyramids of Giza heritage (example)

- Giza's pyramids anchor Egypt's top UNESCO inscription since 1979.
- Khufu's pyramid held tallest-structure record for ~3,800 years.
- Sphinx guards Khafre's causeway with lion body, human head.
- Worker cemeteries prove paid crews built them, not slaves.
- Sound-and-light shows narrate nightly from Sphinx terraces.
- Solar-boat museum displays Khufu's disassembled cedar vessel.
- Robbers emptied burial chambers in antiquity despite seals.
- Urban sprawl now laps the desert plateau's edges.
- Conservation fights groundwater plus mass-tourism wear daily.
- New Grand Egyptian Museum nearby absorbs overflow crowds.
