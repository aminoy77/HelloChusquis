# Great islands (example)

- Greenland is the world's largest island that is not a continent.
- New Guinea is the second-largest island, split between Indonesia and PNG.
- Borneo is divided among Indonesia, Malaysia, and Brunei.
- Madagascar split from Africa ~160 million years ago, evolving unique wildlife.
- Honshu is Japan's largest island, holding Tokyo and Osaka.
- Great Britain and Ireland are Europe's two largest islands.
- Iceland sits on the Mid-Atlantic Ridge, splitting tectonic plates visibly.
- Cuba is the Caribbean's largest island by area.
- Sri Lanka is nicknamed the Pearl of the Indian Ocean.
- Manhattan is among the world's most densely populated islands.
