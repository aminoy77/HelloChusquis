# Yosemite (example)

- Yosemite's granite walls (El Capitan, Half Dome) define climbing history.
- Muir's campaigns made it a park in 1890 against loggers.
- Firefall (February Horsetail Fall) glows like lava at sunset briefly.
- Mist Trail climbs Vernal/Nevada Falls' spray-soaked stairs.
- Day-use reservations now ration summer valley entries.
- Half Dome cables require permits won by lottery months ahead.
- Ansel Adams's Clearing Winter Storm launched conservation photography.
- Tuolumne Meadows cools summer crowds at 2,600 meters altitude.
- Black bears raid careless food stores despite canister rules.
- Fall El Capitan shadows plus golden maples photograph best.
