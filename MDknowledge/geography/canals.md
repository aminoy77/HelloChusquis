# Famous canals (example)

- Suez (1869, 193 km) joins Mediterranean and Red Seas, cutting Africa rounding.
- Panama (1914, 82 km) lifts ships 26 meters via locks across isthmus.
- Kiel Canal saves North-Baltic voyages around Denmark profitably.
- Erie Canal (1825) made New York America's commercial capital.
- Grand Canal (China, 1,776 km) remains the longest artificial river.
- Corinth Canal slices Greece narrowly for yacht-scale traffic.
- White Sea-Baltic Canal cost gulag lives infamously under Stalin.
- Welland Canal bypasses Niagara for Great Lakes shipping.
- Manchester Ship Canal made an inland city a seaport.
- Panama expansion (2016) doubled capacity for Neo-Panamax ships.
