# Liechtenstein (example)

- Liechtenstein nestles 160 km² between Switzerland and Austria.
- Vaduz Castle (princely residence) overlooks the capital, closed inside.
- Low taxes plus foundations built a finance-hub economy.
- Prince Hans-Adam II retains real constitutional powers unusually.
- Rhine plain plus Alpine Oberland split the country geographically.
- Liechtenstein Trail (75 km) traverses the whole nation in days.
- Hilti power tools anchor Liechtenstein industry globally.
- Swiss franc circulates via customs union since 1924.
- Ski areas (Malbun) serve regional weekenders affordably.
- National Day (15 Aug) parties with prince's beer garden famously.
