# Micronesia (example)

- FSM's four states (Yap, Chuuk, Pohnpei, Kosrae) span 2,700 km ocean.
- Palikir (Pohnpei) serves as federal capital since 1989.
- Nan Madol's basalt canals earned 'Venice of the Pacific' fame.
- Chuuk Lagoon wrecks rank among the world's great dive sites.
- Compact funds underwrite budgets in exchange for US defense rights.
- Yap's stone money (rai) still transfers wealth traditionally.
- Pohnpei's mangroves plus waterfalls draw eco-tourists steadily.
- Outmigration (Guam, Hawaii, mainland) shrinks resident counts yearly.
- Tuna access fees dominate export-equivalent revenues.
- Colonial layers (Spanish, German, Japanese, US) stack visibly.
