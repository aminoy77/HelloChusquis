# Great Wall heritage (example)

- UNESCO listed the Wall in 1987 as cultural landscape.
- Ming brick sections draw crowds; wild Jiankou thrills hikers.
- Beacon towers relayed smoke signals along ridges historically.
- Sticky-rice mortar legends credit sweetness for durability.
- Wall marathon runners race its steps yearly in Tianjin section.
- Desert Jiayuguan fort ends the wall dramatically westward.
- Sea-end Shanhaiguan dips into Bohai waters poetically.
- Erosion plus brick theft erode unrestored stretches yearly.
- Mutianyu toboggans descend tourists playfully after climbs.
- Night tours illuminate Badaling sections seasonally.
