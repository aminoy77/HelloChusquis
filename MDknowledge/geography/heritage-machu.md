# Machu Picchu heritage (example)

- UNESCO mixed-listed Machu Picchu (1983) for nature plus culture.
- Inca Trail permits cap at 500 daily including porters.
- Huayna Picchu tickets sell out months ahead reliably.
- Cloud-forest orchids number 400+ species around ruins.
- Sun Gate arrivals reward four-day trekkers at dawn.
- Nearby Aguas Calientes lives off tourism almost entirely.
- Llama photobombs need no staging on terraces.
- New airport debates (Chinchero) split access versus impact.
- Circuit routes (1-5) now ration flows through sectors.
- Quechua porters' rights improved after exploitation exposés.
