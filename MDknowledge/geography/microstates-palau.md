# Palau (example)

- Palau's 340 islands scatter across western Pacific Micronesia.
- Ngerulmud capital (2006) replaced Koror administratively.
- Jellyfish Lake's stingless jellies let snorkelers float among millions.
- Rock Islands' mushroom islets earned UNESCO listing (2012).
- Palau pioneered shark sanctuaries (2009) inspiring global followers.
- Pristine Paradise pledge stamps passports with conservation oaths.
- Diving (Blue Corner) ranks world-class for sharks and currents.
- US Compact funds defense and aid in exchange for access.
- Japanese WWII wrecks dive below Peleliu's battlefields.
- Population (~18,000) fits in a small stadium entirely.
