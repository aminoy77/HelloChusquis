# Solomon Islands (example)

- Solomons count ~1,000 islands east of Papua New Guinea.
- Honiara (Guadalcanal) grew around WWII Henderson Field.
- Guadalcanal campaign (1942-43) turned Pacific momentum Allied-ward.
- Kennedy's PT-109 rescue by islanders made presidential lore.
- Diving (Munda, Gizo) plus surf breaks draw adventure travelers.
- RAMSI intervention (2003-17) stabilized ethnic-tension aftermath.
- Logging exports fund budgets while alarming conservationists.
- Pijin bridges 70+ indigenous languages daily.
- Malaita's shell money still bride-prices traditionally.
- Coral Triangle reefs rank among Earth's most biodiverse waters.
