# Golden Gate Bridge (example)

- Golden Gate span (1,280 m main) linked San Francisco to Marin in 1937.
- International Orange paint fights corrosion plus fog visibility artfully.
- Chief engineer Strauss battled ferry lobbies and Depression doubts.
- Safety nets saved 19 'Halfway to Hell Club' ironworkers famously.
- Suicide deterrent nets completed 2024 after decades of advocacy.
- Foghorns plus aircraft beacons guard the strait around the clock.
- Sidewalk crossings separate bikes and pedestrians by time/direction.
- 1987 50th-anniversary crowds flattened the deck's camber temporarily.
- Seismic retrofits continue protecting the span against Bay quakes.
- Battery viewpoints frame classic north-tower compositions.
