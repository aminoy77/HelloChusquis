# Andorra (example)

- Andorra's co-princes (French president, Spanish bishop) share the headship uniquely.
- Pyrenean valleys (468 km²) host ~80,000 residents plus 8M visitors.
- Duty-free shopping built modern prosperity after smuggling eras.
- Grandvalira ski domain ranks among southern Europe's largest.
- Caldea spa's glass spire dominates Escaldes-Engordany dramatically.
- Catalan is the sole official language of state business.
- Romanesque churches dot valleys along old pilgrim routes.
- Hiking (GR11) plus duty-free draw summer and winter crowds.
- Banking secrecy reforms (2018) aligned Andorra with transparency.
- Madriu Valley UNESCO listing protects glacial highlands.
