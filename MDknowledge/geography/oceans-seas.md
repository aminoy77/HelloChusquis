# Oceans and seas (example)

- The Pacific is the largest and deepest ocean, covering about a third of Earth's surface.
- The Atlantic separates the Americas from Europe and Africa.
- The Indian Ocean is the warmest major ocean on average.
- The Southern Ocean encircles Antarctica and was formally recognized in 2000.
- The Arctic Ocean is the smallest and shallowest ocean, largely ice-covered.
- The Mariana Trench holds the deepest known point (Challenger Deep, nearly 11,000 m).
- The Mediterranean connects to the Atlantic only through the Strait of Gibraltar.
- The Suez Canal links the Mediterranean to the Red Sea, joining Europe-Asia shipping.
- The Panama Canal joins the Atlantic and Pacific across Central America.
- The Bering Strait separates Russia from Alaska by under 100 km.
- The Coral Sea holds the Great Barrier Reef, the largest reef system on Earth.
- The Dead Sea's extreme salinity lets swimmers float effortlessly.
- The Caspian Sea is the world's largest enclosed inland water body.
- The Baltic Sea is brackish, mixing fresh river water with North Sea salt water.
- The Gulf Stream carries warm Caribbean water toward Western Europe, milding its climate.
