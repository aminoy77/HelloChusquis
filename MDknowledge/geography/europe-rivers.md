# European rivers (example)

- The Volga is Europe's longest river, flowing entirely within Russia.
- The Danube crosses ten countries, more than any other river.
- The Rhine carries Western Europe's busiest barge traffic.
- The Thames flows through London to the North Sea.
- The Seine flows through Paris to the English Channel.
- The Tiber flows through Rome to the Tyrrhenian Sea.
- The Danube empties into the Black Sea via its great delta.
- The Elbe links Prague and Hamburg to the North Sea.
- The Loire is France's longest river, famed for its chateaux valley.
- The Douro carves Portugal's port-wine valley to the Atlantic.
