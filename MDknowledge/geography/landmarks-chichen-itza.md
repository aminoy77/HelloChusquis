# Chichen Itza (example)

- El Castillo pyramid aligns serpent shadows at equinox sunsets.
- Great Ball Court's acoustics whisper across 135 meters eerily.
- Cenote Sagrado received offerings including human sacrifices.
- Toltec fusion styles distinguish Chichen from southern Maya sites.
- Observatory (El Caracol) tracked Venus cycles through slit windows.
- Stephens/Catherwood 1843 illustrations introduced ruins to the world.
- New Seven Wonders vote (2007) crowned it globally.
- Night sound-and-light shows narrate history dramatically.
- Vendors line paths selling jaguar whistles and hammocks.
- Equinox crowds now managed with timed entries and ropes.
