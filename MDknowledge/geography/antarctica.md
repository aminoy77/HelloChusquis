# Antarctica (example)

- Antarctica averages the coldest, driest, windiest continent on Earth.
- Vostok Station recorded -89.2°C, the coldest reliably measured temperature.
- Antarctic Treaty (1959) reserves the continent for peace and science.
- No permanent residents exist; ~4,000 summer researchers staff stations.
- Emperor penguins breed through winter darkness on sea ice.
- Ross and Filchner-Ronne ice shelves calve city-sized bergs periodically.
- Mount Erebus steams as an active volcano amid the ice.
- Dry Valleys mimic Mars so closely NASA tests rovers there.
- Ozone hole discovery (1985) over Antarctica drove the Montreal Protocol.
- Tourist landings concentrate on the milder Antarctic Peninsula.
- South Pole station (Amundsen-Scott) endures six-month nights.
- Krill swarms feed whales, seals, and penguins up the food web.
- Ice cores archive 800,000 years of climate history.
- Territorial claims are frozen (not resolved) under treaty terms.
- Greenland's ice is smaller; Antarctica holds ~70 percent of fresh water.
