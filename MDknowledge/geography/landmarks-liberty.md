# Statue of Liberty (example)

- France gifted the Statue of Liberty to the USA, dedicated 1886.
- Bartholdi sculpted it; Eiffel engineered its iron skeleton.
- Copper skin greened via natural patina within decades.
- Crown spikes evoke sun rays across seven seas and continents.
- Tablet reads July IV MDCCLXXVI (US independence date).
- Broken chains at her feet symbolize emancipation from tyranny.
- Ferries from Battery Park and New Jersey serve visitors daily.
- 1996 torch replica replaced the corroded original flame.
- Ellis Island museum neighbors it, pairing immigration stories.
- UNESCO listed the statue in 1984 for universal symbolism.
