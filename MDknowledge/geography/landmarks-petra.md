# Petra (example)

- Petra's Treasury Greets visitors through the narrow Siq canyon dramatically.
- Nabataeans carved facades into rose sandstone around 1st c. BC-AD.
- Water channels and cisterns sustained 20,000+ desert residents.
- Roman annexation (106 AD) rerouted trade, beginning decline.
- Earthquakes (363, 551 AD) plus shifts emptied the city gradually.
- Burckhardt's 1812 disguised visit 'rediscovered' Petra for Europe.
- Monastery (Ad-Deir) dwarfs the Treasury at 50 meters wide.
- Indiana Jones filmed Last Crusade scenes at the Treasury in 1989.
- Bedouin guides still lead most Treasury viewpoints traditionally.
- Back-trail viewpoints (above Treasury) reward early hikers best.
