# Tower Bridge (example)

- Tower Bridge (1894) lifts bascules for Thames shipping ~800 times yearly.
- Gothic cladding disguises its steel skeleton Victorian-fashionably.
- High-level walkways (glass floors since 2014) thrill vertigo-prone visitors.
- Steam engines originally pumped hydraulics; electric motors replaced them.
- Bus jump legend (1952) saw a double-decker clear a rising gap.
- Paint jobs (25,000 liters) refresh the light-blue/white scheme quinquennially.
- St Katharine crowds photograph it mistaking it for London Bridge often.
- Engine rooms museum demonstrates original coal-fired machinery.
- Lift times publish so river traffic plans around road closures.
- Night illumination outlines twin towers postcard-perfectly.
