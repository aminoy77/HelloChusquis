# Moscow Kremlin (example)

- Kremlin walls (2.2 km, 20 towers) fortify Moscow's historic core.
- Red Square's St. Basil's swirls date to Ivan the Terrible's Kazan victory.
- Grand Kremlin Palace hosts presidential ceremonies today.
- Armoury museum hoards Faberge eggs and royal carriages.
- Tsar Cannon (1586, never fired) and Tsar Bell symbolize scale ambition.
- Lenin's mausoleum still queues visitors on Red Square.
- Spasskaya clock chimes Russia's New Year nationally.
- Italian architects (Fioravanti) raised early cathedrals inside.
- Diamond Fund displays imperial crowns behind glass.
- Night illumination outlines towers dramatically for postcards.
