# Tokyo Skytree (example)

- Tokyo Skytree (634 m) is the world's tallest tower structure.
- Completed 2012, it broadcasts digital TV across Kanto plain.
- Height digits (6-3-4) spell 'Mu-sa-shi,' old provincial name.
- Central column (shinbashira) damps quakes like pagoda pillars.
- Two decks (350 m, 450 m) plus glass floors thrill visitors.
- Night lighting alternates Iki (blue) and Miyabi (purple-gold) palettes.
- The tower replaced Tokyo Tower's insufficient digital height.
- Asakusa crowds pair Skytree views with temple visits traditionally.
- Construction survived the 2011 Tohoku quake undamaged mid-build.
- Sumida aquarium and planetarium fill its base complex.
