# Grand Canyon (example)

- The canyon runs 446 km long, up to 29 km wide, 1.8 km deep in Arizona.
- Colorado River downcutting plus uplift carved the mile-deep chasm.
- Strata span ~2 billion years: Vishnu schist basement to Kaibab rim.
- South Rim hosts most visitors; North Rim closes in winter.
- Bright Angel and Kaibab trails descend corridors used for millennia.
- Phantom Ranch lodges hikers at the river since 1922.
- Condors reintroduced in the 1990s soar the canyon again.
- Flash floods reshape side canyons violently after desert storms.
- Dark-sky status preserves stellar views over the gorge.
- Grand Canyon became a national park in 1919 after Roosevelt's push.
- Havasu Falls' turquoise travertine draws permit lotteries.
- River runners brave Lava Falls, the canyon's fiercest rapid.
- Desert bighorn navigate cliffs modern hikers rope through.
- Ancestral Puebloans granaried side-canyon alcoves centuries ago.
- Overflights are restricted to protect natural quiet zones.
