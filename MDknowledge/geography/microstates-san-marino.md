# San Marino (example)

- San Marino claims world's oldest republic (301 AD, Saint Marinus).
- Mount Titano's three towers guard the clifftop capital.
- Italian enclave (61 km²) uses the euro via monetary agreement.
- Crossbow festivals plus medieval days animate summers traditionally.
- Tourism and stamps fund the microstate substantially.
- Liberty Square's guard changes draw midday crowds.
- Wine (Sangiovese) plus olive oil carry local gastronomy.
- UNESCO listed the historic center and Titano in 2008.
- San Marino's football team famously loses with beloved persistence.
- Day trips from Rimini cover highlights in hours easily.
