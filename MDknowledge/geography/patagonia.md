# Patagonia (example)

- Patagonia spans Argentina/Chile's southern cone to Cape Horn.
- Fitz Roy and Torres del Paine spires draw trekkers globally.
- Perito Moreno glacier advances while most worldwide retreat.
- Guanacos, rheas, and pumas animate steppe wildlife viewing.
- Welsh settlers (1865) still tea-culture Chubut towns today.
- Estancia sheep stations partition grasslands enormously.
- Winds (100+ km/h) prune trees sideways (flag trees).
- Carretera Austral threads Chilean fjords spectacularly unfinished.
- Whale watching (Peninsula Valdes) peaks September-December.
- End-of-world tourism brands Ushuaia and Cape Horn mystique.
