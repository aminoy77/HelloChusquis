# Forbidden City (example)

- Beijing's Forbidden City housed 24 Ming/Qing emperors (1420-1912).
- 980 buildings with 8,886 rooms fill the walled complex.
- Yellow glazed roofs signal imperial rank exclusively.
- Meridian Gate (south) staged proclamations and punishments.
- Hall of Supreme Harmony hosted coronations and weddings.
- Moats plus 10-meter walls sealed commoners out for centuries.
- Puyi's 1924 expulsion ended palace imperial use finally.
- Palace Museum (1925) now draws 19M+ visitors yearly.
- Qianlong's retirement quarters pack exquisite craftsmanship densely.
- South-north axis aligns cosmology with imperial power spatially.
