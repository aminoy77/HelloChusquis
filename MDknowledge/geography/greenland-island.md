# Greenland (example)

- Greenland is the world's largest island that is not a continent.
- An ice sheet up to 3 km thick covers about 80 percent of Greenland.
- Nuuk is the capital and largest town of Greenland.
- Greenland is an autonomous Danish territory with self-rule since 2009.
- Inuit (Kalaallit) form the majority of Greenland's 56,000 residents.
- Ilulissat Icefjord calves bergs from the fast Jakobshavn glacier.
- Midnight sun summers contrast with polar-night winters.
- Dog sleds and snowmobiles share winter transport duties.
- Cod and shrimp fisheries underpin the island economy.
- Rare-earth deposits draw mining interest amid independence debates.
- Norse Eastern Settlement vanished mysteriously by the 1400s.
- Thule Air Base (Pituffik) anchors US Arctic presence.
- Greenlandic (Kalaallisut) is the sole official language.
- Climate melt opens shipping lanes and exposes resources simultaneously.
- Arctic Winter Games celebrate circumpolar Indigenous sports.
