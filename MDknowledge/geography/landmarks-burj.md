# Burj Khalifa (example)

- Burj Khalifa (828 m, 163 floors) is the world's tallest building.
- Dubai's tower topped out in 2009 and opened in 2010.
- Y-shaped plan buttresses the spire against desert winds.
- Observation decks (124/125/148) sell timed sunset slots.
- Condensation collection waters surrounding landscaping sustainably.
- Elevators climb 10 m/s among the world's fastest lifts.
- LED facade doubles as record-breaking display surface.
- Armani Hotel occupies lower floors in minimalist luxury.
- Fountain shows (Dubai Mall lake) choreograph water to music nightly.
- Foundation piles drill 50 meters into weak desert soils.
