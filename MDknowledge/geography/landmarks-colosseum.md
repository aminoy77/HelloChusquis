# Colosseum (example)

- Vespasian began the Colosseum (~70 AD); Titus opened it in 80 AD.
- 50,000-80,000 spectators filled Rome's amphitheater free.
- Gladiatorial bouts plus beast hunts ran for ~400 years.
- Hypogeum tunnels staged animals and scenery via lifts.
- Earthquakes toppled the south outer ring, quarried for churches.
- Christian martyrdom associations are later tradition, debated by scholars.
- Arena floor reconstructions cover a fraction for modern shows.
- Night tours reveal moonlit vaults with smaller crowds.
- Travertine, tuff, and brick-faced concrete layer its structure.
- Ticket bundles pair it with Forum and Palatine efficiently.
