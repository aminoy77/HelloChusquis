# Cooking chicken (example)

- Chicken is safe at 74°C/165°F internal; rest 3+ minutes for juices.
- Pat skin bone-dry plus salt for shattering roast crispness.
- Spatchcocking (removing backbone) flattens birds for even fast roasting.
- Brines (wet or dry) buy juiciness insurance for lean breasts.
- Sear thighs skin-down first, rendering fat before flipping once.
- Rest all cuts 5-10 minutes; slicing early spills juices onto boards.
- Thermometers beat timers and color for doneness certainty.
- Marinades flavor surfaces; only brines penetrate meaningfully deep.
- Poach gently (never boil) for tender shreddable breast meat.
- Save carcasses for stock: simmer with aromatics 4+ hours.
