# BBQ and smoking (example)

- Low-and-slow (110-135°C) renders collagen to gelatin over hours.
- Smoke rings form from nitric oxide, not flavor penetration depth.
- Oak/hickory suit beef; fruitwoods suit pork and poultry gently.
- Texas crutch (foil wrap) powers through stalls faster juicily.
- Rest briskets hours in coolers for redistribute-and-tenderize magic.
- Thermometers (not clocks) decide brisket/pork-butt doneness (90-96°C).
- Thin blue smoke flavors; white billows bitter creosote.
- Salt-and-pepper (Dalmatian rub) lets beef speak in Texas style.
- Offsets flow heat/smoke past meat indirectly and evenly.
- Rest, slice against grain, serve immediately for peak juiciness.
