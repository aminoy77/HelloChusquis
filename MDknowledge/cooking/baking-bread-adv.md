# Advanced bread (example)

- Autolyse (flour+water rest) develops gluten before kneading gently.
- Stretch-and-fold replaces kneading for wet doughs elegantly.
- Cold retardation (overnight fridge) deepens flavor plus scheduling.
- Dutch ovens trap steam replicating professional deck ovens.
- Scoring controls bloom direction decoratively and functionally.
- Hydration percentages (65-80%) tune openness versus handling ease.
- Levain maturity (float test) predicts rise reliability simply.
- Whole grains thirst extra water plus patience versus white flour.
- Enriched doughs (brioche) slow ferment from fat/sugar loads.
- Staling retrogrades starch; refresh slices via toasting or freezing.
