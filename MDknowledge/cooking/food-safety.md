# Food safety (example)

- The danger zone (4-60°C) grows bacteria fast; limit exposure to two hours.
- Refrigerate below 4°C and freeze below -18°C to stall microbial growth.
- Thaw in fridges, cold water (changed often), or microwaves — never countertops.
- Separate raw meats from ready-to-eat foods at every stage.
- Wash produce, not raw chicken (splashing spreads bacteria sink-wide).
- Leftovers reheat once to steaming (74°C) throughout, not just edges.
- Color never proves doneness; thermometers do, every time.
- Marinades that touched raw meat need boiling before reuse as sauce.
- FIFO (first in, first out) rotation prevents forgotten-back-of-fridge science.
- When in doubt, throw it out — food poisoning dwarfs grocery savings.
