# Knife skills (example)

- Claw-grip fingertips curl under, guiding blades against knuckles safely.
- Sharp knives cut predictably; dull ones slip dangerously off food.
- Rock-chop herbs, push-cut vegetables, slice proteins in single strokes.
- Hone steels realign edges; whetstones actually remove steel to resharpen.
- Cut stable bases first (halve round produce) before detailed work.
- Let the knife's weight do the work; pressing crushes cell structure.
- Separate tasks by board (raw meat versus ready-to-eat) against cross-contamination.
- Dry handles and hands grip reliably; wet ones invite slips.
- Store edged in sheaths or on magnetic strips, never loose in drawers.
- Match knife to task: chef's for bulk, paring for detail, serrated for crusts.
