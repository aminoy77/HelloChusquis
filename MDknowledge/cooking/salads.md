# Salads (example)

- Dry greens thoroughly; water repels dressing into puddles.
- Dress lightly, toss, taste, then add more — overdressing is irreversible.
- Warm plus cool plus crunchy plus creamy composes satisfying bowls.
- Salt tomatoes and cucumbers early to draw flavored juices into dressings.
- Massage kale with oil and salt to tenderize tough leaves.
- Add delicate herbs and cheese after tossing to keep them visible.
- Grains and beans bulk salads into full meals cheaply.
- Vinaigrette ratio 3:1 oil-to-acid adapts to any flavor profile.
- Serve cheese at room temperature for full aroma impact.
- Compose ahead undressed; dress crunchy salads at the table.
