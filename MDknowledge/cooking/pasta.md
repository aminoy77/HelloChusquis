# Cooking pasta (example)

- Salt pasta water until it tastes like the sea for seasoned noodles.
- Use abundant boiling water so temperature rebounds fast after adding.
- Bronze-die pasta grips sauce via rough micro-texture.
- Reserve starchy pasta water to emulsify glossy pan sauces.
- Finish pasta one minute shy in the sauce itself (mantecatura).
- Fresh egg pasta cooks in 2-4 minutes versus 8-12 for dried.
- Oil in boiling water does nothing except make sauce slide off.
- Match shapes to sauces: tubes catch chunky, strands suit slick coatings.
- Taste-test doneness; package times assume specific altitudes and pots.
- Baked pasta needs extra sauce since noodles drink liquid in ovens.
