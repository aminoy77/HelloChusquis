# Bread basics (example)

- Flour, water, salt, yeast — great bread needs nothing more.
- Kneading develops gluten networks trapping fermentation gases.
- Bulk ferment until roughly doubled, judging dough not clocks.
- Shape tightly for oven spring; loose loaves spread sideways.
- Steam first 10 minutes keeps crusts expandable before setting.
- Internal 93-99°C signals doneness better than hollow thumps alone.
- Cool fully on racks; slicing hot gums crumb structure.
- Sourdough starters need regular flour-water feedings like pets.
- Salt strengthens gluten but retards yeast — balance both deliberately.
- Stale bread revives via water sprinkle plus hot oven minutes.
