# Soups (example)

- Sweat aromatics (onion, carrot, celery) before adding liquid for depth.
- Brown meats first; fond deglazed with wine or stock builds body.
- Simmer gently; hard boils cloud broths and toughen proteins.
- Skim scum early for clear consommés and clean flavors.
- Blend in batches (or immersion-blend in pot) for safe smooth soups.
- Season in layers, finishing salt after reduction concentrates flavors.
- Acid and fresh herbs go last to keep brightness alive.
- Grains/pasta cook separately when meal-prepping to avoid mush.
- Chill stocks fast (ice bath) through the danger zone for safety.
- Freeze flat in bags for stackable portion-ready soup libraries.
