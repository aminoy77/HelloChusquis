# Spices (example)

- Whole spices keep years; ground lose punch within months.
- Toast whole spices dry until fragrant, then grind fresh for maximum aroma.
- Bloom ground spices in hot fat to unlock fat-soluble flavors.
- Add delicate herbs (basil, cilantro) off heat to preserve brightness.
- Chili heat lives in membranes and seeds; remove for milder dishes.
- Salt in layers during cooking, not just at the end, for depth.
- Taste spice blends (garam masala, ras el hanout) before committing pots.
- Store away from heat, light, and steam (never above the stove).
- Smoked paprika adds fireless depth to vegetarian dishes.
- MSG boosts savoriness safely despite its mythologized reputation.
