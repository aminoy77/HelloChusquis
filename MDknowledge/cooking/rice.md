# Cooking rice (example)

- Rinse rice until water runs clear to remove excess surface starch.
- Absorption ratio ~1:1.2 rice-to-water for long grain on stovetops.
- Rest covered 10 minutes off heat; steam finishes grains evenly.
- Pilaf toasts grains in fat before liquid for separate fluffy kernels.
- Risotto adds warm stock ladle by ladle, stirring creamy starch free.
- Brown rice needs ~45 minutes and extra water versus white.
- Day-old refrigerated rice fries best without clumping or gumminess.
- Salt the cooking water; unsalted rice tastes flat whatever the sauce.
- Rice cookers switch to warm automatically via thermal sensors.
- Freeze portions flat in bags; microwave with a water sprinkle revives them.
