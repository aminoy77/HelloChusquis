# Cooking fish (example)

- Fish cooks fast: ~10 minutes per inch of thickness at 230°C.
- Opaque, flaky flesh signals doneness; translucent centers need more time.
- Sear skin-side down 90 percent of the time for crisp crackling skin.
- Pat fillets dry; moisture is crispness's mortal enemy.
- Salt just before cooking; early salting draws water to surfaces.
- Acid (lemon) firms proteins like heat — ceviche 'cooks' without fire.
- Whole fish stay juicier than fillets via bone insulation.
- Frozen-at-sea fish often beats 'fresh' days-old counter fish.
- Oil the fish, not the grates, to prevent sticking.
- Smell fresh ocean, not fishiness, when buying whole fish.
