# Advanced cakes (example)

- Reverse creaming (flour+butter first) yields velvety tender crumbs.
- Swiss meringue buttercream heats whites/sugar before whipping silkily.
- Syrup soaks rescue layers while adding complementary flavors.
- Torting (splitting layers) multiplies filling strata impressively.
- Crumb coats trap strays before final frosting passes.
- Dowel rods support tiered weights structurally, not decoratively.
- Gelatin stabilizes mousses surviving warm venues reliably.
- Mirror glazes pour at precise temperatures for glass finishes.
- Oven strips (wet towels) level domes for stackable layers.
- Freeze-then-carve sculpts shaped cakes without crumb avalanches.
