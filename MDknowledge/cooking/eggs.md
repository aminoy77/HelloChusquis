# Cooking eggs (example)

- Boiled eggs: 6.5 minutes jammy yolks, 9+ minutes fully set, ice-bath after.
- Scrambled low-and-slow with butter stays creamy; high heat turns rubbery.
- Fried sunny-side keeps runny yolks; baste with hot fat to set whites.
- Omelets set edges then fold fillings before centers fully firm.
- Poach in barely simmering vinegared water swirled into a vortex.
- Room-temperature eggs whip higher for meringues and souffles.
- Fresh eggs poach neatly; older eggs peel cleanly when boiled.
- Salt scrambles early for tenderness; pepper at the table to avoid gray.
- Carryover heat finishes eggs off-burner; pull them slightly early.
- One large egg binds, leavens, and enriches batters simultaneously.
