# Sushi basics (example)

- Seasoned rice (su-meshi) defines sushi more than fish does.
- Short-grain rice plus rice-vinegar/sugar/salt seasons correctly.
- Fan rice while cutting (kiru) for gloss without mashing grains.
- Nigiri shapes two-bite ovals pressed, never squeezed.
- Sashimi-grade means handled-cold-chain trust, not official grades.
- Wasabi goes between fish and rice traditionally, not dissolved in soy.
- Eat nigiri fish-side-down on the tongue for full flavor.
- Pickled ginger resets palates between pieces properly.
- Omakase trusts chefs' sequences; note dislikes upfront politely.
- Knives (yanagiba single-bevel) slice in single drawing pulls.
