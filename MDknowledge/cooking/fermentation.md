# Fermentation (example)

- Lacto-fermentation (2% salt brine) pickles vegetables safely at room temp.
- Kahm yeast (harmless white film) skims off; fuzzy mold means discard.
- Kimchi burps daily while active to vent CO₂ pressure safely.
- Sourdough starters balance wild yeast plus lactobacilli symbiotically.
- Miso/soy ferment months-to-years deepening umami complexity.
- Kombucha SCOBYs brew sweet tea into tangy probiotic drinks.
- Temperatures (18-22°C) ferment steadily; heat speeds unpredictably.
- Salt ratios decide safety: weigh ingredients, never eyeball.
- Airlocks vent gases while blocking oxygen-mold pathways.
- Taste timelines (days to months) beat calendar dogma always.
