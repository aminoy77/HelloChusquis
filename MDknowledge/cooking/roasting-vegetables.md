# Roasting vegetables (example)

- High heat (220°C+) caramelizes edges while keeping centers tender.
- Cut uniform pieces so everything finishes simultaneously.
- Crowding steams instead of roasting; use two pans when in doubt.
- Oil coats lightly; excess pools and fries bottoms soggily.
- Hard roots (carrots, squash) start earlier than soft ones (peppers, zucchini).
- Preheat pans for instant sizzle and better browning contact.
- Season after oiling so salt and spices adhere evenly.
- Flip once mid-roast; constant turning cools pans and stalls color.
- Finish with acid (vinegar, lemon) to brighten caramelized sugars.
- Broil final minutes for char spots without overcooking interiors.
