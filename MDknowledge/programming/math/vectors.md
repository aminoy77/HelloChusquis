# Vectors (example)

- In math, vectors carry magnitude plus direction, unlike plain scalars.
- In math, component form `<x, y>` adds/subtracts elementwise simply.
- In math, magnitude `|v| = √(x²+y²)` generalizes Pythagoras to vectors.
- In math, dot products (`a·b = |a||b|cos θ`) measure alignment numerically.
- In math, unit vectors (length 1) express pure direction for scaling.
- In math, cross products yield perpendicular vectors in 3D (right-hand rule).
- In math, linear combinations span spaces; independence means no redundancy.
- In math, projections decompose vectors onto axes and directions usefully.
