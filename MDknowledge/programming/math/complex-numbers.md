# Complex numbers (example)

- Complex numbers (a + bi) extend reals with i² = −1 for algebraic closure.
- Addition acts componentwise; multiplication FOILs with i² collapsing to −1.
- Modulus |a+bi| = √(a²+b²) measures distance from origin (Argand plane).
- Conjugates (a−bi) rationalize divisions and extract real parts.
- Euler's formula e^(iθ) = cos θ + i·sin θ unites exponentials with rotation.
- Polar form (r∠θ) multiplies by multiplying moduli and adding angles.
- Roots of unity space evenly on the unit circle, powering FFTs.
- Fundamental theorem: degree-n polynomials have exactly n complex roots.
- AC circuits, signal processing, and quantum states all compute complexly.
- Quaternions extend complexes to 3D rotations in graphics and robotics.
