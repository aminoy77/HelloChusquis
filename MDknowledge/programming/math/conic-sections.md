# Conic sections (example)

- In math, slicing cones yields circles, ellipses, parabolas, hyperbolas.
- In math, circles center (h,k) radius r obey `(x-h)²+(y-k)² = r²`.
- In math, parabolas focus parallel rays (satellite dishes, headlights).
- In math, ellipses sum focal distances constantly (planetary orbits).
- In math, hyperbolas difference focal distances constantly (navigation LORAN).
- In math, eccentricity classifies: 0 circle, <1 ellipse, 1 parabola, >1 hyperbola.
- In math, completing squares reveals centers and radii from general form.
- In math, degenerate cases (points, lines) appear at critical angles.
