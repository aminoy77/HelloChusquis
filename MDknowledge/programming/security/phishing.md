# Phishing defense

- Phishing steals credentials via spoofed senders and urgent pretexts.
- Verify senders via headers and out-of-band channels, not displayed names.
- Hover links to inspect real destinations before clicking anything sensitive.
- Attachments (macros, HTML smuggling) deliver most commodity malware.
- Report buttons feed SOC pipelines faster than deletions alone.
- DMARC/SPF/DKIM let receivers reject spoofed organizational mail.
- Passkeys resist phishing where passwords and SMS codes fall.
- Simulated campaigns train recognition without shaming clickers.
- QR-code phishing (quishing) bypasses link inspection — preview URLs first.
- Incident playbooks isolate, reset, and review within hours of reports.
