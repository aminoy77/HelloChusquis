# Wi-Fi essentials

- Wi-Fi bands trade speed for range: 6 GHz fastest/shortest, 2.4 GHz farthest/slowest.
- Channels overlap on 2.4 GHz (use 1, 6, 11); 5/6 GHz offer clean wide channels.
- WPA3 secures modern networks; WEP/WPA are crackable and banned in practice.
- SSIDs hide nothing; hidden networks still beacon to scanners.
- Roaming (802.11r/k/v) hands clients between APs without drops.
- Client density, not just signal bars, decides real-world throughput.
- Place APs high, central, and clear of microwaves/mirrors/water.
- Separate IoT/guest VLANs contain compromised cameras and visitors.
- Mesh kits trade half the backhaul for coverage without cables.
- Site-survey heatmaps beat guessing for office and venue layouts.
