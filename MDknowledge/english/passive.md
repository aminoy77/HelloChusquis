# English passive voice (example)

- Passive forms with be plus past participle: the report was finished.
- Active voice suits most prose; passive suits unknown or irrelevant agents.
- By-phrases name agents optionally: discovered by Curie in 1898.
- Stative passives describe states (the door is closed), not actions.
- Get-passives (got fired) sound informal beside be-passives.
- - Intransitive verbs (arrive, sleep) cannot form passives at all.
- Double passives (was attempted to be done) tangle; rewrite actively.
- Scientific methods favor passive to foreground procedure over researcher.
- Headlines drop be-verbs (MPsExpelled) for space, unlike full sentences.
- Check agentless passives for hidden responsibility gaps in reports.
