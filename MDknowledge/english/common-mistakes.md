# Commonly confused English words (example)

- Affect is usually the verb (to influence); effect is usually the noun (the result).
- Their shows possession, there marks a place, and they're contracts they are.
- Your shows possession; you're contracts you are.
- Its shows possession; it's contracts it is or it has.
- Than compares (taller than); then sequences events (first this, then that).
- To is a preposition, too means also or excessively, and two is the number.
- Principal as a noun is a person or sum of money; principle is a rule or belief.
- Compliment praises; complement completes or pairs well with something.
- Stationary means still; stationery means writing paper.
- Discreet means tactful; discrete means separate or distinct.
- Ensure means guarantee an outcome; insure relates to insurance policies.
- Fewer fits countable items; less fits uncountable amounts.
- Farther measures physical distance; further covers figurative extent.
- Imply is what speakers do; infer is what listeners do.
- Continual allows interruptions; continuous means without any break.
