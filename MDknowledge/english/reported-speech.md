# Reported speech (example)

- Direct 'I am tired' becomes reported 'she was tired' with backshift.
- Present simple backshifts to past simple in standard reported speech.
- Will becomes would and can becomes could under backshifting.
- Say reports statements; tell needs an object (told me, not told).
- Ask reports questions with if/whether and uninverted word order.
- Time words shift: today becomes that day, tomorrow becomes the next day.
- Universal truths may keep present tense (water boils at 100°C).
- Reporting verbs (claim, admit, deny) color the reported stance.
- Imperatives report as told/asked someone to do something.
- Questions keep question marks out: she asked where I lived.
