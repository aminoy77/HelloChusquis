# English linking words (example)

- Addition (moreover, furthermore, besides) stacks supporting points.
- Contrast (however, nevertheless, whereas) pivots arguments explicitly.
- Cause (since, as, due to) plus effect (therefore, consequently) chain logic.
- Sequence (firstly, subsequently, finally) orders processes for readers.
- Exemplification (for instance, namely) grounds abstractions concretely.
- Concession (although, despite) acknowledges counters before rebutting.
- Formal essays prefer however-initial over but-initial sentences.
- Overused linkers (very, so, and then) flag immature style to examiners.
- Paragraph-initial position maximizes linking visibility structurally.
- Variety across paragraphs avoids monotonous moreover-chains.
