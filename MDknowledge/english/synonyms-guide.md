# English synonyms (example)

- Synonyms differ in register (slim/slender/skinny) more than meaning.
- Big/large/great overlap partially across abstract versus physical uses.
- Happy/glad/pleased/delighted scale intensity and formality distinctly.
- Thesauruses inspire but context decides (strong tea, powerful engine — never swapped).
- Connotation (cheap vs economical) steers persuasion subtly.
- Collocation constrains choice (rancid butter, not rotten butter).
- Over-synonymizing (elegant variation) confuses more than it beautifies.
- Domain terms (bug/defect/issue) carry team-specific pragmatics.
- Gradable adjectives (very big) versus absolute (unique) resist intensifiers differently.
- Read widely; synonym lists without contexts mislead reliably.
