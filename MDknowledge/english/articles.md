# English articles (example)

- A/an introduces new singular nouns: a cat crossed; the cat returned.
- The marks shared or unique referents: the sun, the meeting we scheduled.
- Zero article covers plurals and uncountables generally: cats sleep; water flows.
- First mention takes a/an, later mentions take the (a dog ... the dog).
- Superlatives and ordinals take the: the best, the first attempt.
- Proper names usually take no article, but rivers and ranges do (the Nile, the Alps).
- An precedes vowel sounds (an hour, an MBA), not just vowel letters.
- Abstract nouns take no article generally but the when specified (life vs the life of...).
- Meals, sports, and languages drop articles: breakfast, tennis, French.
- Job titles after be take a/an: she is an engineer.
