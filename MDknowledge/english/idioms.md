# Everyday English idioms (example)

- Break the ice means easing tension to start conversations.
- Hit the books means studying hard, especially before exams.
- Under the weather means feeling slightly ill.
- Cost an arm and a leg means being very expensive.
- Piece of cake means something very easy to do.
- Once in a blue moon means happening very rarely.
- Spill the beans means revealing a secret accidentally.
- On the ball means alert and performing well.
- Burn the midnight oil means working late into the night.
- Back to square one means restarting after a failed attempt.
