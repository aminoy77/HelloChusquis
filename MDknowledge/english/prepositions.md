# English prepositions (example)

- At marks points (at 5pm, at home), on marks surfaces and days, in marks volumes and months.
- Arrive at buildings but arrive in cities and countries.
- Depend on, listen to, and wait for pair verbs with fixed prepositions.
- By marks deadlines (by Friday) and means (by email); until marks duration endpoints.
- Between divides two parties; among distributes across three or more.
- Into implies motion inward; in states static location.
- Out of centers on origins and materials (out of stock, out of wood).
- Despite takes nouns directly; although needs a full clause.
- According to attributes claims without endorsing them.
- Prepositions end sentences acceptably in modern English despite old prohibitions.
