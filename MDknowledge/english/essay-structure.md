# Essay structure (example)

- Introductions hook, contextualize, then state the thesis last.
- Thesis statements take one arguable position the essay will prove.
- Topic sentences open body paragraphs with their single controlling idea.
- PEEL paragraphs develop Point, Evidence, Explanation, Link in order.
- Transitions (however, moreover, consequently) signpost logical turns.
- Conclusions restate the thesis freshly and widen to implications.
- Quotes need introduction, citation, and analysis — never dropped bare.
- Outlines (thesis plus three reasons) precede drafting for coherence.
- Revision passes target argument, then paragraphs, then sentences, then words.
- Proofread backwards (last sentence first) to catch surface errors afresh.
