# English collocations (example)

- Collocations pair words habitually (heavy rain, not strong rain).
- Make decisions/photos/mistakes but do homework/business/favors.
- Take risks/turns/naps; have breakfast/a break/a look respectively.
- Pay attention/compliments/visits — never 'do attention'.
- Adjective-noun bonds (strong coffee, high hopes) resist synonyms.
- Verb-adverb pairs (deeply regret, strongly oppose) sound native.
- Dictionaries (Oxford Collocations) list partners per headword usefully.
- Reading volume builds collocational intuition faster than lists.
- Errors ('do a mistake') mark learners instantly to natives.
- Teach chunks, not isolated words, for fluent production.
