# English word roots (example)

- Greek 'graph' (write) roots biography, geography, and photography.
- Latin 'port' (carry) roots import, transport, and portable.
- 'Dict' (say) roots predict, dictator, and dictionary.
- 'Spect' (look) roots inspect, respect, and spectacular.
- ' bene' (good) roots benefit, benevolent, and benediction.
- 'Mal' (bad) roots malfunction, malice, and malaria (bad air).
- 'Chron' (time) roots chronic, chronology, and synchronize.
- 'Bio' (life) roots biology, biography, and antibiotic.
- 'Tele' (far) roots telephone, television, and teleport.
- 'Auto' (self) roots automatic, autobiography, and autonomous.
