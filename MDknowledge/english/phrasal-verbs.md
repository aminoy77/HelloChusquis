# English phrasal verbs (example)

- Phrasal verbs fuse verbs plus particles into idiomatic meanings.
- Look after (care), look into (investigate), look up to (admire) diverge widely.
- Separable verbs split around objects (turn it off, never turn off it with pronouns).
- Inseparable verbs (run into = meet) never split around objects.
- Three-part verbs (put up with, get away with) layer particles doubly.
- Formal writing prefers Latinate single verbs (tolerate vs put up with).
- Context disambiguates polysemous sets (take off: planes, clothes, careers).
- Stress particles in speech (TURN off) for native rhythm.
- Test makers love phrasal verbs for B2/C1 listening traps.
- Learn in topic clusters (travel: set off, check in, take off) efficiently.
