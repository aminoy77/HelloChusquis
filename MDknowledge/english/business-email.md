# Business email (example)

- Subject lines state purpose plus deadline (Q3 report — approval by Friday).
- Open with context in one line before any request appears.
- One email carries one ask; multiple asks get numbered explicitly.
- Close with the exact next step, owner, and date for accountability.
- CC sparingly: every recipient should know why they are included.
- Reply-all defaults off in most cultures and inboxes.
- Attachments get named meaningfully and mentioned in the body text.
- Response-time expectations (24h) beat instant replies for deep work.
- Mobile-length paragraphs (2-3 lines) survive phone reading.
- Signatures carry role, phone, and timezone for global teams.
