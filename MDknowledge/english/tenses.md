# English tenses (example)

- Simple present expresses habits and facts: she works downtown.
- Present continuous expresses ongoing action: they are working now.
- Simple past expresses finished events: we worked yesterday.
- Present perfect links past to present: I have worked here for years.
- Past perfect orders two pasts: she had left before I arrived.
- Future simple uses will for promises and spontaneous decisions.
- Going-to future expresses prior plans: we are going to launch Friday.
- Future perfect (will have done) looks back from a future point.
- Past continuous sets background scenes interrupted by simple-past events.
- Sequence of tenses keeps reported speech consistent with the reporting verb.
