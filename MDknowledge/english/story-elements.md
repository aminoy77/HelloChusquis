# Story elements (example)

- Protagonists want something; plots are obstacles to that want.
- Inciting incidents disrupt normality and launch the central question.
- Rising action complicates through reversals and revelations.
- Climaxes force irreversible choices at maximum stakes.
- Resolutions show the new normal after consequences land.
- Show-don't-tell renders emotion through action and sense detail.
- Dialogue advances plot or character; small talk without purpose drags.
- Point of view (first/third, limited/omniscient) bounds what readers can know.
- Foreshadowing plants payoffs early so twists feel earned, not random.
- Theme emerges from choices and costs, never from moralizing speeches.
