# Formal vs informal English (example)

- Formal writing avoids contractions (do not vs don't) in reports and essays.
- Informal requests use 'Can you...?'; formal ones use 'Could you...?' or 'Would you mind...?'.
- Slang (gonna, stuff) suits friends, never applications or complaints.
- Formal closings (Yours faithfully) fit unknown recipients; Cheers fits colleagues.
- Passive constructions sound formal where actives sound direct and plain.
- Latinate vocabulary (commence, purchase) reads formal beside Germanic (start, buy).
- Emails to strangers open with titles plus surnames until invited otherwise.
- Academic hedging (suggests, tends to) replaces casual certainty appropriately.
- Texting abbreviations stay out of every professional channel.
- Match register to audience first; grammar policing comes second.
