# Sequences and series

- In math, arithmetic sequences add a fixed difference; the nth term is `a + (n-1)d`.
- In math, geometric sequences multiply by a ratio; nth term is `a·r^(n-1)`.
- In math, arithmetic series sum to `n/2 · (first + last)` pairing symmetric terms.
- In math, finite geometric series sum to `a(1-r^n)/(1-r)` for ratio r ≠ 1.
- In math, infinite geometric series converge to `a/(1-r)` only when |r| < 1.
- In math, the Fibonacci sequence adds prior pairs: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, ...
- In math, triangular numbers `n(n+1)/2` count handshake pairs and stacked dots.
- In math, factorials `n!` count orderings and grow faster than exponentials.
- In math, the harmonic series diverges despite shrinking terms — terms must shrink fast enough.
- In math, convergence tests (ratio, comparison) decide infinite sums without computing them.
