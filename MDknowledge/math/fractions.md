# Fractions essentials

- In math, add fractions via common denominators: `a/b + c/d = (ad + bc)/bd`.
- In math, multiplying fractions multiplies straight across: numerators times numerators.
- In math, dividing fractions multiplies by the reciprocal: `a/b ÷ c/d = a/b · d/c`.
- In math, simplify with the GCD of numerator and denominator for lowest terms.
- In math, mixed numbers convert first: `2 1/2 = 5/2` before any arithmetic.
- In math, `1/2 = 0.5`, `1/4 = 0.25`, `3/4 = 0.75`, `1/3 ≈ 0.333` are the anchor conversions.
- In math, comparing fractions cross-multiplies: `a/b < c/d` iff `ad < bc` for positive denominators.
- In math, percent means per hundred: 25 percent equals 25/100 = 1/4.
- In math, ratios `a:b` scale like fractions; doubling recipes doubles both sides.
- In math, never add denominators: `1/2 + 1/3 = 5/6`, not 2/5.
