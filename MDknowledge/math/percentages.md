# Percentages essentials

- In math, percent means per hundred: 30 percent of x equals 0.30·x.
- In math, percent change is `(new − old)/old × 100`, signed for growth versus decline.
- In math, percentage points differ from percent: 10 to 12 percent is +2 points but +20 percent.
- In math, successive discounts multiply: 20 percent off then 10 percent off totals 28 percent off.
- In math, `x increased by 200%` means triple the original, not double.
- In math, to reverse a p-percent increase, divide by (1 + p/100), not subtract p percent.
- In math, compound growth applies percents to running totals each period, not the principal.
- In math, interest `A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt)` compounds n times yearly at rate r.
- In math, the rule of 72 estimates doubling time as 72 divided by the percent rate.
- In math, margins divide by revenue while markups divide by cost — same profit, different base.
