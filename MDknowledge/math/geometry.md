# Geometry essentials

- The Pythagorean theorem `a² + b² = c²` relates the legs and hypotenuse of a right triangle.
- Triangle interior angles always sum to 180 degrees; quadrilaterals sum to 360.
- A circle's circumference is `2πr` and its area is `πr²` for radius r.
- The area of a triangle is `½·base·height`; any side can serve as the base.
- Rectangle area is length times width; a square of side s has area s² and perimeter 4s.
- The area of a trapezoid is the average of the parallel sides times the height.
- Complementary angles sum to 90 degrees; supplementary angles sum to 180.
- Vertical angles formed by intersecting lines are always equal.
- Parallel lines cut by a transversal create equal corresponding and alternate interior angles.
- Similar triangles share angles with proportional sides; congruent triangles match exactly.
- The 30-60-90 triangle has sides in ratio `1 : √3 : 2`; the 45-45-90 in ratio `1 : 1 : √2`.
- Sine, cosine, tangent are opposite/hypotenuse, adjacent/hypotenuse, opposite/adjacent in right triangles.
- A sphere of radius r has volume `4/3·πr³` and surface area `4πr²`.
- A cylinder's volume is base area times height (`πr²h`); a cone holds exactly one third of that.
- The distance between points is `√((x₂-x₁)² + (y₂-y₁)²)` from the Pythagorean theorem.
- Pi (π ≈ 3.14159) is the constant ratio of any circle's circumference to its diameter.
- Area scales with the square of length scale: doubling sides quadruples area.
- Volume scales with the cube: doubling edges multiplies volume by eight.
