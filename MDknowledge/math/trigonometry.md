# Trigonometry essentials

- In trig, SOH-CAH-TOA maps sine, cosine, tangent to opposite/hypotenuse, adjacent/hypotenuse, opposite/adjacent.
- In trig, the unit circle defines all six functions for any angle via coordinates.
- In trig, `sin²θ + cos²θ = 1` is the Pythagorean identity behind countless simplifications.
- In trig, sine and cosine repeat every 360 degrees (2π radians); tangent repeats every 180.
- In trig, radians measure angle by arc length: π radians equals 180 degrees.
- In trig, the law of sines `a/sin A = b/sin B` solves triangles from angle-side pairs.
- In trig, the law of cosines `c² = a² + b² − 2ab·cos C` generalizes Pythagoras to any triangle.
- In trig, inverse functions (`arcsin`, `arccos`, `arctan`) recover angles from ratios.
- In trig, amplitude, period, and phase shift describe every sinusoidal wave completely.
- In trig, `tan θ = sin θ / cos θ` links the three primary functions algebraically.
