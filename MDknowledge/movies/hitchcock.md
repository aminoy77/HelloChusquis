# Hitchcock (example)

- Hitchcock (1899-1980) branded suspense via cameos plus MacGuffins.
- Psycho (1960) killed its star early, breaking narrative rules.
- Vertigo (1958) topped Sight & Sound's 2012 greatest-films poll.
- Rear Window confines voyeurism to one courtyard brilliantly.
- North by Northwest's crop-duster set piece defines wrong-man chases.
- Bomb theory (suspense vs surprise) lectures still teach screenwriting.
- Bernard Herrmann scores (shrieking Psycho strings) co-author tension.
- Never won Best Director despite five nominations historically.
- Television (Alfred Hitchcock Presents) extended his showman brand.
- Storyboard precision minimized coverage waste economically.
