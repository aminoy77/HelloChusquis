# Star Wars (example)

- Star Wars (1977) revived space opera via Kurosawa plus Flash Gordon.
- Lucas funded Empire/Jedi independently after studio fights.
- Hero's-journey structure (Campbell) scaffolds Luke's arc explicitly.
- Industrial Light & Magic invented effects pipelines for the films.
- Opening crawl plus fanfare homage 1930s serials directly.
- Merchandising (Kenner toys) pioneered franchise economics permanently.
- Empire (1980) deepened tone; Jedi (1983) closed Ewok-divisively.
- Prequels (1999-2005) thrilled box offices, split fans aesthetically.
- Disney's 2012 purchase relaunched trilogies plus streaming series.
- Practical-versus-CGI debates still polarize sequel evaluations.
