# Kurosawa (example)

- Kurosawa (1910-1998) globalized Japanese cinema decisively.
- Rashomon (1950) won Venice, introducing the West to Japanese film.
- Seven Samurai (1954) remade as westerns/westerns-in-space repeatedly.
- Multiple perspectives (Rashomon effect) entered general language.
- Weather (rain, wind, fog) acts as emotional character in his frames.
- Late color epics (Kagemusha, Ran) painted chaos gorgeously.
- Toho collaborations ended acrimoniously mid-career (Tora! Tora! exit).
- Mifune's feral physicality defined his leading-man era.
- Storyboards painted every shot like canvases beforehand.
- Dreams (1990) anthologized his unconscious in late style.
