# Unit conversions (example)

- 1 inch equals exactly 2.54 centimeters.
- 1 mile equals 1.609 kilometers; 1 kilometer equals 0.621 miles.
- 1 meter equals 3.281 feet; 1 foot equals 30.48 centimeters.
- 1 kilogram equals 2.205 pounds; 1 pound equals 0.454 kilograms.
- 1 liter equals 0.264 US gallons; 1 US gallon equals 3.785 liters.
- 0°C equals 32°F and 100°C equals 212°F; convert Celsius to Fahrenheit with F = C·9/5 + 32.
- 0°C equals 273.15 K; kelvin never takes a degree symbol.
- 1 hectare equals 10,000 square meters (about 2.47 acres).
- 1 knot equals 1.852 km/h, defined as one nautical mile per hour.
- 1 byte is 8 bits; network speeds in Mbps divide by 8 for MB/s.
- 1 kWh equals 3.6 megajoules of energy.
- 1 horsepower (metric) equals about 735.5 watts.
- UTC offsets shift by region and season; store timestamps in UTC, render in local time.
- Unix time counts seconds since 1970-01-01 UTC, ignoring leap seconds.
- A light-year is about 9.46 trillion kilometers, a distance, not a time.
