# Nietzsche (example)

- Nietzsche (1844-1900) declared 'God is dead' diagnosing cultural nihilism.
- Master/slave morality contrasts noble creation with resentful reaction.
- Übermensch imagines self-overcoming beyond herd valuations.
- Eternal recurrence tests affirmation: would you relive this life forever?
- Will to power names life's fundamental drive beneath morals.
- Genealogy traces moral concepts to power struggles historically.
- Apollonian/Dionysian duality frames order versus ecstasy in art.
- Perspectivism denies view-from-nowhere truth while keeping rankable views.
- Breakdown (1889) ended writing; sister's edits distorted his legacy.
- Aphoristic style (Beyond Good and Evil, Zarathustra) performs philosophy literarily.
