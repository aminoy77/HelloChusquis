# Kant (example)

- Kant (1724-1804) synthesized rationalism and empiricism in three Critiques.
- Categorical imperative: act only on maxims fit for universal law.
- Humanity formulation forbids using people merely as means.
- Phenomena (appearances) differ from unknowable noumena (things-in-themselves).
- Space and time are intuitions structuring experience, not things perceived.
- Copernican revolution: objects conform to cognition, not vice versa.
- Perpetual Peace sketches federations ending war among republics.
- Kingdom of Ends imagines rational beings legislating mutually.
- Enlightenment motto 'dare to know' (sapere aude) urges intellectual courage.
- Deontology judges acts by duty-conformity, never by consequences.
