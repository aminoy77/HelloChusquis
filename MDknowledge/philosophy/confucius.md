# Confucius (example)

- Confucius (551-479 BC) taught ethics for social harmony in chaotic Zhou China.
- Ren (humaneness) plus li (ritual propriety) order relationships rightly.
- Five relationships (ruler-subject to friend-friend) map reciprocal duties.
- Filial piety anchors family as society's training ground.
- Junzi (gentleman) cultivates virtue through learning regardless of birth.
- Rectification of names demands titles match actual conduct.
- Golden-rule negative: do not impose what you would not want.
- Analects compile disciples' notes, not the master's own writing.
- Imperial exams (civil service) institutionalized Confucian meritocracy.
- East Asian schooling, hierarchy, and harmony norms still echo him.
