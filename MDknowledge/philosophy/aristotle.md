# Aristotle (example)

- Aristotle (384-322 BC) tutored Alexander before founding the Lyceum.
- Logic's syllogisms (All men mortal...) formalized valid inference first.
- Four causes (material, formal, efficient, final) explain why things exist.
- Golden mean locates virtues between vices of excess and deficiency.
- Eudaimonia (flourishing activity) defines happiness as objective, not feeling.
- Poetics analyzes tragedy through catharsis, plot unity, and character.
- Biology classified 500+ species from Lesbos lagoon observations.
- Unmoved mover argues eternal thought thinking itself (Metaphysics).
- Politics defends natural slavery and citizen rule inconsistently to moderns.
- Scholasticism fused Aristotle with Christianity for medieval universities.
