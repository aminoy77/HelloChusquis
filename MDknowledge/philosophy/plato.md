# Plato (example)

- Plato (428-348 BC) founded the Academy, Europe's first university-like school.
- Theory of Forms places perfect abstractions behind flawed sensibles.
- Allegory of the Cave figures education as ascent from shadows to light.
- Republic designs a just city (and soul) ruled by philosopher-kings.
- Tripartite soul (reason, spirit, appetite) mirrors ideal class structure.
- Symposium stages speeches theorizing love's ladder to Beauty itself.
- Meno's slave-boy geometry argues learning is recollection (anamnesis).
- Philosopher-kings unite power with wisdom to end political evils.
- Aristotle's critiques (Third Man) press Forms' coherence hardest.
- Atlantis story (Timaeus/Critias) likely moralizes Athenian imperialism.
