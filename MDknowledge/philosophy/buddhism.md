# Buddhism basics (example)

- Siddhartha Gautama (Buddha, ~5th c. BC) taught after enlightenment at Bodh Gaya.
- Four Noble Truths diagnose suffering, craving, cessation, and the path.
- Noble Eightfold Path prescribes view, resolve, speech, conduct, livelihood, effort, mindfulness, concentration.
- Impermanence (anicca) plus no-self (anatta) dissolve clinging targets.
- Karma links intentional actions to future conditions impersonally.
- Meditation (samatha/vipassana) trains calm plus insight directly.
- Mahayana adds bodhisattvas postponing nirvana for all beings.
- Zen emphasizes seated meditation and koan breakthroughs.
- Middle Way avoids indulgence and mortification extremes alike.
- Mindfulness entered therapy (MBSR) stripped of metaphysics pragmatically.
