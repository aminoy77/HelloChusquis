# American football (example)

- Offenses get four downs to advance ten yards or turn the ball over.
- Touchdowns score six plus conversion kicks or two-point tries.
- Quarterbacks lead; linemen block; skill positions run and catch.
- Super Bowls decide NFL champions each February since 1967.
- Forward passes must start behind the line of scrimmage legally.
- Punts and field goals trade possession for position and points.
- Salary caps enforce parity across the 32 franchises.
- College football feeds the NFL through annual drafts.
- Overtime rules give both sides possessions in playoff games.
- Crucial penalties (holding, pass interference) swing field position hugely.
