# Marathon running (example)

- Marathons cover 42.195 km, standardized from London 1908's royal finish.
- Kipchum's 1:59:40 (2019, unofficial) broke two hours with pacers.
- World-record progression now targets sub-2:00 in legal races.
- Carb-loading plus race-day gels fuel glycogen-dependent efforts.
- Hitting the wall (~30 km) marks glycogen depletion for the under-fueled.
- Negative splits (second half faster) beat even pacing for most amateurs.
- Qualifying times gate majors like Boston and London ballots.
- Super-shoes with carbon plates rewrote records from 2017 onward.
- Recovery runs plus sleep matter more than extra mileage late-cycle.
- Six World Marathon Majors span Tokyo to New York yearly.
