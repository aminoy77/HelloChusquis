# Golf history (example)

- Linksland Scots shepherds likely birthed golf (15th century).
- St Andrews (1552 archbishop decree) anchors the home of golf.
- James II banned golf (1457) for distracting archery practice!
- Featheries gave way to gutta-percha then Haskell balls technologically.
- Hickory shafts yielded to steel (1920s) then graphite/titanium.
- Bobby Jones's 1930 Grand Slam plus Augusta founding mythologize amateurs.
- Women's majors plus mixed events grow the game diversely now.
- Caddies read greens plus psychology for tour pros critically.
- Handicap indexes equalize casual competition mathematically.
- LIV's billions split loyalties plus OWGR points debates.
