# Sporting legends (example)

- Ali (1942-2016) floated like a butterfly as three-time heavyweight champion.
- Rumble in the Jungle (1974) rope-a-doped Foreman in Kinshasa famously.
- Pele (1940-2022) won three World Cups (1958, 1962, 1970) uniquely.
- Pele's 1,000th goal (1969) stopped Brazil to celebrate.
- Senna's Monaco mastery plus rain genius defined F1's romantic era.
- Imola 1994 (Senna/Ratzenberger weekend) forced modern F1 safety revolutions.
- Bradman's 99.94 Test average doubles nearest rivals statistically absurdly.
- Jordan's flu game plus 'last shot' (1998) mythologize clutch will.
- Comaneci's perfect 10 (1976) redefined gymnastics scoring scales.
- Owens's four Berlin 1936 golds rebutted master-race mythology athletically.
