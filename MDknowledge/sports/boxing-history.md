# Boxing history (example)

- Bare-knuckle London Prize Ring rules exhausted fighters roundlessly.
- Queensberry Rules (1867) gloved, timed, and counted modern boxing.
- Sullivan-Corbett (1892) crowned gloved heavyweight transition.
- Johnson-Jeffries (1910) carried crushing racial symbolism nationally.
- Dempsey's 1920s gates made boxing America's first million-dollar sport.
- Louis-Schmeling II (1938) punched Nazi mythology symbolically.
- Ali's exile (1967-70) cost prime years for Vietnam refusal.
- Rumble in the Jungle (1974) rope-a-doped Foreman famously.
- Women's boxing (2012 Olympics) arrived officially late but hugely.
- Weight-cutting science plus same-day weigh-ins evolve safety debates.
