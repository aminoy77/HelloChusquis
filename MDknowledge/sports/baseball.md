# Baseball (example)

- Nine innings with three outs per half-inning structure games.
- Four balls walk batters; three strikes retire them (strikeouts).
- Home runs clear outfield walls for automatic multi-run scores.
- Pitchers duel through fastballs, curveballs, sliders, and changeups.
- World Series crowns MLB champions in best-of-seven Octobers.
- Perfect games (27 up, 27 down) remain vanishingly rare feats.
- Designated hitters bat for pitchers under modern universal rules.
- Stealing bases trades out-risk for scoring position gains.
- ERA (earned run average) gauges pitchers; OPS gauges hitters.
- Spring training plus 162 games make baseball a marathon season.
