# Cricket (example)

- Cricket pits bat-and-ball sides across Tests, ODIs, and T20 formats.
- Tests run up to five days; T20 compresses to three hours.
- Batsmen score runs; bowlers take ten wickets to end innings.
- Boundaries score four (ground) or six (cleared) automatically.
- LBW dismisses batters blocking wicket-bound deliveries with pads.
- The Ashes (England-Australia since 1882) is Test cricket's fiercest rivalry.
- India's IPL made T20 a billion-dollar entertainment product.
- Spin (turn) versus pace (speed) splits bowling crafts fundamentally.
- Umpire reviews (DRS) correct howlers via ball-tracking tech.
- Follow-ons force batting again when first innings trail hugely.
