# Formula 1 (example)

- F1 grands prix award 25-18-15... points to top-ten finishers.
- Constructors' and Drivers' championships run in parallel each season.
- DRS flaps open on straights for sanctioned overtaking boosts.
- Pit stops (sub-2 seconds) swap all four tires with choreographed crews.
- Tire compounds (soft/medium/hard) trade grip against lifespan strategically.
- Budget caps (~$135M) plus wind-tunnel limits compress the field.
- Monaco's street circuit rewards qualifying over race pace uniquely.
- Sprint weekends add Saturday mini-races setting Sunday grids.
- Verstappen/Red Bull dominated the early-2020s regulation era.
- Hybrid V6 turbos with energy recovery define current power units.
