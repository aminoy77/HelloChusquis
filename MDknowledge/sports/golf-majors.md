# Golf majors (example)

- Men's majors: Masters, PGA Championship, US Open, The Open Championship.
- The Masters invites winners back annually to Augusta each April.
- The Open (1860) is the oldest major, rotating British links courses.
- Par sets expected strokes per hole; birdies beat it by one.
- Grand Slam means all four majors in one calendar year (never completed modernly).
- Tiger Woods's 15 majors include the 1997 record-shattering Masters win.
- Match play pits golfers head-to-head; stroke play totals all shots.
- Handicaps equalize amateurs of differing skill for fair games.
- Ryder Cup biennially pits USA against Europe in team match play.
- LIV Golf's 2022 arrival split the men's professional game.
