# FIFA World Cup (example)

- Uruguay hosted and won the first World Cup in 1930.
- Brazil holds five titles, the most of any nation.
- The tournament runs every four years with 32 teams (48 from 2026).
- Pele won three World Cups (1958, 1962, 1970) with Brazil.
- Maradona's 1986 'Hand of God' plus solo goal defined a tournament.
- Germany's 7-1 semifinal over Brazil shocked the 2014 hosts.
- Women's World Cup began in 1991; the USA leads with four titles.
- Golden Boot topscorers and Golden Ball best players earn individual honors.
- Qualifying spans years across six continental confederations.
- Qatar 2022 was the first winter-scheduled men's edition.
