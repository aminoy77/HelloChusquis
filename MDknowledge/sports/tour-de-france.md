# Tour de France (example)

- The Tour has run (mostly) annually since 1903 over ~3,500 km.
- Yellow jersey marks the overall leader on total time.
- Green jersey rewards sprinters' stage points; polka-dot crowns climbers.
- Team time trials and mountain stages decide modern editions.
- Lance Armstrong's seven titles were stripped for doping.
- Peloton drafting saves ~30 percent energy versus solo riding.
- _feed zones and team cars resupply riders across stages.
- Alpe d'Huez's 21 hairpins host legendary climbing battles.
- Lantern rouge honors the last-placed finisher fondly.
- Grand Tours also include Italy's Giro and Spain's Vuelta.
