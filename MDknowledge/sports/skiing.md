# Alpine skiing (example)

- Downhill (speed), slalom (technique), super-G/giant slalom blend both.
- Shaped skis (1990s) carved turns accessible to intermediates.
- St releasable bindings prevent tibia fractures at set torques.
- Wax matches snow temperature for glide versus grip tradeoffs.
- Off-piste demands avalanche gear (beacon, shovel, probe) plus training.
- Streif (Kitzbuhel) terrifies even World Cup racers yearly.
- Après-ski culture rivals slopes for resort economics.
- Climate shifts push resorts uphill plus snowmaking-dependent.
- Cross-country (skating/classic) burns more calories than any winter sport.
- Parallels (vs snowplow) mark the beginner-to-intermediate graduation.
