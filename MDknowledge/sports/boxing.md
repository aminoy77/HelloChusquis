# Boxing basics (example)

- Boxing scores clean punches across 12 (pro) or 3 (amateur) rounds.
- Weight classes (17 pro divisions) equalize fighters fairly.
- Jab-cross-hook-uppercut form the fundamental combination alphabet.
- Judges score 10-9 rounds (10-8 with knockdowns) under must-system rules.
- Knockouts end instantly; TKOs stop one-sided damage mercifully.
- Footwork (angles, pivots) beats raw power at elite levels.
- Heavyweight lineage (Ali, Tyson, Klitschko, Fury) carries cultural weight.
- Amateur headgear rules changed repeatedly over safety debates.
- Training camps (8-12 weeks) peak fighters for single nights.
- Boxing Day football fixtures share only the name, not the sport.
