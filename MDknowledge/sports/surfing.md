# Surfing basics (example)

- Wave priority (closest to peak) governs lineup etiquette strictly.
- Pop-ups must land feet positioned before the drop completes.
- Whitewater starts teach balance; green waves come after competence.
- Leashes save boards (and skulls) in wipeout zones.
- Rip currents escape sideways, never by paddling straight in.
- Wax matches water temperature for grip (tropical vs cold formulas).
- Swell period matters more than height for wave quality.
- Olympic surfing debuted Tokyo 2020 (Tahiti 2024) officially.
- Localism enforces pecking orders tensely at famous breaks.
- Foam boards forgive beginners; fiberglass rewards commitment.
