# Tennis history (example)

- Real tennis (16th c. France) precedes lawn tennis (1873 Wingfield).
- Wimbledon (1877) crowns the oldest major championship.
- Open Era (1968) admitted pros, exploding prize money since.
- Borg-McEnroe 1980 final mythologized fire-versus-ice perfectly.
- Graf's 1988 Golden Slam (all four + Olympics) stands unmatched.
- Federer-Nadal-Djokovic (Big Three) hoarded 66 Slams collectively.
- Equal prize money (US Open 1973 first) spread unevenly still.
- Tiebreaks (Van Alen) ended endless deuce sets pragmatically.
- Davis Cup national teams predate individual tour structures.
- Pickleball's boom now pressures tennis participation numbers.
