# Rugby (example)

- Rugby union fields 15 players; league variant fields 13 with different rules.
- Forward passes are illegal; kicks and laterals advance territory instead.
- Tries (5 points) plus conversions anchor rugby union scoring.
- Scrums restart after minor infringements with packed shoving contests.
- Lineouts throw sideline balls to lifted jumpers athletically.
- New Zealand's All Blacks haka precedes internationals intimidatingly.
- Rugby World Cups run quadrennially since 1987 (South Africa won 2019, 2023).
- Rucks recycle tackled ball with arriving support players legally bound.
- Sin-bins (10 minutes) punish foul play without full dismissals.
- Sevens (7-a-side, 14-minute games) joined the Olympics in 2016.
