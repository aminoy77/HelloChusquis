# NBA basketball (example)

- The NBA formed in 1946 (as BAA) merging with the NBL in 1949.
- Games run four 12-minute quarters with a 24-second shot clock.
- Michael Jordan's six Bulls titles (1991-93, 1996-98) defined an era.
- LeBron James passed Kareem as all-time scoring leader in 2023.
- Three-pointers (1979 adoption) reshaped modern spacing and strategy.
- Draft lotteries allocate top picks to the weakest teams.
- Salary caps plus luxury taxes constrain superteam spending.
- Triple-doubles (double digits in three stats) mark all-around games.
- Playoffs seed 16 teams through four best-of-seven rounds.
- Global academies now feed MVPs from Serbia, Greece, and Cameroon.
