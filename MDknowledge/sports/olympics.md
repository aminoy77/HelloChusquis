# Olympic Games (example)

- Modern Olympics revived in Athens in 1896 through Coubertin's campaign.
- Five rings symbolize inhabited continents joined in competition.
- Summer and Winter Games split to alternating even years since 1994.
- Marathon commemorates Pheidippides' legendary run from Marathon to Athens.
- Gold medals are mostly silver coated with six grams of gold.
- Amateurism rules fell; professionals now dominate most sports.
- Boycotts (1980 Moscow, 1984 Los Angeles) weaponized Cold War editions.
- Paralympics run parallel since Rome 1960 for athletes with disabilities.
- Host-city costs routinely overrun, sparking referendum rejections lately.
- Breaking (breakdancing) debuted at Paris 2024's program.
