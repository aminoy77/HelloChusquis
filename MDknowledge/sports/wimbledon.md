# Wimbledon (example)

- Wimbledon (1877) is the oldest tennis major, played on grass.
- All-white dress codes still govern players strictly.
- Strawberries and cream is the signature spectator snack.
- Centre Court's roof (2009) ended rain-delayed finals.
- Federer's eight men's titles lead the Open Era counts.
- Longest match: Isner-Mahut 11 hours over three days (2010).
- Final-set tiebreaks (12-12, now 10-point) replaced endless deciders.
- Royal Box hosts British royalty plus invited champions.
- Ball boys/girls (BBGs) train months for the fortnight's pace.
- Queue campers earn Centre Court tickets through overnight lines.
