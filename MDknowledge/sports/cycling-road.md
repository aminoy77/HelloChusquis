# Road cycling (example)

- Drafting saves ~30 percent energy in peloton cores.
- Grand Tours (Giro, Tour, Vuelta) span three weeks each yearly.
- Climbers, sprinters, GC riders, domestiques specialize distinctly.
- Power meters (watts/kg) quantify training precisely now.
- Bonking (glycogen depletion) ends rides abruptly and miserably.
- Pacelines rotate pulls fairly on group rides traditionally.
- Helmet laws vary; head-injury data favors wearing regardless.
- Bike fits prevent knee/back damage across thousands of pedal strokes.
- Tubeless sealant plus CO₂ fix most roadside flats fastest.
- Zwift gamified indoor winters into social competition.
