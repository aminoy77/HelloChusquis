# US Declaration of Independence (example)

- Congress adopted the Declaration on 4 July 1776, drafted mainly by Jefferson.
- 'Life, liberty, pursuit of happiness' reworks Locke for revolution.
- Grievances list George III's abuses justifying separation legally.
- Signers (56) pledged lives, fortunes, sacred honor knowingly.
- Engrossed parchment signed mostly 2 August, not July 4th itself.
- Original slavery passage cut to keep Southern colonies aboard.
- National Archives displays it faded under argon and low light.
- Jefferson/Adams both died 4 July 1826, fifty years to the day.
- Copies (Dunlap broadsides) spread the news at horse speed.
- Independence Day outshines the Constitution's quieter September birthday.
