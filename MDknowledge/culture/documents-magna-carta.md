# Magna Carta (example)

- King John sealed Magna Carta at Runnymede in June 1215.
- Rebel barons demanded limits after taxes, wars, and quarrels with Rome.
- Clause 39 promises no free man imprisoned without lawful judgment.
- Clause 40 vows justice sold, denied, or delayed to none.
- Pope Innocent III annulled it within months; civil war resumed.
- Reissues (1216, 1217, 1225) trimmed it into workable statute law.
- American revolutionaries cited it against Parliamentary overreach.
- Four originals survive (British Library ×2, Lincoln, Salisbury).
- Most clauses concerned feudal dues, not universal rights originally.
- Mythologizing outran text: symbol eclipsed medieval technicalities.
