# Paris Agreement (example)

- 196 parties adopted the climate pact in December 2015.
- Goal: hold warming well below 2°C, pursuing 1.5°C limits.
- NDCs (national pledges) ratchet upward every five years by design.
- Binding procedure (reporting, reviews) wraps non-binding targets deliberately.
- US exits/re-entries (2017, 2021, 2025 swings) test durability.
- $100B yearly climate-finance promise (then successor goals) funds transitions.
- Global stocktakes (first 2023) grade collective progress publicly.
- Loss-and-damage fund (2022) compensates vulnerable states separately.
- 1.5°C pathways demand halving emissions this decade, scientists warn.
- COP summits negotiate implementation details annually since.
