# Portuguese language (example)

- Portuguese counts ~260M speakers, overwhelmingly Brazilian.
-  nasal vowels (ão, õe) distinguish meaning musically.
- European versus Brazilian norms differ in pronouns, gerunds, and lexicon.
- Galician proximity makes Spain's northwest partially intelligible.
- Age-of-Discovery spread seeded Africa (Angola, Mozambique) and Asia (Goa, Macau).
- 1990 Orthographic Agreement unified spellings across Lusophonie controversially.
- Bossa nova lyrics globalized the language's soft cadences.
- Formal você coexists with regional tu usage confusing learners.
- African Portuguese literatures (Mia Couto) win global prizes increasingly.
- Fado music's saudade untranslatably aches in song.
