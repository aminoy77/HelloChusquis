# Japanese yen (example)

- Yen (¥) divides into sen (unused) with coins to ¥500 and notes to ¥10,000.
- Meiji modernization (1871) decimalized currency replacing ryo system.
- Bank of Japan's ultra-loose policy (QQE) fought deflation for decades.
- Yen carry trades borrow cheap yen investing higher-yield currencies.
- Cash dominance persists despite cashless pushes (Suica, PayPay).
- New series (2024) honor Shibusawa, Tsuda, Kitasato with holograms.
- Watermark portraits authenticate alongside holograms.
- 10,000-yen notes feature Fukuzawa Yukichi (until 2024 series).
- Deflation decades made yen a safe-haven currency in crises.
- Coin purses (gamaguchi) remain daily fashion accessories.
