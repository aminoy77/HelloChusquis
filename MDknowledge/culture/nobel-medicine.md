# Nobel Medicine highlights (example)

- Behring (1901, serum therapy) opened medicine's prize account.
- Fleming/Florey/Chain (1945) turned penicillin into salvation.
- Watson/Crick/Wilkins (1962) revealed DNA's double helix (Franklin omitted).
- mRNA pioneers (Karikó/Weissman, 2023) enabled COVID vaccines.
- CRISPR duo (Charpentier/Doudna, 2020) edited genes precisely.
- IVF pioneer Edwards (2010) enabled millions of births.
- Prions (Prusiner, 1997) proved infectious proteins possible.
- Monoclonal antibodies (1984) spawned targeted therapies industry-wide.
- Malaria duo (2015 artemisinin) saved hundreds of millions.
- Human Genome mappers went unprized; tool-builders often precede discoverers.
