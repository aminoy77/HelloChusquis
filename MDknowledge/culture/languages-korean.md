# Korean language (example)

- Korean counts ~80M speakers split North/South with growing divergence.
- Hangul alphabet (1443, Sejong) fits phonetics scientifically by design.
- Honorific verb endings calibrate formality across seven speech levels.
- Topic/subject particles (eun/neun, i/ga) structure information flow.
- SOV order plus agglutinative endings echo Japanese/Turkish patterns.
- Hanja (Chinese characters) faded from dailies but persist academically.
- K-pop lyrics globalize Konglish phrases among learners rapidly.
- Counting needs native versus Sino-Korean sets by object type.
- North Korean vocabulary purges foreign loans ideologically.
- TOPIK exams gate university and work visas by level.
