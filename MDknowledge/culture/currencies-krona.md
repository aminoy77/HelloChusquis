# Scandinavian crowns (example)

- Sweden (krona), Norway/Denmark (krone) descend from 1873 monetary union.
- Symbols kr plus SEK/NOK/DKK codes distinguish the three crowns.
- Sweden mulls e-krona CBDC as cash fades fastest globally.
- Norway's oil fund buffers krone swings from crude prices.
- Denmark pegs to the euro via ERM-II, shadowing ECB policy.
- Iceland's krona (ISK) collapsed spectacularly in the 2008 crisis.
- Cashless Sweden leaves tourists card-dependent entirely.
- Commemorative crowns celebrate monarchs and anniversaries regularly.
- Union dissolution (1905) split Swedish-Norwegian monetary paths.
- Exchange kiosks (Forex) survive at borders and airports.
