# Spanish language (example)

- Spanish counts ~500M native speakers, second globally after Mandarin.
- Latin roots plus Arabic loans (~4,000 words) flavor its vocabulary.
- Royal Academy (RAE) standardizes norms across 23 academies worldwide.
- Voseo (Argentina, Uruguay) replaces tu conjugations regionally.
- Usted preserves formal distance; tutear signals closeness socially.
- Rolling RR plus five pure vowels simplify pronunciation predictably.
- Gendered nouns (el/la) extend to objects without natural gender.
- Subjunctive moods encode doubt, desire, and unreality grammatically.
- Spanish-language media (teleseryes, reggaeton) globalize culture rapidly.
- US Spanish speakers (~42M native) exceed Spain's population.
