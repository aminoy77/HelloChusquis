# MLK 'I Have a Dream' (example)

- King spoke 28 August 1963 to 250,000 at the Lincoln Memorial.
- Mahalia Jackson's shouted prompt ('tell them about the dream!') improvised the climax.
- Dream sequence riffs on Amos, Isaiah, and American creeds musically.
- Bad-check metaphor indicted America's promissory default to Black citizens.
- Live TV carried civil rights moral witness into living rooms.
-  FBI wiretaps stalked King before, during, and after the march.
- March demands (jobs, freedom) get overshadowed by dream soundbites.
- Nobel Prize followed in 1964 as movement leverage.
- Annual replays ritualize the speech every January holiday.
- Full text spans economic justice passages rarely quoted.
