# Nobel Physics highlights (example)

- Rontgen (1901, X-rays) opened the prize's first ceremony account.
- Einstein (1921) won for photoelectric effect, not relativity.
- Marie Curie (1903, shared) remains the only double-Nobel woman ever.
- Transistor trio (Bardeen, Brattain, Shockley, 1956) enabled electronics age.
- CMB discoverers (Penzias/Wilson, 1978) confirmed Big Bang glow.
- Higgs/Englert (2013) crowned half-century boson hunts at CERN.
- Gravitational-wave trio (2017) heard colliding black holes first.
- Attosecond pioneers (2023) filmed electron motions in real time.
- Quantum entanglement trio (2022) settled Einstein-Bohr debates experimentally.
- Women remain starkly underrepresented among physics laureates.
