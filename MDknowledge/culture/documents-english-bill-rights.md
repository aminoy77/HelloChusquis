# English Bill of Rights (example)

- Parliament enacted it in 1689 after James II's overthrow (Glorious Revolution).
- Barred standing armies, excessive bail, and cruel punishments explicitly.
- Guaranteed parliamentary speech freedom plus frequent parliaments.
- Protestant succession conditions shaped monarchy rules durably.
- American Bill of Rights borrowed phrasing and principles directly.
- 1689 plus 1688 invasion double as constitutional watershed.
- Petition rights protected subjects approaching the monarch.
- Jurors must serve in prescribed cases under its terms.
- Parliamentary sovereignty doctrine grew from its settlement logic.
- Originals rest in Parliamentary Archives for scholars.
