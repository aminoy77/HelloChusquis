# Euro (example)

- Euro (€) serves ~350M people across 20 eurozone states.
- Coins share common sides; national sides vary by issuer.
- Banknotes depict fictional bridges/doors, avoiding national favoritism.
- ECB (Frankfurt) sets rates for the whole bloc controversially at times.
- Convergence criteria (deficit, debt, inflation) gate membership strictly.
- Greece's crisis (2010s) tested irreversibility ('whatever it takes').
- Euro eliminated conversion costs boosting intra-EU trade measurably.
- Cash euros remain beloved despite digital-payment growth.
- Microstates (Andorra, Monaco) mint euros via monetary agreements.
- Eurozone enlargement continues as candidates meet criteria.
