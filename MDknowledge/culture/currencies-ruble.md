# Russian ruble (example)

- Ruble (₽) divides into 100 kopeks with 5,000 top notes.
- Kopek name descends from spear (kopiyo) on Novgorod coins.
- 1998 redenomination (1,000:1) followed Soviet collapse hyperinflation.
- Sanctions (2022+) spurred capital controls plus yuan/rupee settlement.
- Central Bank rate shocks (20% in 2022) defend sliding rubles.
- Oil/gas revenues fund budgets, transmitting crude swings to currency.
- Mir payment system substitutes for sanctioned Visa/Mastercard rails.
- Cash dollars/euros trade at premiums in street markets during crises.
- Digital ruble pilots programmable state money cautiously.
- 1990s kiosk dollars taught distrust no reform fully erased.
