# Churchill speeches (example)

- 'We shall fight on the beaches' (4 June 1940) stiffened Dunkirk aftermath.
- 'Finest hour' speech framed survival as civilization's hinge moment.
- 'Never was so much owed...' honored RAF pilots during the Blitz.
- Blood, toil, tears, sweat (May 1940) promised only struggle honestly.
- Rhetoric drew on English Bible rhythms plus classical periods.
- Broadcast repeats (not live Commons delivery) spread most famous lines.
- Sinews-of-peace Fulton speech (1946) named the Iron Curtain publicly.
- Painting and bricklaying hobbies humanized the war leader later.
- Nobel Prize in Literature (1953) crowned the orator-historian.
- Speech drafts show relentless revision behind seeming spontaneity.
