# English language (example)

- English counts ~1.5B speakers total, ~380M natively, topping global use.
- Germanic core plus Norman French plus Latin loans triple vocabulary options.
- Shakespeare (~1,700 coinages) plus King James Bible shaped modern diction.
- Spelling fossilizes history (knight, colonel) frustrating learners eternally.
- Stress-timed rhythm contrasts syllable-timed Spanish/French clearly.
- Global Englishes (Indian, Nigerian, Singlish) localize norms legitimately.
- Lingua-franca roles span aviation, science, and diplomacy by treaty.
- Phonics irregularity (though/through/tough) reflects layered invasions.
- New words (~5,000 yearly tracked) enter via tech and slang constantly.
- Plain-English movements fight bureaucratic fog in law and government.
