# Nobel Peace highlights (example)

- Dunant (Red Cross founder) shared the first 1901 peace prize.
- MLK (1964) channeled prize money back into civil rights work.
- Mother Teresa (1979) split opinion on care versus conditions.
- Mandela and de Klerk shared the 1993 prize jointly.
- Malala (2014, age 17) remains the youngest laureate ever.
- EU (2012) won for six decades of continental peace.
- Controversial picks (Kissinger 1973, Obama 2009) sparked resignations and debate.
- Narges Mohammadi won in 2023 while jailed in Iran.
- Norwegian parliament appoints the five-member peace committee.
- Gandhi's omission stands the committee's most regretted absence.
