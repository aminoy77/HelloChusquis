# Dutch language (example)

- Dutch counts ~24M speakers across Netherlands, Belgium, Suriname.
- English's closest major Germanic cousin after Frisian and German.
- Afrikaans descends from 17th-century Dutch colonial speech.
- Guttural 'g' challenges learners more than any grammar rule.
- Word order (V2 main, verb-final subordinate) mirrors German logic.
- Diminutives (-je) express affection ubiquitously (biertje!).
- English loans flood tech and business registers daily.
- Tolerant drug/prostitution policies lexicalized globally (coffeeshop!).
- Polder vocabulary (dijk, molen) encodes water-management history.
- NT2 exams gate civic integration with language proof.
