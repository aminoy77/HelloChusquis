# Swahili language (example)

- Swahili counts ~200M speakers, Africa's most-spoken indigenous tongue.
- Bantu grammar plus Arabic loans reflect Indian Ocean trade history.
- Noun classes (m-/wa-, ki-/vi-) govern agreement throughout sentences.
- No tones simplify pronunciation versus many African neighbors.
- AU and EAC official status elevates it continentally.
- Hakuna matata globalized one phrase beyond all others.
- Standardized on Zanzibar dialect under colonial administration.
- Latin script replaced Arabic script in the 19th century.
- Lingua-franca role spans Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda, DRC markets.
- Taarab music fuses Swahili poetry with global instrumentation.
