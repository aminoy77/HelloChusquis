# Nobel Prizes (example)

- Alfred Nobel's 1895 will funded prizes from dynamite fortune interest.
- Physics, Chemistry, Medicine, Literature, Peace awarded since 1901.
- Economics (1969, Riksbank-funded) is technically a memorial prize.
- Stockholm hosts all but Peace (Oslo, per Nobel's will).
- Medals plus ~$1M rewards fluctuate with foundation returns.
- Laureates lecture obligingly, preserving knowledge publicly.
- Shared prizes split halves/quarters among collaborators routinely.
- Controversies (Gandhi's omission, literature politics) recur yearly.
- Peace concert plus torchlight parades mark Oslo ceremonies.
- Nominations seal 50 years, feeding speculation industries.
