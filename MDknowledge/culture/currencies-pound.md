# British pound (example)

- Sterling (£) divides into 100 pence with £5-£50 polymer notes.
- Oldest continuously used currency, originating ~775 AD silver pennies.
- Decimal Day (1971) replaced shillings/pence with new pence.
- Bank of England (1694) issues England/Wales notes; Scots/NI banks vary designs.
- Pound's reserve role faded postwar to dollar dominance gradually.
- Black Wednesday (1992) ejected sterling from the ERM traumatically.
- Polymer notes (2016+) survive washes better than paper predecessors.
- Cash use collapsed below 15 percent of UK payments recently.
- Guinea/pricing traditions linger in auctions and horse racing.
- Brexit ended euro-adoption debates definitively for Britain.
