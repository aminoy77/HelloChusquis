# Mexican peso (example)

- Peso ($) divides into 100 centavos with $1000 top notes.
- Spanish dollars (pieces of eight) were the first global currency.
- $ sign itself likely descends from peso abbreviations historically.
- 1993 nuevo peso lopped three zeros after 1980s inflation.
- Banxico floats freely, intervening only against disorderly moves.
- Remittances (~$60B yearly, mostly US) rival oil export earnings.
- Commemorative coins celebrate independence heroes.
- Polymer 20/50-peso notes resist tropical humidity better.
- Aztec calendar stone graces the $10 coin face iconically.
- Tourist dollars accepted widely but at poor effective rates.
