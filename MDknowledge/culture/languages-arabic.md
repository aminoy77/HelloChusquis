# Arabic language (example)

- Arabic counts ~400M speakers across 25+ official-status states.
- Right-to-left script with 28 letters changing shape by position.
- Root-and-pattern morphology builds word families from consonant skeletons.
- Diglossia splits formal Modern Standard from daily dialects sharply.
- Quranic classical prestige anchors 1,400 years of literary continuity.
- Definite article al- prefixes nouns (and sun/moon-letter assimilations).
- No capital letters; emphasis comes from wording, not typography.
- Calligraphy elevates script to sacred visual art form.
- Loanwords (algebra, coffee, sofa) entered European tongues via Arabic.
- Dialects (Egyptian, Levantine, Maghrebi) challenge mutual intelligibility.
