# Turkish language (example)

- Turkish counts ~85M speakers from Balkans to Central Asia's edge.
- Agglutination stacks suffixes (ev-ler-iniz-den = from your houses).
- Vowel harmony constrains suffix vowels to front/back classes.
- Ataturk's 1928 Latin alphabet replaced Ottoman Arabic script abruptly.
- SOV order plus postpositions mirror Japanese structure surprisingly.
- No grammatical gender simplifies pronouns to single 'o'.
- Azeri mutual intelligibility reaches conversational levels.
- French loans (kuaför, pantalon) mark Tanzimat modernization waves.
- Honorifics (Bey, Hanim) plus plural-you encode respect compactly.
- State broadcaster TRT standardizes norms nationwide daily.
