# Hindi language (example)

- Hindi counts ~340M natives (600M+ total) centered on North India.
- Devanagari script writes left-to-right with characteristic headline bars.
- Sanskrit roots plus Persian/Arabic/English loans layer its lexicon.
- Gendered nouns (masculine/feminine) govern adjective and verb agreement.
- Postpositions replace prepositions (table-on versus on-table order).
- Honorific tiers (tu/tum/aap) calibrate intimacy and respect precisely.
- Bollywood films spread Hindi comprehension far beyond native zones.
- Hinglish code-mixing dominates urban youth and advertising speech.
- Urdu shares grammar/colloquial base, diverging in script and formal lexicon.
- Constitutional official status (with English) anchors administration.
