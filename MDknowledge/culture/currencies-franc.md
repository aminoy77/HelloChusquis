# Swiss franc (example)

- Franc (CHF) divides into 100 rappen/centimes stably for centuries.
- Franc peg to euro (2011-2015) ended in the famous Frankenschock surge.
- Safe-haven demand spikes the franc in every global scare.
- 1,000-franc notes rank among the world's highest denominations.
- SNB charges negative rates at times to deter hot-money inflows.
- Liechtenstein uses Swiss francs via customs union agreement.
- Coins feature Helvetia and William Tell motifs nationally.
- Cash remains beloved: Swiss ATMs dispense 200-franc notes routinely.
- Gold backing ended 1999, freeing policy after referendum.
- Strong franc squeezes exporters (watches, chocolate) cyclically.
