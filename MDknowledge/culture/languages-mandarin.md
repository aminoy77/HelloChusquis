# Mandarin Chinese (example)

- Mandarin counts ~1B speakers, the most native tongues worldwide.
- Four tones (plus neutral) distinguish otherwise identical syllables.
- Characters encode morphemes, not sounds; ~3,000 cover daily literacy.
- Pinyin romanization teaches pronunciation to learners systematically.
- Simplified (mainland) versus traditional (Taiwan/HK) scripts split readers.
- Measure words (ge, ben, tiao) classify nouns obligatorily.
- No conjugations or plurals: aspect particles mark time instead.
- Tonal minimal pairs (ma: mother/hemp/horse/scold) punish sloppy pitch.
- Chengyu four-character idioms compress classical allusions densely.
- HSK exams gate academic and work opportunities by level.
