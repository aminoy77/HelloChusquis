# Kennedy moon speech (example)

- 'We choose to go to the Moon' (Rice Stadium, 12 Sept 1962) set the deadline.
- 'Not because easy, but because hard' framed difficulty as the reason.
- Speech answered critics of lunar program costs directly.
- Space Race prestige versus Soviets drove the deadline politics.
- Apollo 11 landed 1969, meeting Kennedy's decade pledge posthumously.
- Rice's football stadium crowd (35,000) heard it live in Texas heat.
- Speechwriters (Sorensen) polished Kennedy's cadences meticulously.
- Cold War soft power motivated budgets Congress then approved.
- Moonshot entered language as shorthand for audacious goals.
- Johnson carried the program through after Dallas assassination.
