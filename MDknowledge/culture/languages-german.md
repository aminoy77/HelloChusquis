# German language (example)

- German counts ~100M natives, Europe's most-spoken mother tongue.
- Compound nouns stack precisely (Donaudampfschifffahrt) without spaces.
- Three genders (der/die/das) plus four cases challenge learners systematically.
- Verb-final subordinate clauses delay payoffs characteristically.
- Capitalized nouns visually mark entities in every sentence.
- Umlauts (ä/ö/ü) plus ß encode sounds English lacks entirely.
- Formal Sie versus familiar du mirrors French tu/vous socially.
- Dialects (Bavarian, Swiss German) diverge toward mutual difficulty.
- Philosophy and science German (Kant, Einstein) shaped modern thought.
- Spelling reforms (1996) still irritate traditionalists decades on.
