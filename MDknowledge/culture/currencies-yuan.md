# Chinese yuan (example)

- Yuan/renminbi (¥/元) divides into 10 jiao and 100 fen officially.
- PBOC manages a crawling band against currency baskets, not free float.
- Redbacks (red ¥100 notes) feature Mao Zedong's portrait.
- Digital yuan (e-CNY) pilots state digital cash at Olympic scale.
- IMF SDR basket inclusion (2016) certified reserve-currency arrival.
- Capital controls limit outflows to $50,000 yearly per citizen.
- Hong Kong dollars and Macanese patacas circulate separately under one country.
- Counterfeit pens test the security threads of market-stall cash.
- Mobile pay (Alipay/WeChat Pay) leapfrogged cards across China.
- Yuan internationalization funds Belt-and-Road loans increasingly.
