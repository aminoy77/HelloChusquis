# Gettysburg Address (example)

- Lincoln delivered 272 words at Gettysburg cemetery dedication (19 Nov 1863).
- 'Four score and seven' dates the nation to 1776 founding.
- 'Government of/by/for the people' redefined democracy's promise.
- Everett's two-hour oration preceded Lincoln's two-minute remarks.
- Five manuscript versions differ slightly in wording and titles.
- Newspaper transcriptions garbled phrases amid telegraph haste.
- Conceived-in-liberty framing made Union cause moral, not merely territorial.
- Memorized by generations of American schoolchildren since.
- Bliss copy (signed, titled) hangs as the standard text in the White House.
- Brevity itself became the speech's rhetorical legend.
