# UN Charter (example)

- San Francisco Conference (1945) drafted the Charter with 50 states.
- Purposes: peace, rights, self-determination, cooperation in order.
- Security Council five permanents wield individual vetoes controversially.
- General Assembly resolutions recommend; Council decisions bind legally.
- Chapter VII authorizes sanctions through military action graduatedly.
- ICJ (The Hague) settles state disputes consentingly plus advisory opinions.
- Secretary-General good offices mediate where councils deadlock.
- Membership grew 51 to 193 as decolonization unfolded.
- Charter amendment needs P5 plus two-thirds Assembly assent (never used).
- Critics cite veto paralysis (Syria, Ukraine) as design flaw.
