# Italian language (example)

- Italian counts ~65M natives, closest major tongue to Latin.
- Dante's Tuscan vernacular became the national standard via literature.
- Musical terms (allegro, forte, opera) Italianized global scores.
- Double consonants (pala/palla) distinguish meanings audibly.
- Formal Lei versus informal tu mirrors French social grammar.
- Regional dialects (Neapolitan, Sicilian) remain first languages locally.
- Gesture accompanies speech famously (though stereotyped).
- Slow Food movement defends culinary lexicon alongside biodiversity.
- Fashion lexicon (prêt-à-porter rivals) borrows Italian terms widely.
- Diaspora communities (Argentina, USA) preserve distinct varieties.
