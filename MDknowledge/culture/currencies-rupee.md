# Indian rupee (example)

- Rupee (₹) divides into 100 paise with ₹2000 notes withdrawn in 2023.
- Devanagari Ra plus Latin R fused into the 2010 ₹ symbol.
- Demonetization (2016) voided high notes overnight against black money.
- UPI instant payments made India a cashless-transaction leader.
- RBI manages floats within bands defending disorderly slides.
- Colonial rupees standardized dozens of princely currencies historically.
- Pakistan/Nepal/Sri Lanka rupees share names with distinct values.
- Gold demand (weddings, festivals) moves household savings traditionally.
- Coins below ₹5 cost more to mint than face value famously.
- Hawala informal networks predate and parallel formal banking.
