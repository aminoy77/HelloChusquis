# Energy essentials

- In physics, energy conservation says closed-system totals never change, only transform.
- In physics, kinetic energy is `½mv²` and gravitational potential is `mgh` near Earth.
- In physics, work equals force times displacement along the force: W = F·d·cos θ.
- In physics, power is work rate in watts: one watt equals one joule per second.
- In physics, efficiency divides useful output by total input, always below 100 percent.
- In physics, elastic potential `½kx²` stores spring energy releasable as motion.
- In physics, heat flows spontaneously from hot to cold, never the reverse unaided.
- In physics, specific heat capacity sets how much energy raises one kilogram one degree.
- In physics, E = mc² equates mass with enormous energy via light speed squared.
- In physics, renewable flows (sun, wind) differ from finite stocks (coal, uranium).
