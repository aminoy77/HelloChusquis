# SI units (example)

- SI bases: meter, kilogram, second, ampere, kelvin, mole, candela.
- Prefixes scale powers of ten (milli 10⁻³ through mega 10⁶) uniformly.
- Derived units combine bases: newton (kg·m/s²), joule, watt, volt.
- Dimensional analysis checks equations by unit consistency mechanically.
- Radians/steradians measure angles dimensionlessly but explicitly.
- Avogadro's number bridges atomic and macroscopic scales via the mole.
- Kelvin starts at absolute zero, unlike Celsius/Fahrenheit offsets.
- UTC plus leap seconds keep civil time aligned with Earth's wobble.
- Imperial vestiges (miles, pounds) persist in US/UK daily life.
- Convert systematically (factor-label method) to avoid mission-losing slips.
