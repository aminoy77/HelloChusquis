# Nervous system (example)

- Neurons signal via electrical spikes plus chemical synapses.
- The brain's ~86 billion neurons connect through trillions of synapses.
- Central (brain, spinal cord) commands; peripheral nerves relay to the body.
- Myelin sheaths speed axon signals up to ~120 m/s.
- Reflex arcs bypass the brain for millisecond withdrawals from danger.
- Neurotransmitters (dopamine, serotonin) tune mood, reward, and movement.
- Left brain controls right body and vice versa through decussation.
- Neuroplasticity rewires circuits with learning throughout life.
- Blood-brain barrier filters chemicals reaching neural tissue.
- Sleep consolidates memories and clears metabolic waste via glymphatics.
