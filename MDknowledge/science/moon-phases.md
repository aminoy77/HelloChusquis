# Moon phases (example)

- Moon phases reflect changing sunlit fractions, not Earth's shadow (except eclipses).
- New, crescent, quarter, gibbous, full cycle every ~29.5 days.
- Waxing grows toward full; waning shrinks back toward new.
- First/third quarters show half-discs lit from opposite sides.
- Supermoons coincide full moons with closest approach, looking slightly larger.
- Blue moons are second full moons in a month, not actually blue.
- Lunar eclipses redden the Moon through Earth's atmospheric filtering.
- The Moon always shows the same face due to tidal locking.
- Phases enabled early calendars across farming civilizations.
- Neap/spring tides strengthen around quarter and new/full moons respectively.
