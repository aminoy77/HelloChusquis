# Rocks and minerals (example)

- Igneous rocks crystallize from magma (granite intrusive, basalt extrusive).
- Sedimentary rocks layer compacted sediments (sandstone, limestone, shale).
- Metamorphic rocks transform under heat/pressure (marble from limestone).
- Minerals need crystalline structure plus definite chemistry (5-part definition).
- Mohs scale ranks hardness 1 (talc) to 10 (diamond) scratch-wise.
- Cleavage planes split minerals predictably (mica sheets, halite cubes).
- Rock cycle weathers, deposits, buries, melts, and erupts endlessly.
- Fossils date strata via index species with known ranges.
- Ore grades decide mines; gangue is the worthless remainder.
- Field tests (streak, acid, magnetism) identify hand samples cheaply.
