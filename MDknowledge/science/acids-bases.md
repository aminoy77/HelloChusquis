# Acids and bases

- In chemistry, acids donate protons (H⁺) and bases accept them in water.
- In chemistry, strong acids/bases dissociate fully; weak ones establish equilibria.
- In chemistry, neutralization of acid plus base yields salt plus water.
- In chemistry, indicators like litmus flip color across characteristic pH ranges.
- In chemistry, buffers resist pH change by absorbing added acid or base.
- In chemistry, stomach acid (~pH 2) digests food while blood holds ~pH 7.4 tightly.
- In chemistry, acid rain (pH below ~5.6) erodes stone and stresses aquatic life.
- In chemistry, titration finds unknown concentrations from measured neutralization volumes.
- In chemistry, antacids neutralize excess stomach acid with mild bases.
- In chemistry, concentrated acids demand goggles, gloves, and water-first dilution order.
