# Electricity essentials

- In physics, current measures charge flow in amperes; voltage pushes it in volts.
- In physics, Ohm's law links them through resistance: V = IR.
- In physics, series circuits share current while parallel branches share voltage.
- In physics, power dissipates as P = VI, heating resistive loads and wires.
- In physics, fuses and breakers open circuits before overloads start fires.
- In physics, grounding routes fault current safely into the earth.
- In physics, AC alternates direction (mains) while DC flows one way (batteries).
- In physics, transformers step AC voltage via magnetic induction between coils.
- In physics, insulators block flow and conductors (copper, aluminium) carry it.
- In physics, capacitors store charge and smooth ripples; batteries store chemical energy.
