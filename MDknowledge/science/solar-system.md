# Solar system (example)

- The Sun holds about 99.8 percent of the solar system's mass.
- The planets in order are Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune.
- Mercury is the smallest planet and closest to the Sun, with extreme temperature swings.
- Venus spins backwards and its CO2 atmosphere makes it the hottest planet.
- Earth is the only known planet with life and liquid-water oceans.
- Mars hosts Olympus Mons, the tallest known volcano at nearly 22 km.
- Jupiter is the largest planet; its Great Red Spot is a centuries-old storm.
- Saturn's rings are mostly water ice and debris, spanning hundreds of thousands of kilometers.
- Uranus rotates on its side with an axial tilt around 98 degrees.
- Neptune has the fastest winds recorded, exceeding 2,000 km/h.
- Pluto was reclassified as a dwarf planet by the IAU in 2006.
- The asteroid belt orbits between Mars and Jupiter.
- The Kuiper Belt beyond Neptune holds Pluto and other icy dwarf planets.
- Light takes about 8 minutes 20 seconds to travel from the Sun to Earth.
- A year on Mercury lasts 88 Earth days; on Neptune nearly 165 Earth years.
