# Black holes (example)

- Black holes pack mass inside event horizons where escape needs light speed-plus.
- Stellar black holes form when massive stars collapse past neutron degeneracy.
- Sagittarius A* (4M solar masses) anchors our galaxy's center.
- Event Horizon Telescope imaged M87* (2019) then Sagittarius A* (2022).
- Accretion disks heat infalling gas to X-ray brilliance before capture.
- Hawking radiation predicts ultra-slow quantum evaporation over eons.
- Supermassive giants (billions of suns) power quasars in young galaxies.
- Spaghettification stretches infallers via tidal gradients near small holes.
- LIGO hears black-hole mergers as spacetime chirps regularly now.
- Information paradox debates whether swallowed data is truly lost.
