# Thermodynamics (example)

- Zeroth law defines temperature via mutual equilibrium transitively.
- First law conserves energy: heat plus work balance internal changes.
- Second law raises universal entropy; ordered states decay statistically.
- Heat flows hot-to-cold spontaneously, never reverse unaided.
- Carnot efficiency caps heat engines by temperature ratios fundamentally.
- Third law makes absolute zero unreachable in finite steps.
- Entropy counts microstate multiplicity behind macroscopic irreversibility.
- Free energy (Gibbs/Helmholtz) predicts spontaneity at fixed conditions.
- Refrigerators pump heat uphill paying work (reversed engines).
- Maxwell's demon thought experiments probe information-energy links.
