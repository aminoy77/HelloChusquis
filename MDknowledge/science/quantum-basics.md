# Quantum basics (example)

- Quanta discretize energy, momentum, and charge at microscopic scales.
- Wave-particle duality grants matter wavelength (de Broglie) measurably.
- Superposition holds multiple states until measurement selects outcomes.
- Uncertainty principle bounds simultaneous position/momentum knowledge fundamentally.
- Entanglement correlates distant particles beyond classical explanation.
- Double-slit buildup (single particles interfering) demonstrates duality starkly.
- Decoherence leaks quantumness into environments, yielding classical appearances.
- Tunneling lets particles cross classically forbidden barriers (STM, fusion).
- Spin (up/down) underpins MRI magnets and quantum bits alike.
- Interpretations (Copenhagen, many-worlds) debate what math means philosophically.
