# Dinosaurs (example)

- Dinosaurs dominated Mesozoic land from ~240 to 66 million years ago.
- Triassic origins were small bipeds diversifying after Permian extinction.
- Sauropods (Argentinosaurus, 70+ tonnes) remain the largest land animals.
- T. rex's bite (~6 tonnes force) crushed bone routinely.
- Feathered fossils (China's Liaoning beds) link birds as living dinosaurs.
- Asteroid impact (Chicxulub, 66M years ago) ended non-avian dinosaurs.
- Deinonychus pack-hunting inspired Jurassic Park's raptors (oversized).
- Bonebeds (Ghost Ranch) preserve mass-death assemblages informatively.
- Growth rings reveal dinosaur metabolisms ran hot (mesothermic).
- Museums mount ~50 percent casts typically; real bones stay vaulted.
