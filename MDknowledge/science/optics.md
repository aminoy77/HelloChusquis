# Optics (example)

- Reflection obeys angle-in-equals-angle-out on smooth surfaces.
- Refraction bends at interfaces per Snell's law (n₁sinθ₁ = n₂sinθ₂).
- Lenses focus via curvature: convex converges, concave diverges.
- Total internal reflection traps light in fibers and diamonds brilliantly.
- Dispersion splits white into spectra through prisms and rainbows.
- Polarization filters transverse waves to single oscillation planes.
- Diffraction spreads waves around obstacles, limiting microscope resolution.
- Interference (thin films, holograms) colors via path differences.
- Lasers amplify coherent monochromatic light for surgery to telecom.
- Adaptive optics deform mirrors counteracting atmospheric blur live.
