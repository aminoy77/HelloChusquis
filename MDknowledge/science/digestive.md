# Digestive system (example)

- Digestion runs mouth, esophagus, stomach, small intestine, large intestine in order.
- Salivary amylase starts starch breakdown before swallowing.
- Stomach acid plus pepsin dissolve proteins while mucus shields walls.
- Small intestine villi absorb nutrients across huge folded surface.
- Pancreatic enzymes digest all macronutrient classes centrally.
- Bile (liver-made, gallbladder-stored) emulsifies fats for enzymes.
- Gut bacteria ferment fiber into vitamins and signals.
- Peristalsis waves push food without conscious control.
- Ulcers usually trace to H. pylori bacteria, not stress or spice.
- Fiber speeds transit and feeds beneficial gut flora.
