# Waves essentials

- In physics, wave speed equals frequency times wavelength: v = fλ.
- In physics, amplitude sets intensity (loudness/brightness) while frequency sets pitch/color.
- In physics, the electromagnetic spectrum runs radio, micro, infrared, visible, UV, X-ray, gamma.
- In physics, sound needs a medium; light crosses vacuum at about 300,000 km/s.
- In physics, the Doppler effect shifts pitch and color with source motion toward or away.
- In physics, reflection bounces, refraction bends at boundaries, diffraction spreads around edges.
- In physics, superposition adds overlapping waves; interference builds or cancels amplitudes.
- In physics, standing waves quantize musical notes and atomic orbitals alike.
- In physics, polarization filters transverse waves to one oscillation plane.
- In physics, resonance amplifies systems driven at their natural frequency.
