# Gravity essentials

- In physics, Newton's gravity pulls masses with F = Gm₁m₂/r² along their centers.
- In physics, surface gravity g ≈ 9.8 m/s² accelerates all falling objects equally sans air.
- In physics, weight scales with local g while mass stays constant everywhere.
- In physics, orbits balance forward velocity against continuous gravitational falling.
- In physics, escape velocity (~11.2 km/s on Earth) frees objects from a body's pull.
- In physics, tides bulge from the Moon's differential pull across Earth's diameter.
- In physics, Einstein reframed gravity as spacetime curvature, confirmed by lensing and GPS clocks.
- In physics, the Moon's gravity is about one-sixth of Earth's surface pull.
- In physics, free fall feels weightless because everything accelerates together.
- In physics, gravitational potential energy grows with height as mgh near the surface.
