# Star life cycles (example)

- Stars ignite when collapsing gas heats cores to hydrogen-fusion temperatures.
- Main-sequence stars (like the Sun) fuse hydrogen steadily for most of their lives.
- Red giants bloat when core hydrogen exhausts and shells ignite.
- Sun-like stars shed planetary nebulae, leaving white dwarf cinders.
- Massive stars fuse to iron, then collapse and rebound as supernovae.
- Supernovae forge elements heavier than iron and seed new systems.
- Neutron stars pack suns into city-sized ultradense remnants.
- White dwarfs cool over billions of years toward black dwarfs.
- Star color encodes temperature: blue hottest, red coolest.
- Hertzsprung-Russell diagrams map stellar lives by brightness versus color.
