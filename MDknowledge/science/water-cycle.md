# Water cycle (example)

- Evaporation lifts ocean water; transpiration adds plant moisture to air.
- Condensation forms clouds as rising vapor cools past dew point.
- Precipitation returns water as rain, snow, sleet, or hail.
- Infiltration recharges aquifers; runoff feeds rivers back to seas.
- The sun powers the whole cycle via evaporative energy input.
- Residence times range from days (atmosphere) to millennia (deep aquifers).
- Watersheds divide drainage to different river systems and seas.
- Glaciers store ~70 percent of fresh water, releasing it slowly.
- Urban paving short-circuits infiltration, spiking flood peaks.
- Desalination supplements arid coasts at high energy cost.
