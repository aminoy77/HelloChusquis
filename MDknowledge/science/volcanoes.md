# Volcanoes (example)

- Volcanoes vent magma where plates diverge, converge, or hotspot plumes rise.
- Shield volcanoes (Hawaii) ooze fluid basalt into gentle broad slopes.
- Stratovolcanoes (Fuji, Vesuvius) stack explosive ash and lava layers steeply.
- Calderas collapse when emptied magma chambers cave inward (Yellowstone, Toba).
- VEI scale ranks eruptions 0-8 logarithmically by ejecta volume.
- 1815 Tambora caused 1816's 'year without a summer' globally.
- Pompeii's 79 AD burial preserved Roman life under Vesuvius ash.
- Geysers and hot springs mark shallow magmatic heat (Iceland, Yellowstone).
- Lahars (volcanic mudflows) kill farther from vents than lava usually reaches.
- Volcanic soils rank among Earth's most fertile farmlands.
