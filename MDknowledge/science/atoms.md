# Atoms and matter

- In chemistry, atoms combine protons, neutrons (nucleus), and electrons (shells).
- In chemistry, atomic number counts protons and defines each element uniquely.
- In chemistry, isotopes vary neutron counts; some decay radioactively over time.
- In chemistry, electron shells fill 2, 8, 18 patterns governing reactivity.
- In chemistry, the periodic table orders elements by number, grouping similar chemistry.
- In chemistry, metals conduct and deform; nonmetals insulate and shatter; metalloids mix traits.
- In chemistry, ionic bonds transfer electrons (NaCl) while covalent bonds share them (H₂O).
- In chemistry, moles count particles: one mole holds Avogadro's 6.022×10²³ entities.
- In chemistry, pH below 7 is acidic, above 7 basic, with each unit a tenfold concentration shift.
- In chemistry, catalysts speed reactions without being consumed by lowering activation barriers.
