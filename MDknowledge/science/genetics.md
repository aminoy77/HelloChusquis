# Genetics essentials

- In genetics, DNA's four bases (A, T, C, G) pair A-T and C-G in the double helix.
- In genetics, genes encode proteins; the genome is an organism's full DNA set.
- In genetics, dominant alleles mask recessive ones in heterozygotes.
- In genetics, Punnett squares predict offspring genotype ratios from parental crosses.
- In genetics, mutations alter sequences; most are neutral, some harmful, few beneficial.
- In genetics, humans share about 99.9 percent of DNA with each other.
- In genetics, sex chromosomes (XX/XY) determine biological sex in mammals.
- In genetics, mRNA carries gene copies to ribosomes where tRNA delivers amino acids.
- In genetics, CRISPR edits targeted DNA sequences with guide-RNA precision.
- In genetics, epigenetic marks regulate genes without changing the underlying sequence.
