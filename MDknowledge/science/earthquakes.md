# Earthquakes (example)

- Earthquakes release accumulated fault stress as seismic waves.
- P waves compress fast; S waves shear slower; surface waves damage most.
- Magnitude (moment scale) is logarithmic: each unit means ~32x energy.
- Epicenters sit above hypocenters where rupture initiates underground.
- Ring of Fire hosts ~90 percent of quakes along Pacific subduction.
- 1960 Chile (M9.5) remains the largest instrumentally recorded quake.
- Tsunamis follow undersea thrusts displacing whole water columns.
- Building codes (base isolation, shear walls) decide death tolls more than magnitude.
- Early-warning networks buy seconds to tens of seconds before shaking arrives.
- Aftershock sequences decay roughly inversely with time (Omori's law).
