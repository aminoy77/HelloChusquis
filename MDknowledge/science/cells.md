# Cell biology

- In biology, cells are life's basic units; all known organisms consist of one or more.
- In biology, prokaryotes lack nuclei while eukaryotes compartmentalize DNA in one.
- In biology, mitochondria release energy via respiration and carry their own DNA.
- In biology, chloroplasts photosynthesize in plants using chlorophyll pigment.
- In biology, the nucleus stores DNA; ribosomes translate RNA into proteins.
- In biology, membranes are selective phospholipid bilayers studded with transport proteins.
- In biology, mitosis copies one cell into two genetically identical daughters.
- In biology, meiosis halves chromosomes to make gametes for sexual reproduction.
- In biology, osmosis moves water toward higher solute concentration across membranes.
- In biology, stem cells differentiate into specialized types during development and repair.
