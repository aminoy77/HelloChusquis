# Big Bang (example)

- The universe began hot and dense about 13.8 billion years ago.
- Cosmic microwave background (1965, Penzias/Wilson) is the afterglow proof.
- Hubble expansion shows galaxies receding proportionally to distance.
- Big Bang nucleosynthesis forged hydrogen, helium, and trace lithium.
- Inflation theory explains the universe's flatness and uniformity.
- Dark matter (~27 percent) and dark energy (~68 percent) dominate the budget.
- Stars forge heavy elements; supernovae scatter them for planets and life.
- First stars ignited hundreds of millions of years after the bang.
- James Webb images earliest galaxies forming under a billion years in.
- Heat death (endless expansion) is the leading long-term forecast.
