# Plate tectonics (example)

- Earth's lithosphere splits into plates drifting centimeters yearly on the mantle.
- Seafloor spreading at ridges plus subduction recycles oceanic crust continuously.
- Subduction zones spawn deep trenches, volcanoes, and megaquakes.
- Transform faults (San Andreas) grind laterally, storing quake stress.
- Continental drift (Wegener) was vindicated by magnetic and fossil evidence.
- Pangaea assembled ~335 million years ago and rifted ~175 million years ago.
- Hotspots (Hawaii, Yellowstone) burn through moving plates independently.
- Ring of Fire rims the Pacific with most quakes and eruptions.
- Mountain belts (Himalayas, Andes) crumple where continents collide.
- GPS now measures plate motions directly in millimeters per year.
