# Evolution (example)

- Natural selection favors heritable traits improving survival and reproduction.
- Common descent means all life shares ancestry readable in DNA similarities.
- Fossil transitions (Tiktaalik, Archaeopteryx) document major evolutionary shifts.
- Homologous structures (human arm, bat wing) reveal shared blueprints repurposed.
- Vestigial traits (wisdom teeth, tailbones) are evolutionary leftovers.
- Antibiotic resistance evolves visibly as drugs select survivors.
- Speciation splits populations via isolation until interbreeding fails.
- Genetic drift shifts small populations randomly, unlike directional selection.
- Convergent evolution reinvents similar forms (dolphins, ichthyosaurs) independently.
- Endosymbiosis explains mitochondria as engulfed bacteria turned organelles.
