# Photosynthesis

- In biology, photosynthesis converts CO₂ plus water into glucose plus oxygen using light.
- In biology, chlorophyll absorbs red and blue light, reflecting the green we see.
- In biology, light reactions split water (releasing O₂) and charge ATP/NADPH carriers.
- In biology, the Calvin cycle fixes CO₂ into sugars using that stored energy.
- In biology, stomata trade CO₂ intake against water loss through leaf pores.
- In biology, C4 and CAM variants concentrate CO₂ for hot, arid climates.
- In biology, photosynthesis underpins nearly all food chains directly or indirectly.
- In biology, oceans' phytoplankton produce roughly half of Earth's oxygen.
- In biology, limiting factors (light, CO₂, water) cap growth rates in farms and forests.
- In biology, respiration reverses the equation, releasing glucose energy day and night.
