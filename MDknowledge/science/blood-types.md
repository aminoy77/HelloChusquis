# Blood types (example)

- ABO groups (A, B, AB, O) plus Rh (+/-) define transfusion compatibility.
- O-negative donates to all (universal donor); AB-positive receives from all.
- Mismatches trigger hemolytic reactions, potentially fatal rapidly.
- Landsteiner's 1901 discovery earned the 1930 Nobel Prize.
- Plasma compatibility inverts cellular rules (AB plasma universal).
- Rh-negative mothers need RhoGAM shots against hemolytic disease.
- Blood type diets lack evidence; eat balanced regardless of group.
- Donation intervals (8-12 weeks) let iron stores recover safely.
- Apheresis harvests platelets/plasma selectively per demand.
- Artificial blood substitutes remain experimental, not routine.
