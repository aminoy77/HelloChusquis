# Ice ages (example)

- Milankovitch orbital cycles pace glaciations over ~100,000-year rhythms.
- Last Glacial Maximum (~20,000 years ago) locked seas 120 m lower.
- Ice sheets kilometers thick scoured Canada and Scandinavia cleanly.
- Megafauna (mammoths, saber-tooths) vanished amid warming plus hunting.
- Interglacials (like today's Holocene) punctuate longer frozen spells.
- Moraines, drumlins, and fjords read as glacial fingerprints on landscapes.
- Ice cores archive 800,000 years of air, ash, and temperature.
- Younger Dryas cold snap (~12,900 years ago) interrupted warming abruptly.
- Sea-level rise drowned Beringia and Doggerland inhabited plains.
- Anthropocene warming now outpaces all natural deglaciation rates.
