# Circulatory system (example)

- The heart's four chambers pump double circulation (lungs, then body).
- Arteries carry oxygenated blood out; veins return it (except pulmonary swap).
- Capillaries exchange gases and nutrients at single-cell thickness.
- Red cells haul oxygen; platelets clot; white cells defend.
- Blood pressure (120/80 ideal) balances perfusion against vessel strain.
- Coronary arteries feed the heart itself; blockage causes heart attacks.
- Strokes strike when brain blood flow blocks or bursts.
- Atherosclerosis narrows arteries via plaque over decades.
- ABO/Rh blood types must match in transfusions to avoid reactions.
- Aerobic exercise strengthens stroke volume, lowering resting heart rate.
