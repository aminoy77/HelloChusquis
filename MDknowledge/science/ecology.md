# Ecology basics (example)

- Ecosystems link communities plus abiotic factors into functional wholes.
- Producers (plants) fix energy; consumers eat; decomposers recycle all.
- Trophic levels lose ~90 percent of energy each step upward.
- Keystone species (otters, wolves) disproportionately stabilize webs.
- Biodiversity buffers shocks; monocultures invite collapse.
- Succession rebuilds forests from bare ground over centuries predictably.
- Carrying capacity caps populations resources sustain long-term.
- Invasive species explode minus native predators and diseases.
- Biomes (tundra to rainforest) sort by temperature plus precipitation.
- Human appropriation (~25 percent of productivity) strains global systems.
