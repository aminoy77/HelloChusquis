# Human evolution (example)

- Hominins split from chimpanzees ~7 million years ago in Africa.
- Bipedalism (Laetoli footprints, 3.6M years) preceded brain expansion.
- Lucy (Australopithecus, 3.2M years) walked upright with ape-sized brains.
- Homo erectus tamed fire and left Africa first (~1.8M years ago).
- Neanderthal DNA (1-2 percent) persists in non-African genomes.
- Modern humans (~300,000 years, Jebel Irhoud) overlapped cousins for millennia.
- Agriculture (10,000 BC) traded health for calories and civilization.
- Lactase persistence evolved with dairying in pastoralist populations.
- Skin tones track UV exposure balancing folate versus vitamin D.
- Human-chimp genomes differ ~1.2 percent in alignable regions.
