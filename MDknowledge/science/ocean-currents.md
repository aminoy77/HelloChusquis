# Ocean currents (example)

- Gyres rotate clockwise north (Coriolis) and counterclockwise south of equator.
- Gulf Stream warms Western Europe beyond its latitude's due.
- Humboldt current chills Peru's coast enabling anchovy booms.
- Thermohaline circulation (global conveyor) mixes deep oceans over millennia.
- Upwelling zones fertilize fisheries with deep nutrients reliably.
- El Nino periodically weakens Pacific upwelling, collapsing catches.
- Agulhas leakage seeds Atlantic salinity affecting circulation strength.
- Tidal currents reverse predictably with lunar cycles in straits.
- Western boundary currents (Kuroshio, Gulf Stream) run fast and warm.
- Plastic gyres accumulate debris in convergent zones tragically.
