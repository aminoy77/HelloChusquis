# Respiratory system (example)

- Diaphragm contraction draws air in; relaxation plus elasticity expels it.
- Alveoli (~480M tiny sacs) exchange gases across thin membranes.
- Oxygen binds hemoglobin; CO₂ returns mostly as blood bicarbonate.
- Surfactant keeps alveoli from collapsing between breaths.
- Cilia sweep mucus-trapped particles up and out of airways.
- Asthma narrows airways reversibly via inflammation and spasm.
- Pneumonia fills alveoli with fluid, blocking gas exchange.
- High altitude triggers extra red-cell production within weeks.
- Smoking destroys alveolar walls (emphysema) irreversibly.
- Ventilators assist when muscles or lungs cannot sustain exchange.
