# Shakespeare (example)

- Shakespeare (1564-1616) wrote ~37 plays plus 154 sonnets in London's theater boom.
- Tragedies (Hamlet, Lear, Macbeth, Othello) anatomize fatal character flaws.
- Comedies end in marriages; histories dramatize English kingship crises.
- Hamlet's delay stages thought versus action inexhaustibly.
- Globe Theatre's thrust stage shaped soliloquies and crowd scenes.
- Iambic pentameter mimics heartbeat rhythms under elevated speech.
- Authorship doubters lack evidence; Stratford records support Shakespeare.
- First Folio (1623, Heminges/Condell) saved half the plays from loss.
- Invented or popularized ~1,700 English words (eyeball, lonely, majestic).
- Film adaptations (Kurosawa's Ran) prove cross-cultural plot durability.
