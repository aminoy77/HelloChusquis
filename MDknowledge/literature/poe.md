# Edgar Allan Poe (example)

- Poe (1809-1849) invented detective fiction with Dupin's Rue Morgue (1841).
- The Raven's tapping refrain made him famous without making him rich.
- Tell-Tale Heart paces guilt through sound-obsessed narration.
- Fall of the House of Usher collapses family and mansion together.
- Philosophy of Composition claims cold calculation behind Raven effects.
- Cryptographic contests in newspapers displayed his code-breaking skill.
- West Point expulsion followed deliberate court-martial baiting.
- Baltimore death at 40 remains mysterious (cooping theory persists).
- French Symbolists translated Poe, amplifying European influence.
- Premature burial and mesmerism obsessions mark his horror science.
