# Dickens (example)

- Dickens (1812-1870) serialized novels affordable to Victorian masses.
- Oliver Twist exposed workhouse cruelty and child exploitation.
- A Christmas Carol redeemed Scrooge into shorthand for transformation.
- David Copperfield fictionalized Dickens's own factory-boy childhood.
- Bleak House satirized Chancery courts strangling suitors for generations.
- Great Expectations tracks Pip's gentleman-making disillusionment.
- Tale of Two Cities opens with the famous best/worst of times contrast.
- Pickwick Papers' sporting-club farce launched his career serially.
- Readings tours performed his characters to roaring audiences.
- Gad's Hill Place, his Rochester home, features across the novels.
