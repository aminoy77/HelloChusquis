# Homer (example)

- Iliad's wrath-of-Achilles plot spans Troy's final war year.
- Odyssey follows Odysseus's ten-year voyage home.
- Homeric epithets (rosy-fingered dawn) aid oral-formulaic memory.
- Iliad gods intervene partisanly like superpowered spectators.
- Odyssey monsters (Cyclops, Sirens) test cunning over strength.
- Homeric question debates single versus collective authorship.
- Dactylic hexameter carries both epics' rolling momentum.
- Hospitality (xenia) violations drive much of the moral plot.
- Achilles' heel proverbializes one fatal vulnerability.
- Alexandrian scholars preserved the texts establishing canons.
