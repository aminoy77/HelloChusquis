# Dante (example)

- Divine Comedy tours Hell, Purgatory, Paradise in 100 cantos.
- Inferno's contrapasso fits punishments to sins ironically.
- Beatrice guides Paradise as idealized love and theology.
- Virgil guides Hell and Purgatory as human reason's limit.
- Florentine exile (1302) fueled the poem's political settling of scores.
- Terza rima interlocking triplets chain the whole journey musically.
- Ulysses' damned voyage warns against overreaching knowledge.
- Italian vernacular (not Latin) made the epic nationally foundational.
- Paolo and Francesca episode humanizes damned lovers unforgettably.
- Manuscripts number 800+, dwarfing other medieval bestsellers.
