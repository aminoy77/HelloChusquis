# Garcia Marquez (example)

- Garcia Marquez (1927-2014) won the 1982 Nobel for One Hundred Years of Solitude.
- Macondo's Buendia saga defines magical realism's founding text.
- Journalism funded early fiction across Caribbean postings.
- Chronicle of a Death Foretold inverts mystery (killer known, motive puzzle).
- Autumn of the Patriarch sustains a dictator's endless sentences experimentally.
- Love in the Time of Cholera waited fifty years for its lovers' union.
- Friendship with Castro complicated his political reception permanently.
- Memoir Living to Tell the Tale stops before fame years deliberately.
- Magical realism treats marvels with deadpan bureaucratic normalcy.
- Spanish-reading world made him its most translated novelist.
