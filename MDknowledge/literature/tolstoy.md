# Tolstoy (example)

- Tolstoy (1828-1910) wrote War and Peace and Anna Karenina monumentally.
- War and Peace braids fictional families with Napoleonic history and essays.
- Anna Karenina opens: happy families alike, unhappy each in its own way.
- Death of Ivan Ilyich anatomizes bourgeois dying with terrifying precision.
- Confession chronicles his spiritual crisis toward Christian anarchism.
- Yasnaya Polyana school educated peasant children experimentally.
- Excommunication (1901) followed his attacks on church and state.
- Flight from home at 82 ended in Astapovo station death.
- Pacifism influenced Gandhi, who corresponded with Tolstoy directly.
- Short stories (How Much Land) distill his moral vision accessibly.
