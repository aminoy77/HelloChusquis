# Jane Austen (example)

- Austen (1775-1817) published six major novels anonymously as 'a Lady'.
- Pride and Prejudice opens with the famous universal-truth first line.
- Emma's matchmaking meddling matures into self-knowledge comedically.
- Sense and Sensibility contrasts Elinor's restraint with Marianne's feeling.
- Persuasion's second-chance naval romance reads most autumnally.
- Irony operates as Austen's scalpel on class, money, and marriage markets.
- Free indirect style slides between narrator and character consciousness.
- Chawton cottage years (1809-17) produced all six novels astonishingly fast.
- Unfinished Sanditon and juvenilia reveal experimental edges.
- Film adaptations (1995 BBC Pride onward) globalized her readership.
