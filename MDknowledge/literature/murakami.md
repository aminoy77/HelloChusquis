# Murakami (example)

- Murakami (born 1949) blends mundane routine with surreal intrusions.
- Norwegian Wood's 1960s student romance became a Japanese phenomenon.
- Wind-Up Bird Chronicle tunnels from missing cats to wartime atrocities.
- 1Q84's parallel 1984 explores cults and alternate realities.
- Jazz clubs (Peter Cat) funded early writing before full-time authorship.
- Running memoirs parallel marathon discipline with novel drafting.
- Translators (Rubin, Gabriel) co-create his English voices distinctly.
- Cats, wells, ears, and spaghetti recur as signature motifs.
- Perennial Nobel speculation keeps October watch parties annual.
- Short stories (Elephant Vanishes) distill his uncanny everyday mode.
