# Kafka (example)

- Kafka (1883-1924) published little; Brod defied burning instructions.
- Metamorphosis wakes Gregor Samsa transformed into an insect unexplained.
- Trial prosecutes Josef K. by inaccessible law for unnamed crimes.
- Castle surveyors strive toward authorities receding forever.
- Kafkaesque means bewildering bureaucracy beyond individual comprehension.
- Insurance day-job funded nocturnal writing in Prague German.
- Diaries record self-lacerating perfectionism and father conflicts.
- Parables (Before the Law) compress his vision into single pages.
- Tuberculosis ended writing at 40 in a Vienna sanatorium.
- Posthumous fame inverted his lifetime near-obscurity completely.
