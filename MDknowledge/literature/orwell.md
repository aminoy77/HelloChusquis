# Orwell (example)

- Orwell (1903-1950) fused journalism, satire, and dystopia enduringly.
- Animal Farm allegorizes revolution betrayed through Manor Farm pigs.
- Nineteen Eighty-Four coined Big Brother, doublethink, and Newspeak.
- Homage to Catalonia reports Spanish Civil War disillusion firsthand.
- Politics and the English Language prescribes concrete Anglo-Saxon prose.
- Shooting an Elephant examines imperial guilt in colonial Burma.
- Down and Out in Paris and London documents poverty immersion directly.
- Wigan Pier investigates northern English working-class life.
- Tuberculosis killed him at 46 as 1984's fame arrived.
- Orwellian now means totalitarian language manipulation universally.
