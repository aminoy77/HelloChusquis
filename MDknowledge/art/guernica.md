# Guernica (example)

- Picasso's Guernica (1937) mourns a Basque town bombed in civil war.
- Monochrome palette mimics newsprint urgency deliberately.
- Bull, horse, and fallen soldier compress Spanish suffering symbolically.
- Paris Exposition pavilion premiered it as Republican propaganda-art.
- MoMA sheltered it during Franco years per Picasso's condition.
- Returned to Spain (1981, Reina Sofia) after democracy's arrival.
- Tapestry copy hangs outside UN Security Council chambers pointedly.
- Commissioned for 1 million francs in under two months' fury.
- Cubist fragmentation renders bombing's simultaneity formally.
- Sketchbooks document 45 studies refining every figure.
