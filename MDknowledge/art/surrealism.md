# Surrealism (example)

- Surrealism (1924 Breton manifesto) mined dreams via Freudian free association.
- Dali's melting clocks (Persistence of Memory) iconize dream-logic time.
- Magritte's bowler-hatted paradoxes ('not a pipe') puzzle representation.
- Exquisite corpse games generated collective unconscious imagery.
- Automatism (Ernst frottage) bypassed conscious control deliberately.
- Frida Kahlo rejected the label while sharing dream symbolism.
- Surrealist cinema (Bunuel's Un Chien Andalou) slashed narrative logic famously.
- Women surrealists (Carrington, Tanning) reclaimed the movement recently.
- Advertising and fashion absorbed surrealist shock into commerce.
- WWII exile transplanted Paris surrealism to New York's Abstract Expressionism.
