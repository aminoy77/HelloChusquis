# Ukiyo-e (example)

- Ukiyo-e woodblock prints depicted Edo's floating-world pleasures.
- Hokusai's Great Wave (~1831) may be history's most reproduced image.
- Hiroshige's road stations (Tokaido) serialized travel views bestsellingly.
- Carvers, printers, publishers collaborated; artists designed only images.
- Prussian blue pigment arrival electrified palettes in the 1820s.
- Paris Japonisme (Van Gogh copies) transmitted composition to Impressionism.
- Censorship seals date prints precisely for modern scholars.
- Kabuki actors and courtesans starred as celebrity subjects.
- Multiple impressions vary colorings prized differently by collectors.
- Meiji photography displaced prints within decades tragically fast.
