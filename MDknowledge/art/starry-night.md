# Starry Night (example)

- Van Gogh painted Starry Night (1889) from a Saint-Remy asylum room.
- Swirling impasto skies render turbulence unlabored by photography.
- Cypress flames bridge village below and cosmic motion above.
- Painted from memory/daylight, not direct nocturnal observation.
- MoMA acquired it in 1941, anchoring modern holdings.
- Letters to Theo document its trudging, difficult execution.
- Eleven orbs count suns, moon, and Venus together in the sky.
- Color science finds physically accurate turbulence statistics in swirls.
- Don McLean's 'Vincent' ballad elegizes the painting's painter.
- Arles night cafe series led technically toward this climax.
