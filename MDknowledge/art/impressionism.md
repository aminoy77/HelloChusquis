# Impressionism (example)

- Impressionism (1870s Paris) painted light's fleeting optical effects.
- Monet's Impression, Sunrise (1872) named the movement mockingly-then-proudly.
- Plein-air painting plus portable tube paints enabled outdoor speed.
- Broken color (adjacent dabs) mixes optically in viewers' eyes.
- Salon rejections forced independent 1874 inaugural exhibition.
- Renoir's leisure scenes, Degas's dancers, Pissarro's boulevards diversified it.
- Photography's rise freed painting from documentary duty partly.
- Dealer Durand-Ruel bankrolled and American-collected the movement early.
- Post-Impressionists (Van Gogh, Cezanne) pushed past optical fidelity.
- Water Lilies murals (Orangerie) immerse viewers in Monet's garden.
