# Mona Lisa (example)

- Leonardo painted the Mona Lisa (La Gioconda) around 1503-1519.
- Sfumato softens contours into the famous ambiguous smile.
- Lisa del Giocondo, a Florentine merchant's wife, likely sat for it.
- 1911 theft (Peruggia) plus 1913 recovery made it globally famous.
- Bulletproof glass now shields it amid Louvre crowds daily.
- Landscape backgrounds use aerial perspective fading blues with distance.
- Eyebrows' absence fuels endless technical speculation.
- Parodies (Duchamp's mustache) certify its cultural saturation.
- French royal collection acquired it; revolution moved it to the Louvre.
- Insurance valuations would exceed $1B, so it never travels now.
