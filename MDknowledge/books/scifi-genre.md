# Science fiction genre (example)

- Sci-fi extrapolates science into futures testing present anxieties.
- Asimov's Foundation plus robot laws codified the genre's ethics.
- Clarke's Rama/2001 paired hard physics with cosmic mystery.
- Le Guin (Left Hand, Dispossessed) anthropologized gender and anarchy.
- Gibson's Neuromancer (1984) named cyberspace before the web existed.
- Butler's Xenogenesis probes power, consent, and survival biologically.
- Chinese sci-fi (Liu Cixin) globalized the field post-2010s.
- Hard SF foregrounds physics; space opera foregrounds adventure.
- Dystopias warn; utopias (rare) blueprint hoped-for worlds.
- Hugo/Nebula awards track yearly consensus imperfectly.
