# Mystery genre (example)

- Detective fiction promises fair-play clues solvable by readers.
- Poe's Dupin (1841) founded ratiocination before Holmes perfected it.
- Golden Age (Christie, Sayers) puzzled locked rooms elegantly.
- Hardboiled (Hammett, Chandler) traded manors for mean streets.
- Police procedurals (Rankin, Connelly) proceduralize investigation realistically.
- Cozy mysteries keep violence offstage for comfort reading.
- Noir dooms protagonists where hardboiled merely bruises them.
- Red herrings misdirect legitimately; deus-ex-machina cheats unforgivably.
- Series detectives age in real time (or float timelessly) by authorial choice.
- True crime's boom blurs reportage with narrative techniques.
