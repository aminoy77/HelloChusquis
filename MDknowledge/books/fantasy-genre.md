# Fantasy genre (example)

- Fantasy builds secondary worlds with internally consistent magic systems.
- Tolkien's legendarium (Hobbit, LOTR, Silmarillion) codified epic fantasy.
- Le Guin's Earthsea prices magic with true names and balance.
- Martin's Westeros subverts prophecy tropes brutally.
- Sanderson's hard-magic laws systematize costs and limits explicitly.
- Portal fantasies (Narnia) bridge mundane and marvelous worlds.
- Urban fantasy hides magic inside modern cities (Gaiman, Butcher).
- Grimdark (Abercrombie) drains heroism leaving moral murk.
- Romantasy's 2020s boom fused romance plots with fantasy settings.
- Magic costs (memory, pain, death) create stakes mechanically.
