# Classical periods (example)

- Medieval chant (Gregorian) monophonically praised God anonymously.
- Renaissance polyphony (Palestrina) wove independent voices equally.
- Baroque (1600-1750) ornamented bass-driven drama (Bach, Handel).
- Classical (1750-1820) clarified forms (sonata, symphony) elegantly.
- Romantic (1820-1910) swelled emotion, orchestras, and virtuosity.
- Impressionism (Debussy) blurred edges into color washes musically.
- Modernism (Stravinsky, Schoenberg) broke tonality deliberately.
- Minimalism (Reich, Glass) loops patterns into transcendent states.
- Film scores (Williams, Morricone) became the popular classical.
- Historically informed performance revives period instruments/speeds.
