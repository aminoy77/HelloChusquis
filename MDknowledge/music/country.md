- Country blends Appalachian folk, blues, and honky-tonk plainly.
- Nashville's Music Row centralizes songwriting industrially.
- Outlaw movement (Cash, Jennings) rebelled against polished Nashville sound.
- Bluegrass (Monroe, Scruggs) picks breakneck acoustic virtuosity.
- Story songs (El Paso, Harper Valley) narrate mini-movies lyrically.
- CMA Awards plus Grand Ole Opry membership crown careers.
- Pop crossover (Swift's arc) expanded audiences then departed genres.
- Line dancing plus two-stepping socialize the honky-tonk floor.
- Alt-country (Wilco, Isbell) courts rock audiences literarily.
- Trucker anthems mythologize highways enduringly.
