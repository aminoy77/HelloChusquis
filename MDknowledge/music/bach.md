# Bach (example)

- Bach (1685-1750) systematized Baroque counterpoint supremely.
- Well-Tempered Clavier tunes through all 24 keys twice pedagogically.
- Mass in B minor compiles a lifetime's sacred mastery posthumously.
- Brandenburg Concertos showcase six different ensemble colorings.
- Goldberg Variations spin an aria into 30 character studies.
- Fugues state subjects then answer them in stretto and inversion.
- Leipzig cantor duties demanded weekly sacred cantatas for years.
- 18 children (several composers) extended the Bach musical dynasty.
- Chorale harmonizations still teach four-part writing worldwide.
- Mendelssohn's 1829 St. Matthew revival launched the Bach renaissance.
