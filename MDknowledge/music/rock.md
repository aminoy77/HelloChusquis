# Rock origins (example)

- Rock and roll fused blues, country, and R&B in 1950s America.
- Chuck Berry's guitar riffs plus showmanship wrote the rock template.
- Elvis Presley's 1956 breakthrough televised rock to mass America.
- British Invasion bands (Beatles, Stones) re-exported American roots globally.
- Backbeat drumming (2 and 4) drives rock's physical momentum.
- Power chords (root+fifth) simplify harmony for volume and speed.
- Album era (1965-2000) made LPs artistic statements beyond singles.
- Punk (1976) stripped prog excess back to fast loud simplicity.
- MTV (1981) made image inseparable from rock stardom.
- Streaming unbundled albums back into singles-driven economics.
