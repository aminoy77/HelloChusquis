# Blues origins (example)

- Blues fused African work songs with European harmony in the Deep South.
- Twelve-bar form (I-I-IV-I-V-IV-I) structures thousands of songs.
- Blue notes (bent thirds/sevenths) voice its ache microtonally.
- Delta acoustic (Robert Johnson) preceded Chicago electric amplification.
- Call-and-response patterns echo field-holler work origins.
- British Invasion bands (Stones, Yardbirds) re-exported blues globally.
- Chicago labels (Chess) electrified Muddy Waters and Howlin' Wolf.
- Blues scale (minor pentatonic plus blue note) solos rock guitar still.
- Bessie Smith's empress-era records outsold all 1920s rivals.
- Festivals (Chicago, King Biscuit) pilgrimage sites yearly.
