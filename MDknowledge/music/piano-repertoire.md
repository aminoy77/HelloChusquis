# Piano repertoire (example)

- Bach's Well-Tempered Clavier tunes all keys pedagogically twice.
- Moonlight/Pathetique/Appassionata (Beethoven) triad romantic piano entry.
- Chopin nocturnes plus etudes wed poetry to technique inseparably.
- Liszt's transcendental etudes pushed virtuosity past prior limits.
- Debussy preludes paint impressions rather than develop classically.
- Rachmaninoff concertos demand huge hands plus bigger hearts.
- Gershwin's Rhapsody in Blue fused jazz with concert hall boldly.
- Glass minimalism loops patterns into trance states deliberately.
- Jazz standards (Gershwin, Porter) double as improv vehicles.
- Video-game scores (Uematsu) headline concert halls now routinely.
