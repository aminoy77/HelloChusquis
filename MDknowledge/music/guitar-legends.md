# Guitar legends (example)

- Hendrix redefined electric possibility in four years flat.
- Clapton's 'woman tone' plus Crossroads mythologize blues-rock lineage.
- Page's bowed solos plus production architected Led Zeppelin.
- Van Halen's tapping (Eruption, 1978) reset technique overnight.
- Gilmour's bends prove fewer notes wound deeper emotionally.
- SRV revived Texas blues with supernatural intensity briefly.
- Metallica's Hetfield downpicking built thrash rhythm science.
- Segovia legitimized classical guitar on concert stages globally.
- Montgomery's thumb-octaves voiced jazz guitar uniquely warmly.
- Polyphia's virtuosity streams instrumental guitar to Gen Z.
