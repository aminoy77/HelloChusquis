# Hip-hop (example)

- Hip-hop was born at a 1973 Bronx block party (DJ Kool Herc).
- Four elements: MCing, DJing, breaking, graffiti writing.
- Sampling loops funk breaks into new compositions (with clearance battles).
- Grandmaster Flash pioneered scratching and quick-mix techniques.
- Run-DMC crossed rock/rap with Walk This Way (1986).
- Golden age (late 80s) diversified conscious, gangsta, and native-tongue styles.
- East/West rivalry's 1996-97 murders traumatized the culture.
- Jay-Z's business empire modeled rap moguldom beyond music.
- Kendrick's Pulitzer (2018) certified hip-hop's literary standing.
- Global scenes (UK grime, K-hiphop, Afrobeats fusions) localize the form.
