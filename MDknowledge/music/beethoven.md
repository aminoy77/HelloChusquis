# Beethoven (example)

- Beethoven (1770-1827) bridged Classical clarity and Romantic force.
- Fifth Symphony's four-note motif may be the most famous in music.
- Ninth's Ode to Joy finale added voices to symphony unprecedentedly.
- Deafness from his late twenties forced purely inner-hearing composition.
- Moonlight Sonata's opening movement subverts sonata expectations deliberately.
- Late string quartets baffled contemporaries, now crown the repertoire.
- Heiligenstadt Testament confesses deafness despair plus artistic resolve.
- Third (Eroica) originally honored, then un-dedicated from, Napoleon.
- 32 piano sonatas chart his full stylistic evolution nickname by nickname.
- Funeral procession drew thousands through 1827 Vienna.
