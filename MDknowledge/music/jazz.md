# Jazz origins (example)

- Jazz fused African rhythms with European harmony in early-1900s New Orleans.
- Improvisation over chord changes defines jazz versus classical fidelity.
- Blues twelve-bar forms underpin countless jazz compositions.
- Louis Armstrong's phrasing taught jazz to sing through instruments.
- Duke Ellington composed 1,000+ works elevating jazz to concert halls.
- Bebop (Parker, Gillespie) accelerated tempos for listening, not dancing.
- Miles Davis reinvented jazz repeatedly (cool, modal, fusion eras).
- John Coltrane's sheets-of-sound pushed harmonic limits spiritually.
- Swing rhythm's lilt separates jazz time-feel from straight eighths.
- Jazz harmony (ii-V-I, tritone subs) now teaches pop songwriting too.
