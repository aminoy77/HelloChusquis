- Bob Marley globalized Rastafari messages via Island Records.
- One-drop drumming emphasizes beat three hypnotically.
- Sound systems (King Tubby) pioneered remix/dub studio science.
- Rastafari theology (Haile Selassie, repatriation) infuses lyrics spiritually.
- Dancehall (1980s digital riddims) sped up and slackened lyrically.
- Reggaeton fuses dancehall dembow with Latin pop globally now.
- 420 associations shadow the herb-sacrament stereotype reductively.
- Rototom Sunsplash (Spain) anchors Europe's reggae summers.
- Carnival road marches (Trinidad) share sound-system DNA.
