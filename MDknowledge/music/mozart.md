# Mozart (example)

- Mozart (1756-1791) composed 600+ works in a 35-year life.
- Child prodigy tours displayed his keyboard and composing gifts by age six.
- Figaro, Don Giovanni, and Magic Flute anchor the opera repertoire.
- Requiem remained unfinished at his death; Süssmayr completed it.
- Haydn and Mozart's friendship sharpened both composers mutually.
- Jupiter Symphony's finale weaves five themes contrapuntally.
- Clarinet Concerto exploits the instrument's chalumeau depths.
- Vienna freelance years traded Salzburg security for artistic freedom.
- Buried in a common grave, mythologized by poverty legends since.
-  Köchel numbers (K. 545) catalog works chronologically for reference.
