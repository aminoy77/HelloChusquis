# Electronic music (example)

- Synthesizers (Moog, 1964) made voltages sing musically first.
- Kraftwerk's autobahn aesthetics blueprinted techno/house/hip-hop.
- Detroit techno plus Chicago house birthed global club culture.
- Rave/Second Summer of Love (1988) mainstreamed ecstasy-fueled weekends.
- EDM festivals (Tomorrowland) industrialized DJ superstardom.
- DAWs (Ableton) collapsed studios into laptops affordably.
- Sampling lawsuits (Bridgeport) chilled then licensed collage economics.
- Ambient (Eno) functionalized music as atmosphere deliberately.
- Drum-and-bass tempos (~174 BPM) split UK rave lineages.
- Streaming playlists revived DJ mix formats as podcasts.
