# The Beatles (example)

- The Beatles (Lennon, McCartney, Harrison, Starr) formed in Liverpool in 1960.
- Beatlemania (1963-66) packed stadiums with screaming fans globally.
- Sgt. Pepper (1967) pioneered the album-as-art concept record.
- Abbey Road's medley closes their recorded career ingeniously.
- The band split in 1970 amid business and creative tensions.
- Lennon-McCartney songwriting credits mask mostly separate compositions.
- George Martin's production shaped their studio experimentation decisively.
- Ed Sullivan Show (1964, 73M viewers) launched the US invasion.
- Anthology and Get Back projects mined archives decades later.
- Best-selling band ever: 600M+ records claimed sold worldwide.
