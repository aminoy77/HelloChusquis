# Space telescopes (example)

- Hubble (1990) overcame spherical aberration via 1993 servicing heroics.
- Deep Fields imaged thousands of galaxies in pinprick sky patches.
- Webb (2021, L2 halo orbit) sees infrared first-light galaxies routinely.
- Webb's sunshield cools instruments to -223°C passively.
- Kepler/TESS exoplanet surveys catalogued 5,000+ worlds statistically.
- Chandra X-ray plus Fermi gamma observatories complete spectra beyond visible.
- Ground giants (ELT 39 m) will rival space resolution adaptively.
- Roman Space Telescope (mid-2020s) surveys dark energy wide-field.
- Servicing ended with shuttles; robotic refuels extend lives now.
- Archive mining keeps old observations publishing decades later.
