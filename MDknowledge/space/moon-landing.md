# Moon landing (example)

- Apollo 11 landed 20 July 1969; Armstrong stepped out hours later.
- Saturn V remains the most powerful successful rocket flown.
- Lunar Module (Eagle) plus Command Module (Columbia) split landing/orbit roles.
- 1201/1202 computer alarms nearly aborted the descent automatically.
- Manual takeover dodged boulders with ~25 seconds of fuel remaining.
- Plaques, flags, laser reflectors, and footprints remain onsite.
- Six landings (1969-72) brought 842 kg of moon rocks total.
- Third-stage (S-IVB) crash seismology probed lunar interiors.
- Quarantine (Mobile Quarantine Facility) guarded against moon germs theatrically.
- Broadcasts reached ~600M viewers, a fifth of humanity then.
