- Selection odds run thousands-to-one across STEM plus operational excellence.
- Neutral-buoyancy pools rehearse spacewalks for 7 hours per real hour.
- T-38 jets maintain flight judgment plus crew coordination continuously.
- Launch g-loads (~3g) plus vibration punish bodies briefly but intensely.
- Fluid shifts puff faces and congest heads until adaptation (~days).
- Bone/muscle loss needs 2+ daily exercise hours countermeasured.
- Sleep stations strap bags to walls amid 16 daily sunrises.
- Toilets funnel airflow in zero-g with practiced precision.
- Mission patches encode crew identity plus flight symbolism densely.
- Post-flight reconditioning rebuilds balance and strength over weeks.
