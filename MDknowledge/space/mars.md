# Mars exploration (example)

- Mars glows red from iron-oxide dust coating its surface.
- Olympus Mons (~22 km) is the tallest known volcano.
- Valles Marineris canyons would span the continental United States.
- Thin CO₂ atmosphere plus no magnetosphere challenge settlers harshly.
- Viking landers (1976) first succeeded on the surface enduringly.
- Rovers (Sojourner to Perseverance) escalated mobility and autonomy steadily.
- Perseverance caches samples for future return missions hopefully.
- Ingenuity helicopter flew 72 sorties, far past its 5-flight plan.
- Water ice plus ancient riverbeds evidence warmer wetter pasts.
- Starship-scale plans target crewed landings within coming decades.
