# International Space Station (example)

- ISS assembly (1998-2011) joined US, Russian, European, Japanese, Canadian modules.
- Crews rotate ~6-month expeditions continuously since 2000.
- Microgravity labs grow crystals and study bodies unloaded by weight.
- Canadarm2 plus Dextre handle external maintenance robotically.
- Reboosts counteract atmospheric drag decaying the 400-km orbit.
- Cupola windows frame Earth photography's golden age from orbit.
- Water recycling reclaims ~98 percent of station humidity and urine.
- Deorbit (planned ~2030 Pacific) will end station operations deliberately.
- Commercial successors (Axiom, Starlab) court post-ISS research markets.
- Amateur radio contacts let schoolkids hail crews directly.
