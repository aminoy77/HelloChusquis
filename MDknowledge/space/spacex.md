# SpaceX (example)

- SpaceX (2002) pursued reusability after three Falcon 1 failures nearly bankrupted it.
- Falcon 9 first-stage landings (2015) made reuse routine economically.
- Dragon capsules restored US crew launch capability in 2020.
- Starship aims fully reusable super-heavy lift for Moon and Mars.
- Starlink's thousands of satellites sell global broadband directly.
- Merlin engines iterate cheaply versus expendable predecessors.
- Fairing recovery (boat nets, then water landings) trims costs further.
- NASA contracts (COTS, Commercial Crew, HLS) funded milestones progressively.
- Raptor full-flow staged combustion chases efficiency records.
- Launch cadence (100+ yearly) now exceeds all 20th-century programs.
