# Mental health basics (example)

- Exercise rivals medication for mild-moderate depression in trials.
- Sleep disruption both signals and worsens most mental conditions.
- CBT reframes distorted thought patterns with homework-backed efficacy.
- Social connection predicts longevity better than fitness trackers suggest.
- Mindfulness (MBSR protocols) reduces rumination measurably in studies.
- Alcohol and cannabis mask symptoms while degrading sleep architecture.
- Routine (light, movement, meals, work blocks) stabilizes mood baselines.
- Journaling externalizes loops the mind would otherwise rehearse endlessly.
- Professional help (therapy, psychiatry) complements self-care, not replaces.
- Crisis lines (988 US, 116 123 UK/Samaritans) answer around the clock.
