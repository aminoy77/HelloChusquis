# Exercise basics (example)

- 150 weekly moderate minutes (or 75 vigorous) anchors health guidelines.
- Strength sessions twice weekly preserve muscle and bone past 30.
- Progressive overload (gradually more) drives all adaptation safely.
- Compound lifts (squat, hinge, push, pull, carry) cover fundamental patterns.
- Zone-2 cardio builds aerobic base conversationally for hours.
- Sleep plus protein timing multiply training returns substantially.
- Deload weeks dissipate accumulated fatigue productively.
- Form first, load second — injuries erase months of progress instantly.
- NEAT (daily steps, stairs) often outweighs formal workouts calorically.
- Consistency across years beats perfect months followed by burnout.
