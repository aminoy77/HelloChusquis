# Nutrition basics (example)

- Macronutrients (protein, carbs, fat) supply energy; micros enable metabolism.
- Protein (~1.6 g/kg for active) repairs muscle and signals satiety strongly.
- Fiber (25-30 g daily) feeds gut flora and steadies glucose curves.
- Added sugars hide in sauces and drinks; labels reveal per-100g truth.
- Whole foods beat ultra-processed on satiety per calorie consistently.
- Hydration needs vary; pale-yellow urine guides better than fixed liters.
- Mediterranean patterns top evidence charts for longevity outcomes.
- Crash diets rebound metabolically; gradual deficits preserve muscle.
- Protein timing matters less than daily totals for most trainees.
- Supplements fill diagnosed gaps, never substitute dietary patterns.
