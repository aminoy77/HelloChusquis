# Taxation (example)

- Income taxes scale with earnings; progressive rates rise with brackets.
- VAT/sales taxes fall on consumption, often regressively in practice.
- Corporate taxes target profits; payroll taxes fund social insurance.
- Capital-gains rates (usually lower) reward investment holding periods.
- Tax incidence (who truly pays) differs from legal liability via price shifts.
- Deductions shrink taxable income; credits shrink tax bills dollar-for-dollar.
- Sin taxes (tobacco, carbon) price externalities while raising revenue.
- Tax havens plus transfer pricing shift multinational profits legally-ish.
- Laffer effects bound revenue: 0 and 100 percent rates both yield nothing.
- Simplicity versus fairness trades define every reform debate.
