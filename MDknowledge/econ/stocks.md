# Stock market (example)

- Stocks are fractional company ownership with residual profit claims.
- Exchanges (NYSE, Nasdaq) match buyers and sellers continuously.
- Indices (S&P 500, FTSE) average baskets as market thermometers.
- Bulls expect rises; bears expect falls; corrections dip 10 percent+.
- Dividends share profits; buybacks boost per-share metrics instead.
- Diversification across assets cuts idiosyncratic risk cheaply.
- Index funds beat most active managers net of fees over decades.
- P/E ratios price earnings multiples; high implies growth expectations.
- Market orders fill instantly; limit orders wait for target prices.
- Crashes (1929, 2008) trace to leverage plus panic feedback loops.
