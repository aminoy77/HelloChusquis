# GDP (example)

- GDP totals market value of final goods/services produced domestically yearly.
- Consumption plus investment plus government plus net exports compose expenditure GDP.
- Nominal GDP uses current prices; real GDP strips inflation for growth truth.
- GDP per capita divides output by population for rough prosperity comparisons.
- GNP/GNI credit nationals abroad instead of domestic geography.
- Unpaid care, black markets, and nature escape GDP accounting entirely.
- Recessions commonly mean two quarterly GDP contractions (rule of thumb).
- Purchasing-power parity adjusts cross-country comparisons for price levels.
- China/US PPP leadership debates hinge on exactly these adjustments.
- Beyond-GDP dashboards (HDI, happiness) supplement output worship.
