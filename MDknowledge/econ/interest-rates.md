# Interest rates (example)

- Interest prices time: lenders charge borrowers for waiting and risk.
- Central-bank policy rates anchor mortgages, cards, and business loans.
- Raising rates cools inflation; cutting stimulates borrowing and hiring.
- Yield curves plot rates by maturity; inversions often precede recessions.
- Compound interest grows balances exponentially, for debts and savings alike.
- Real rates (nominal minus inflation) decide true borrowing costs.
- Fixed rates lock certainty; variable rates gamble on policy paths.
- Credit spreads over safe rates price default risk per borrower.
- Negative rates (Europe, Japan experiments) charge savers to force spending.
- Rule of 72 divides rates into doubling times for quick estimates.
