# Bonds (example)

- Bonds are IOUs paying coupons plus principal at maturity.
- Prices fall when yields rise (and vice versa) mechanically.
- Duration measures price sensitivity to rate moves in years.
- Credit ratings (AAA to junk) price default risk into yields.
- Government bonds anchor risk-free curves per currency.
- Corporate spreads compensate default and liquidity risk over sovereigns.
- Municipal bonds often carry tax exemptions for local investors.
- Inflation-linked bonds (TIPS) adjust principal with price indices.
- Bond funds have no maturity; duration, not calendar, sets risk.
- Diversification into bonds dampens equity drawdowns historically.
