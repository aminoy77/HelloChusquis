# Inflation (example)

- Inflation measures sustained general price rises eroding money value.
- CPI baskets track consumer prices; core CPI strips volatile food/energy.
- Demand-pull (hot spending) versus cost-push (oil shocks) diagnose causes differently.
- Hyperinflation (Weimar, Zimbabwe, Venezuela) destroys savings and planning.
- Deflation (falling prices) can spiral as buyers delay purchases.
- Central banks target ~2 percent inflation in advanced economies.
- Real interest subtracts inflation from nominal rates for true returns.
- Wage-price spirals embed expectations into contracts persistently.
- Shrinkflation hides rises in smaller packages at equal prices.
- TIPS and indexed bonds protect savers contractually against inflation.
