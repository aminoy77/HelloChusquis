# Budgeting (example)

- 50/30/20 splits needs/wants/savings as a starting template, not law.
- Pay-yourself-first automations beat willpower-based saving reliably.
- Track every euro for 30 days before optimizing anything blindly.
- Emergency funds (3-6 months expenses) precede all investing activity.
- Zero-based budgets assign every unit a job monthly, killing drift.
- Sinking funds pre-save irregular bills (insurance, holidays) smoothly.
- Cancel-by-default audits subscriptions quarterly for zombies.
- Cash envelopes (physical or virtual) cap leaky categories viscerally.
- Net-worth tracking (assets minus debts) measures true progress.
- Raise-splitting (half to savings) grows wealth without lifestyle pain.
