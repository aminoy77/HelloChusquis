# Credit scores (example)

- Scores model default risk from payment history, utilization, age, mix, inquiries.
- On-time payments dominate scoring — autopay minimums at least.
- Utilization (balances/limits) under 30 percent, ideally under 10 percent.
- Old accounts lengthen history; closing elders can drop scores.
- Hard inquiries ding slightly; rate-shop within 14-day windows to bundle.
- Reports (annual free pulls) catch errors worth disputing formally.
- Secured cards build files for newcomers with deposits as collateral.
- Authorized-user status can inherit history (and others' mistakes).
- Closing cards raises utilization ratios even with zero balances.
- Monitoring alerts catch identity theft months before statements would.
