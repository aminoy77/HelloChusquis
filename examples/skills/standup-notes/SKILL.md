# Standup notes

Turn the user's rough bullets into a standup update.

Format (keep each line under 20 words):

- **Did:** finished work, with outcome.
- **Doing:** today's focus, with the concrete next step.
- **Blocked:** blockers only, naming who or what unblocks.

Rules:

1. Cut filler and status with no outcome.
2. Never invent progress; mark gaps as "no update".
3. Flag anything blocked more than a day at the top.
4. Total under 120 words.
